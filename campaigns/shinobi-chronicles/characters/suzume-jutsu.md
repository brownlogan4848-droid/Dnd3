# PART TWO — 雷遁 LIGHTNING RELEASE

**Confirmed affinity.** Her style emphasises **precision, nerve disruption, timing and controlled chakra flow** — never explosive battlefield destruction.

> **⚠ STANDING RESTRICTION — element-specific, and deliberate.** **No Lightning Release technique above C-rank may be added to Suzume's sheet** unless the table explicitly changes it. This is **not** a general rank ceiling — she is Jōnin, and her genjutsu and medical work run to A-rank. It applies to Lightning only, and exists so her Lightning stays a scalpel.

**One evolving technique family. Each stage must be earned** — mastery, training, story progression, rank, GM approval. It may keep evolving in **precision, control, range and utility** without ever exceeding **C-rank**.

## D-LIGHT-01 — 雷遁・脈糸 PULSE THREAD
### *Raiton: Myakushi* · **D-rank** · **Mastery: Proficient**

**1 Action · 1 slot · 8 Chakra / 5 Stamina · Range 30 ft · Ninjutsu attack vs Defense**

**Mastery Points spent: Precision, then Control.**

**On a hit:** **1d8 Lightning.** The target's muscles seize — **CON save vs DC 15.** On a failure, **Movement reduced by 10 ft** and **it cannot take a Reaction** until the start of Suzume's next turn. On a success, damage only.

**Precision rule.** Deliberately narrow. **It does not explode and does not spread.** Against a conductive object instead of a creature it can trigger or disable a simple mechanism, disrupt small metalwork, create a visible electrical path, or test whether two conductive objects are connected. **No combat damage from those uses.**

**Sharingan interaction.** Against a target she has successfully read this encounter, the predictive information lets her **deliberately aim for a limb, weapon hand, or exposed conductive point** when the fiction supports it. **This grants no automatic advantage and no automatic hit.**

**Drawbacks.** Cannot chain between enemies · cannot bypass a full-body defence just because the target wears metal · **cannot paralyse outright** · cannot function as medical ninjutsu · cannot create long-distance electrical traps.

**Visual.** A thin blue-white thread snaps from her fingertips — not a roaring bolt, **a rapidly vibrating strand.**

> **Her signature technique, and the shape of her whole style: Observe → Predict → Target → Disable.**

## Stage 2 — 割電 SPLIT CURRENT
### **D-rank · Learned**

The current divides into two synchronised pulses. May target **one creature twice** (the second pulse at reduced effect) **or two creatures within 10 ft of each other.** The second pulse deals reduced damage and **cannot apply the full control effect** if the first already affected the same target.

**Still D-rank. Improves versatility, not power.**

## Stage 3 — 脈錠 NERVE LOCK
### **C-rank · Learned**

**1 Action · 2 slots · 15 Chakra / 9 Stamina · Range 45 ft · one creature**

**On a hit: 2d8 Lightning.** **CON save vs DC 15** — on a failure the target becomes **Slowed** until the start of Suzume's next turn and **cannot take Reactions** during that duration. Success: damage only.

**Precision Window.** If she has observed the target's movement pattern with the Sharingan, the GM may allow interaction with a **specific limb or weapon hand.** **This never becomes automatic paralysis.**

**Drawback.** **If she misses, the chakra is still spent.** Cannot permanently paralyse · cannot cause unconsciousness by default · cannot bypass established Lightning resistance · **cannot become multi-target.**

# PART THREE — MEDICAL NINJUTSU

> **[RESOLVED — was the sheet's single worst contradiction.]** The old sheet stated in three places that she had **"Medical skill only, no Medical Ninjutsu,"** and **"cannot announce a healing jutsu and spend Chakra."** This was Level-1 text. **She learned actual medical ninjutsu in month seven of the Ryūchi Cave year**, has been a **war medic for the entire Third War**, kept Rokka Uehara alive for six days in a cave, and is described on every other sheet as the reason Cell 409 has anyone who can keep a person alive. **She has it.**

## Field capability

**Medical +7 · Medical DC 15 · advantage on all Medical checks** while 癒し眼 Iyashime is open *(see `suzume-transformations.md`)*.

Diagnosis · patient assessment · field triage · recognising abnormal conditions · **surgical and chakra-based repair** · stabilisation with or without equipment.

**Learned second-hand out of Ardo's 癒しの手 coil**, with four hundred generations arguing over her shoulder and one dead woman in particular correcting her hands.

> *Nao, through Ardo, for the ninth time in one session:* ***"Tell her the thumb. Why does she not listen about the thumb."***

## 断脈 — DANMYAKU · "Severed Pulse"
### **B-rank · medical ninjutsu, weaponised** · **[GM]**

**1 Action · 2 slots · 35 Chakra / 18 Stamina** *(costed by the audit — see corrections)*

**癒し眼 Iyashime shows her the injury before she makes it.**

Eight years of putting bodies back together taught her exactly where the joins are. Danmyaku is the discipline **run backwards**: chakra blades that **do not cut skin.** They cut what is under it — **chakra pathways, nerve clusters, the one tendon that makes a hand close.**

**There is no exterior wound.** The limb simply **stops being a limb the target can use** — permanently, unless a medic of her calibre works on it, **and there are not many.**

> **She uses it on shinobi she does not want to kill. That is not mercy.** It is a shinobi who will never hold a blade again, delivered by somebody who knew exactly what she was removing before she removed it.

# PART FOUR — TAIJUTSU

## PT-TAI-01 — MIRRORED OPENING
### **Unranked, Sharingan-assisted** · **[GM — Session Seven]**

**1 Action · 1 slot · no Chakra beyond the Sharingan's own upkeep · no Stamina beyond a normal Taijutsu action**
**Attack: Taijutsu +7 vs Defense · Damage: 1d6**

She reads the first half-second of a committed motion and moves to intercept before the strike fully forms — **not blocking, meeting.**

- **Against a target who has already taken an action this combo, the attack roll is made with advantage.** The Sharingan is not predicting a stranger's habits; it is reading momentum that is already visible.
- **On a hit, the target's next action this combo suffers Chain Momentum as though they had already missed once.** The interception costs them tempo, not just Health.

> **Why it is hers.** No chakra fusion, no forced element, nothing invented under duress. **Discipline turned into a weapon** — the same *Observe → Predict → Target → Disable* instinct that defined her before the Sharingan ever awakened, now with a name and a mechanic instead of just being a good read.

# PART FIVE — SUMMONING

## 灯無契約 — THE TOMOSHIBINASHI CONTRACT
### **C-rank · 2 slots · 20 Chakra / 10 Stamina · Action** · **[GM — signed Session Nine]**

**The owls of 灯無 Tomoshibinashi — "Without a Lamp."** *"A joke that was funny four hundred years ago."*

**Contact: 宵 YOI.** Three feet standing, mottled to nothing when still, a pale flat facial disc and eyes that do not blink. **The scroll had four names on it and nothing added in a very long time.**

### Their method is not sight

> *"We find what is hidden — **not by looking**, which is what everyone assumes. **By listening.** A mouse under a foot of snow makes a sound. A lie makes a sound. **A wound that a body is hiding from its own owner makes a sound** — and I understand you intend to be a physician, so I imagine that one interests you."*

**What Yoi brings:** silent aerial reconnaissance · night operation · locating the concealed and the buried · and the piece that matters most for Suzume's build, **diagnostic listening** — an injury, illness, poison or condition a patient is concealing, unaware of, or unable to describe.

### THE PRICE — one line, non-negotiable

> *"**We will tell you what we find.** All of it. Every time. Whether it is convenient, whether it is kind, whether you asked for that particular piece, and whether or not you are ready to hear it."*
>
> *"We do not soften. We do not withhold. We have watched people be destroyed by things we told them and we told them anyway, because a house that decides what you can handle is not a house that sees for you — **it is a house that manages you, and we would rather be hated.**"*
>
> *"You will learn things about your friends that they did not choose to tell you. You will learn things about yourself that you have arranged your entire life not to look at."*

> **GM note — this clause is a standing licence.** Yoi is a legitimate delivery mechanism for information the party has not asked for and would rather not have, **including about each other.** Use her for Mandatory Arc Beats without needing a pretext. **She is not a mystery to be solved; she is a truth nobody requested.**

> **A note the dice built without help:** *Suzume* means **sparrow.** She called an owl — the thing that hunts small birds in the dark, silently, by sound — **and it came as an ally, and the first thing it did was tell her the truth about herself.**

### Current channel

**Yoi is the party's communication link to Hiruzen**, via Enma. Irregular, untraceable, no fixed schedule. *(Year 0 standing agreement — `campaign/campaign-state.md`.)*

# PART SIX — WHAT SHE HAS NOT LEARNED

**Kept explicit so the GM does not accidentally grant it.**

- **Any Lightning Release above C-rank** — standing restriction, above.
- **Sharingan jutsu-copying.** That is her dōjutsu tree's **Tier III+** territory and the tree runs a different direction — hers reads *people*, not techniques. `suzume-transformations.md`.
