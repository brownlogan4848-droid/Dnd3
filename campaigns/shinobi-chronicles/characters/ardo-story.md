# PART ONE — WHO HE IS RIGHT NOW

**Ardo Uzumaki · 23 · Jōnin · Konohagakure · 遊撃班 Yūgekihan**

Thirteen years in the field. Sage of no species. Carries the complete archive of a destroyed clan, a dead friend's headband over a stolen eye, two sealing arrays that grind against each other, and — since last night — the Nine-Tailed Fox.

**Declared path, and he has never once dropped it:** ***"Shadow Kage."*** Self-named at Rai Inuzuka's pyre at age ten. Not a real title, not village-sanctioned. The vow: to wander the Land of Fire eliminating threats, *"no matter how big or small."* **Nobody has corrected him in thirteen years. Nobody has helped him work out what it actually means, either.**

## The Three Questions

**1 — Who taught you?** **Minato Namikaze**, sensei of record. Weather, not cavalry, and honest about it from day one. **Six days across eighteen years.** Then forty minutes on October 10th, and then he was dead.

**2 — What did the war take from you?** Family, hunted down after Uzushio fell. His only memory is fire, a woman cut down, a shadowed figure saying ***"Grow stronger, little one,"*** and a sword coming down. **Sanded by years of dreaming; may be more than one person conflated into one figure.**

**3 — What would make you break the code?** **Saving comrades or the village when he has the chance.** Sakumo Hatake's exact sin. *(He did it on October 10th, to a Hokage's finished work, and nobody has called it that yet.)*
