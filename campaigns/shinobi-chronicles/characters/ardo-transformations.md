**Never his to trigger.** Required the trustee actively offering, mid-extremity, in direct response to something real.

**What it actually was:** the trustee **demonstrating a fragment of Shiokaze** — one piece at a time, only when a moment justified it, because handing a six-year-old the whole form would have killed him. He believed he was being loaned something exotic. **He was being taught his own inheritance in instalments, by someone who could not explain why.**

**With the estate signed for, this has no source left to come from.** Historical entry.

## 紅風 KŌFŪ — "Crimson Wind" · Stage 1 **[GM — Ardo's own reach]**

Built the same visit as Shakufū, but **distinct in kind**: his own strained reach toward what he touched, not the trustee's loan. **Nothing moderates the risk from the other side** — which is exactly why it is more dangerous, not less.

**Visual.** Chakra peels outward in visible sheets, wind given colour and weight, catching his dark red hair. Eyes go fully red — the same shade the mindscape water turned when the trustee reached back. Every gust off him carries a faint red tint.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Activation** | **2 slots** (of his 5). **60 Chakra / 30 Stamina / 5 Health** on commit. Standard Rule 5 vulnerability during the window — **fails outright if interrupted, costs already spent.** 3 slots remain once transformed |
| **Duration** | Up to **3 rounds**, end early any time |
| **Effect** | Applies to **all** his attacks, Taijutsu *and* Ninjutsu: **+1d6** damage · **advantage** on the attack roll · **Move doubled.** Every landed hit launches the target **~20 ft**, no save — *except where the base technique already pushes harder (Gale Fist's own 30 ft does not stack)* |
| **Sustain** | **20 Chakra / 15 Stamina** per round held, on top of activation |
| **Risk** | **CON save every round held, DC 12 → 15 → 18.** Fail = instant Rule 5 Interruption + real backlash damage, worse the deeper it was pushed |
| **The Crash** | However it ends: **disadvantage on everything the following round** |

**Clone interaction.** A Shadow Clone summoned **while Kōfū is already up** is created as a true snapshot — transformed identically, no extra cost, tied to Ardo's own duration. A clone summoned **before** stays ordinary; a snapshot does not retroactively upgrade.

**Rank-ceiling override [GM — standing rule].** While Kōfū is active, Ardo may cast up to **S-rank**, overriding his **Jōnin** ceiling of B-plus-one-A-signature. **This teaches him nothing** — he must actually know the technique through the normal channels. It removes the rank gate in advance, it does not hand out jutsu. General mechanic: Book IV §3 Part Five, which cites this as its first instance.

## 渦風 UZUFŪ — "Whirlpool Wind" · Stage 2 **[GM — Session Eight]**

**How it unlocked.** Not by reaching. Souji's newly-awakened Senju chakra surged nearby while Ardo was strained and hurting, and **the thing behind the seal moved first, unprompted** — frantic, protective, panicking, trying to force its way out to defend him. Three escalating CON saves, **DC 15 → 18 → 21, all passed** — and passed by *refusing to ride the rage*: he recognised the feeling from Rai's pyre, remembered rage buys nothing, and reached back through the flood to calm the thing on the other side.

**What he did to the seal.** With both palms on burning linework, untaught, never having seen a fūinjutsu scroll, **he turned the whirl-pattern.** The seal did not break and did not open — the inward-facing spiral **rotated** and became a **channel**. A whirlpool that takes the flood and gives it somewhere to go, rather than a dam trying to stop it.

> **This is the single most important fact about Stage 2: the containment stayed intact.** A controlled aperture, not a breach. **It is now the door his senjutsu comes through.**

### Entry requirement — the hard gate

> **Cannot be entered on rage, fury, or vengeance. Ever.** A rage-shaped reach **fails automatically — no roll, no cost refunded.** Only enterable from something *offered*: calm, gratitude, protection, connection. **This is a fail-safe built into the form, not a GM restriction bolted on.** See the Grief/Rage Distinction below.

| **Sustain** | **25 Chakra / 20 Stamina** per round (net ~10 Ch after the regen below) |
| **Risk** | **CON save every round, DC 13 → 16 → 19 → 22.** On a failure: instant Rule 5 Interruption, real backlash damage, **and the transformation inverts** — see Sakauzu |
| **The Crash** | **Two full rounds** at disadvantage on everything, and **Chakra/Stamina cannot be restored above 25% of max** for the rest of the encounter |

### The three passives — active the whole time Uzufū is up

**無隙 MUGEKI — "No Opening."** He is not moving fast, exactly. He is *arriving.*

- **Substitution and every other Displace-type escape auto-fail against his attacks.** No roll. The Reaction is spent, the Chakra and Stamina are spent, and the attack lands anyway — the swap has nowhere to happen because there was no interval to swap *during*.
- **Reaction-cast jutsu cannot intercept, clash with, or block his strikes.**
- **No Counter Window ever opens against him** — a missed opener grants the defender nothing, because he is already gone from where the miss happened.
- **Bonus-action repositioning does not create distance.** He is simply there again.
- **The one thing that still works: Brace.** A defender with literally zero legal response stops being a participant; Brace is the "you may still choose to tank it" floor.

**空爆 KŪBAKU — "Air Detonation."** Contact *is* the explosion. No wind-up, no gust — a body suddenly leaving, very fast, in a straight line.

- **Every landed Taijutsu hit hurls the target 30 ft. No save, no resistance, no exceptions.**
- **Anything within 5 ft of the point of impact takes half the strike's damage** as concussive backwash.
- Whatever the target hits on the way is on top of that, GM's call on terrain.

**開門 KAIMON — "The Open Gate."**

- **Any senjutsu practice or attempt made in Uzufū rolls with advantage**, and all progress accrues at **triple rate.**
- **[SUPERSEDED, Session Nine.]** Written when Uzufū was the fastest road to senjutsu. **He reached senjutsu in a cave, by his own method, without ever entering Uzufū.** Kaimon remains a genuine training accelerator and **is no longer worth a limb.**

## 逆渦 SAKAUZU — the inversion **[GM — failure state, GM-run]**

**Not a form he enters. The thing Uzufū becomes when he fails the save.**

Control passes to the frantic, protective thing that was trying to take over in the first place. **GM-run, not player-run, and not friendly to anyone standing nearby.** It keeps Mugeki and Kūbaku and loses every scrap of judgement.

**It has happened once**, at Training Ground Six, and it put 99 damage into Suzume at zero range while she was shielding an unconscious Souji. **It stopped on its own** — it saw what Ardo's hand had done in the mud and *let go of the aperture like it burned.*

> **The danger of Stage 2 is this and it does not go away.**

## THE GRIEF / RAGE DISTINCTION **[GM — standing rule, confirmed Session Five]**

Tested directly at Rai's pyre: Ardo, grief curdling into rage, tried to force Kōfū on fury alone — *"they're all gonna pay for this."* **The Seam did not ignite defensively and did not grant anything.** It went cold and still, as if the gate had never been there. No transformation. Real Chakra spent chasing nothing.

> **The rule, fixed:** these forms answer **grief offered with gratitude** — reaching out, saying thank you, wanting connection. They **do not answer grief spent as a demand for vengeance.** Rage gets silence: not punishment, not power.

**Rage-fuelled reaches fail by default, no roll needed**, unless something in the fiction changes what kind of reach it actually is. **This governs 渦潮 Uzushio absolutely**, at the largest scale it will ever operate.

# PART TWO — 荒れ川 ARAKAWA · "The Raging River"

**[GM — permanent until treated. The condition, not a form.]**

### The retroactive reveal

**It was never Ardo's chakra control.** Everything unexplainable about him was the trustee quietly doing the work:

- 200 Chakra at Genin that no document in Konoha could account for.
- The Wind/Lightning fusion forced open four ranks early, twice, by an untrained child.
- **Gale Fist's recoil not firing whenever the Seam was open** — logged as a curiosity since Session One. **It was the tell.**

She spent herself to nothing holding Suzume together for four minutes. **The regulation went with it.**

### Baseline condition

An enormous, unattended volume of chakra churning constantly with nothing steering it.

| Band | Effect |
|---|---|
| **E and D-rank techniques** | **Fine.** His own honest training covers them |
| **C-rank and above** | **Control check before resolving.** His whole fusion kit was built on borrowed regulation |
| **Failure** | Overflow |

### Transformation table — roll d20 every attempt **[Kōfū and Uzufū ONLY]**

**潮風 Shiokaze is exempt, and that is the entire point of owning it.**

| d20 | Result |
|---:|---|
| **1–4** | **BLOWOUT.** Fires at **S-rank output or beyond** — and **the channelling limb is destroyed.** Shattered, cooked, or gone. GM's call on recoverability; sometimes the answer is no |
| **5–9** | **TEARING.** Severe internal damage, chakra pathways scorched, lasting injury. The technique lands at wild, unpredictable power |
| **10–14** | **SLIP.** He loses the beat mid-transformation. **Rule 5 Interruption**, and the round is spent getting the rhythm back rather than fighting |
| **15–18** | **RIDE IT.** Works, and works too well. Every technique this round jumps a rank band; **real damage taken for each** |
| **19–20** | **CLEAN** |

> **What changed:** the old **10–14 EMERGENT** result — 荒神 Aragami forming, three saves, fail two and it takes him — **is deleted and cannot occur.** There is no material left for it to form out of. **The three saves (CON 15 / WIS 17 / CON 19) are void and must not be rolled.** Reasoning in `ardo-inheritance.md`.

### The other direction — inward

The aperture runs both ways. **Untrained senjutsu means too much natural energy and he turns to stone, permanently** — and he came within two inches of his elbow proving it on day one in Ryūchi Cave.

**潮合 Shioai does not remove this.** It gives him a method for not accumulating; it does not make him immune to losing the method. **A tide that loses its period is just a flood.**

# PART THREE — 潮合 SHIOAI · "Keeping Time"

**[GM — Session Nine. Senjutsu, achieved, by a method nobody in this world teaches.]**

**潮合 *shioai*** — tidal timing; idiomatically, *the right moment.*

### Why every existing method would have killed him

Senjutsu has one first instruction everywhere it is taught: **be still, then gather.** Quiet your own chakra to nothing and draw natural energy into the silence.

**Arakawa removes the prerequisite.** Monuke's day-one verdict: *"You are the worst possible candidate for the only discipline that can save you. That is not cruelty, it is arithmetic."*

### What he worked out instead

**Stillness was never the requirement. It was a technique for the deaf** — a way of making a *small* chakra quiet enough to hear the world with. Every human Monuke had taught in four centuries needed it because their own chakra was noise.

**Ardo's isn't noise. It's a tide.** And a thing with a period does not need to be silenced.

> ### *You don't still a tide. You keep time with it.*

He matched his breathing to the rhythm under his ribs, let the river fall into the same beat, and then **let the cave in on the in-breath and out on the out-breath.** Nothing is balanced. Nothing is held. **It cycles.** *(WIS save, natural 20.)*

### Mechanics

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | **Senjutsu. Real, functional, his** — not toad, snake, or slug |
| **Why it doesn't accumulate** | Nothing pools if it never stops moving. **The in-breath takes and the out-breath returns** |
| **Effect on Arakawa** | The river is **inside the cycle** rather than fighting it. **No control checks while the rhythm holds** |
| **Cost** | Enormous concentration. **His own hands on his own reins**, and it is exhausting |
| **Failure** | **Losing the beat, not losing a grip.** It slips gently — and a tide that loses its period is just a flood, with the same stone waiting |
| **Mastery** | **Held indefinitely, in combat**, against Roku and Monuke simultaneously, since month eleven of the training year. The eleven-minute seated first hold is thirteen years behind him |

**Visual.** Natural energy **rotates around him**, out and back, matching a rhythm coming from under his ribs — the room breathes with him. Fine inward-facing Uzushio linework spreads from the stomach along every path his chakra runs, **pale and turning.** **His right iris divides** — light above, dark below, one clean horizontal line. **A tide mark.** It rises on the in-breath and falls on the out.

### The three gates, as actually cleared

| Gate | As written | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — THE CONTRACT** | A species must accept him | **NEVER CLEARED.** Ryūchi's scroll is spoken for and Monuke will not say by whom. **He has signed nothing and owes nobody** |
| **2 — THE REVERSE SUMMON** | A summon must pull him to their realm | **Cleared by accident** — asked for power in the wrong voice on a 24 and was aimed at the right door |
| **3 — THE BALANCE** | Perfect stillness | **Cleared by disproving it** |

> *"Every sage this world has produced was made by a species… **You are not ours.** You are a sage of a place that has been at the bottom of the sea for twenty years and did not know it made you."*

**The stone clause is still live.** Shioai stops accumulation; it does not make him immune to losing the rhythm.

# PART FOUR — 潮風 SHIOKAZE · "The Tide Wind"

**[GM — the fourth coil of the estate. TAKEN, and entered.]**

## He never invented anything

**Kōfū, Uzufū, Borrowed Gale — all three were reconstructions of this**, built from the inside by a boy who did not know he had a source.

> *"You did not invent your transformations. You **recognised** them, from the inside, with no teacher, no scroll, and no idea you had a source — which is rarer and frankly more alarming than invention."* — Monuke

## What it did not do, and why that mattered

The other three coils are property and do not care whether he uses them. **Shiokaze waited** — eight years, a hundred opportunities, and it never once moved toward him.

> *"Whatever your mother did to that form before she folded it up, **she made certain it could only ever be taken. Never given, never forced, never inherited by default.** Which means she knew there was a version of you who might not want it — and she built the will so the boy got the **choice she did not have.**"*

## The toll **[paid]**

**Taking it cost him the belief that he built his own power.** Every transformation he was ever proud of, reclassified from invention to memory, permanently, by his own hand, knowingly.

**What he keeps:** 拮抗 Kikkō and 潮合 Shioai are genuinely his, invented in two days, and four centuries of Ryūchi Cave never thought of either.

## Mechanics

**Exempt from the Arakawa transformation table.** 拮抗 Kikkō is native to it, so the river is inside the rotation rather than fighting it. **This is the whole reason the two reconstructions are obsolete.**

### The tide has a rhythm, and he does not choose it

Combat alternates **automatically, round by round.** This is the mechanical signature of the form and the reason it is not simply a bigger Uzufū.

| Beat | | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **満ち MICHI** | *the flood* | **He draws.** Chakra/Stamina **return rather than deplete**, natural energy accumulates toward the next beat, incoming techniques are **pulled off-line**, and allies in range are **stabilised** |
| **引き HIKI** | *the ebb* | **He releases.** Everything gathered on the in-breath goes out at once — damage, speed, displacement, all scaled to what the flood collected |

- **All three Uzufū passives run permanently and simultaneously** — 無隙 Mugeki, 空爆 Kūbaku, 開門 Kaimon.
- **No Arakawa control checks while Shiokaze is up.**
- **No 荒神 Aragami risk.** The estate is being *used* rather than accreting.
- **器 Utsuwa is what makes it survivable.** The vessel coil exists precisely so a body could hold this — **and even so it is at the limit.**

## Why it is not the endpoint

**Shiokaze is one person's tide.** 渦潮 Uzushio is the same rotation grown large enough to carry other people inside it.

# PART FIVE — 大仙 DAISEN · "The Great Sage"

**[GM — taught personally by the White Snake Sage over three months. The Sage's condition is met.]**

**Not new techniques. The same techniques with the world inside them.** Every form he owns is now prefixed:

**大仙・潮風 Daisen Shiokaze · 大仙・潮合 Daisen Shioai · 大仙・手綱 Daisen Tazuna**

## Why it needed teaching at all

Everything Ardo built himself — Kikkō, Shioai, a year of holding the beat under attack — was **the discipline of not dying of natural energy.** Nobody ever taught him what it was *for.*

> *"You built a container. That was the hard part and I could not have taught it to you. **You have been carrying an empty cup very carefully for a year and calling it drinking.**"*
>
> *"You have been treating it as ballast — a weight on the other arm of a scale so your river does not tip you over. It is not ballast. **It is the world agreeing to participate.**"*

## The body

Senjutsu chakra does not sit still in a body and has opinions about the one it is in.

**While transformed** he gains four or five inches he does not otherwise have, and a frame leaner and harder than his own. The chakra no longer hangs around him: it **whips**, constantly, in long fast lashes off the shoulders, forearms and heels, cracking the air.

*(Historical note, Year −12: at ten years old the effect was that he did not look ten, which was deeply unpleasant for people who knew him — and incidentally the best disguise he owned. At 23 the transformation reads as an adult at the peak of something rather than a child wearing a stranger.)*

## THE SPEED — 無隙 Mugeki, evolved

Mugeki used to mean *he arrives instead of travelling.* **Now there is not even an arrival.**

**No blur. No displaced air. No crack, no line, no afterimage.** The eye does not register a transit because there is nothing in the transit to register — he is over there, and then the world contains him over here, and **the interval was not short, it was absent.**

- **No Reaction of any kind functions against him.** Substitution, Displace, reactive jutsu, Counter Windows, bonus-action repositioning — none of it.
- **Brace remains the only legal response**, and against Daisen it is closer to a formality than a defence.
- Monuke — who fought him daily for a year — **loses him completely inside ten seconds**, and says so with genuine offence.

> *"A lightning strike does not travel to the ground. It is simply, suddenly, a line between two places. **You have stopped being fast. You have started being a line.**"*

**Its deliberate use has a name: 音置き Otooki.** See `ardo-jutsu.md`.

## THE DETONATIONS — 空爆 Kūbaku, evolved

Kūbaku was a Taijutsu-only clause. **Now it is everything he touches.**

Wind chakra compressed past any density it should hold, threaded with senjutsu, delivered at the point of contact — **and it goes off.** Not displacement: a **detonation**, small and vicious.

**A punch. A kunai. A blade. A technique. A fingertip.** Anything.

> A bout ends in month two when Ardo **taps Roku's forearm** at half speed as a demonstration, knocks a jōnin off his feet, and cracks the stone behind him. *"That was a **tap.**" / "Yeah. I'm still working on that bit."*

## THE JUTSU — past the band

**Everything he casts in Daisen operates above the S-rank band.** Not "counts as" — *past* it. His existing kit rescaled by natural energy carrying the load, the way Sky-Torrent Downfall was only ever possible because senjutsu carried the mass.

### And they keep moving **[open-ended, GM-driven]**

> *"They are still moving."*

**His techniques evolve on their own.** Senjutsu chakra keeps working on a technique after he has finished with it — refining, compressing, occasionally producing something he did not build.

- **Gale Fist's recoil is gone. Permanently.** The Burned Page flaw that "could not be trained away" simply is not there anymore.
- **Compression Shell** has started doing something to the air around the target that he cannot account for.
- **Barometric Tsunami** came out **silent** one morning.

**He does not choose which and does not choose when. He finds out by casting.** *(Nao, from the archive, genuinely rattled for the first time: "…that is not a thing techniques* **do.**")

> **GM note:** this is a licence to hand Ardo an unrequested change to a technique at any time. It is not XP-driven and does not belong in the mastery table.

## THE COST — and it is not chakra

| | |
|---|---|
| **Chakra/Stamina** | **Still nothing.** The rotation returns what it takes |
| **The real price** | **Visibility. Every use is a decision to be found** |
| **Who notices** | Sensors at range · **every sage alive** · eventually, the people who erased Uzushio |

> *"I taught you to be uninteresting. And then I taught you this — and I want you to understand that **I have handed you two things that cannot both be used.**"*
>
> *"大仙 does not hide. It **cannot** hide. Natural energy at that volume is a **fire on a hill.**"*
>
> *"You are a sage of no species, taught by one anyway, carrying a dead clan and wearing a stolen eye. There has never been one of you. There will not be another. **Go and be boring, Ardo Uzumaki. For as long as you can stand it.**"*

**A Kumo bingo-book entry already exists**, unnamed: *wind, sage-class, do not engage alone.*

# PART SIX — 渦潮 UZUSHIO · "The Whirling Tide"

**[GM — the endpoint. Not available. Designed so it can be earned.]**

> **The name.** He will produce it himself, untaught, the way his hands produced Uzumaki sealwork without ever having seen a scroll. **渦潮 Uzushio is the name of the destroyed village of his own blood, and he will not know that when he says it.** Somebody will have to tell him afterward. ***Do not let him learn the word before he names it.***

### The principle

Every transformation in this setting is a thing **pushing** — more chakra, more force, more output, burning something to make it. Kōfū pushes. Sakauzu pushed until it broke everything nearby.

**A whirlpool does not push. It turns**, and everything that enters it joins the rotation.

Uzushio is **拮抗 Kikkō scaled to everything at once.** **The balance is the power. Not the force.**

### The four things in the rotation

1. **The river** — Arakawa, no longer a fault but the mass on one arm of the scale.
2. **Natural energy** — senjutsu, through the aperture he opened himself.
3. **The estate** — fully signed for, and **driven by him** rather than driving.
4. **The bonds** — 相眼 Sōgan and the closed circuit, scaled up. **This is the part no one else in this world has**, and it is what makes the form his rather than a variant of somebody else's.

### What it does **[design targets — tune at unlock]**

- **Nothing is spent.** In a closed rotation chakra circulates rather than depletes. **Uzushio has no sustain cost — it *returns* resources.** Alone among transformations in this setting.
- **Incoming techniques join the rotation.** Ninjutsu that reaches him is drawn into the whirl and released along a vector of his choosing.
- **The eye of the storm is the seal.** The whirl-pattern on his stomach is the still point, and the still point is where the estate's knowledge is legible — **fūinjutsu, medical technique, and skills he never learned become available.**
- **He can extend the circuit.** Allies inside the field receive the counterweight: their own chakra stabilised, their own exhaustion held off. **He does not get stronger alone. He gets stronger with people in the field.**
- **Senjutsu-grade throughout**, and Aragami is not present — there is nothing left to form.

### The three gates **[all required, none skippable]**

1. **THE BALANCE.** Senjutsu properly learned. ✅ **Cleared.**

# PART SEVEN — THE EYE

## 写輪眼 — SUZUME'S MANGEKYŌ, LEFT SOCKET **[GM — permanent]**

Suzume stepped in front of a full-force Kūbaku detonation from the inverted Sakauzu to shield an unconscious Souji. It killed her — 99 damage against 80 Health. Dying, she awakened the Mangekyō, removed her own left eye, put it in Ardo's hands, and said:

> *"See what I see... carry my ninja way with you."*

**Ardo dug out his own left eye in the mud and transplanted hers into the socket immediately** — no medic, no anaesthetic, nothing sterile, at the absolute floor of every resource he had.

**It was an exchange, not a gift.** He put his own removed eye into her empty socket in the same working. Both directions, both hands, in the dark. **Her instruction was not a metaphor — it was a build order, and she was a medic with thirty seconds left.**

### The Kakashi Problem **[permanent, no workaround]**

**Ardo is not an Uchiha.** A transplanted Sharingan in a non-Uchiha skull behaves exactly as the setting says:

- **It does not turn off. Ever.** No deactivating, no resting, no covering it to stop the drain — covering it helps him hide it, not power it.
- **Constant Chakra drain**, awake or asleep, in combat or out. **Treat his effective ceiling as permanently reduced.**
- **The Vision track applies to him**, and he has none of an Uchiha's tolerance for it.

**The gift:** every **passive** tier in his tree runs **always**, with no activation and no action cost. **He is never caught with his eye closed, because he does not have one to close.**

> **Neither he nor Kakashi Hatake knows about the other.** Two men in one village doing identical daily arithmetic, thirteen years running.

## 相眼 SŌGAN — "Paired Eyes" **[GM — permanent, unprecedented]**

| | Left | Right |
|---|---|---|
| **Ardo** | **Suzume's Mangekyō** *(never deactivates)* | his own |
| **Suzume** | **Ardo's ordinary eye** | her own Mangekyō |

**Nobody in this world has seen this.** Transplants run one way — Kakashi carries Obito's eye and Obito does not carry Kakashi's. **The circuit has never been closed.** Ardo closed it by accident, in mud, following instructions from a dying twelve-year-old.

### Confirmed behaviour

- **Sight bleeds across in both directions, uncontrolled**, at any range.
- **It functions while either party is unconscious.** Her Mangekyō opened while she was three days comatose, tracked him across a hospital room, and **looked directly at itself in his socket.**
- **Ardo's end never closes.**

### The cost

- **Neither of them is private from the other again. Ever.**
- **Whatever happens to one, the other attends** — at any distance, with no ability to refuse or intervene.

*She said "see what I see." She did not say it would only run one way.*

### THE CLOSED CIRCUIT **[the most dangerous thing he has learned]**

In her hospital room he took her hand, moved Rai's headband to cover **his own** eye so he was seeing only through hers, locked Mangekyō to Mangekyō, and pushed chakra out through her and back into himself in a closed loop. **Control check 21 vs DC 15.** It woke her.

**But this is the part that matters going forward.** The instant the loop closed, **Arakawa stopped fighting him.** Not calmed, not controlled — *given somewhere to go.*

**That is what the trustee was doing his entire life, and he now knows it.**

> **The circuit requires a second person.** The only relief he has ever found from Arakawa is a closed loop with someone on the other end. **This is not a solution. It is a temptation**, and it is there every time senjutsu feels too slow, too far, or too hard.

## THE THREE ROADS OUT **[the arc — all genuinely open]**
