# PART ONE — 写輪眼 THE SHARINGAN

| | Left socket | Right socket |
|---|---|---|
| **Suzume** | **Ardo's ordinary eye** *(transplanted)* | **Her own MANGEKYŌ SHARINGAN** |

**She is not missing an eye and is not half-blind. She is carrying his.**

## Awakening — the first time

**Rai Inuzuka died in front of her** during the Shepherd fight. Her eyes changed before she had time to consciously understand what she was seeing — **two tomoe in each eye**, at Level 1.

**[CONFLICT — PRESERVED.]** The printed progression places the first Sharingan stages behind level and trauma requirements. She awakened at Level 1 and reached 2 tomoe in her first major trauma trigger. **Table ruling: a narrative extremity exception**, not an erasure of the normal progression. The factors were all established beforehand — long-term unexplained migraines, abnormal visual strain, a dormant Uchiha bloodline, a confirmed medical connection, extreme battlefield trauma, and Rai dying directly in front of her.

## Baseline Sharingan benefits — always available

| | |
|---|---|
| **Genjutsu perception** | **Advantage against Genjutsu** |
| **Hand-seal reading** | Reads and recognises hand-seal sequences far faster than an ordinary shinobi |
| **Predictive Reaction** | **Reaction: impose disadvantage on one enemy attack** |
| **Upkeep** | **3 Chakra per round while active** |

## 万華鏡写輪眼 — THE MANGEKYŌ **[Session Eight, permanent]**

**Printed unlock:** Level 14 + the death of someone loved, played out in fiction.
**Actual unlock:** **Level 5, nine levels early.**

### How it actually happened — the trigger fired sideways

She threw herself between an unconscious Souji and a full-force 空爆 Kūbaku detonation from the inverted Sakauzu wearing Ardo. **She took 99 damage against 80 maximum Health and did not dodge, brace, or substitute** — she turned her back on the punch so her body would cover Souji's completely. **It worked exactly as intended. Souji took nothing.**

> **The printed condition is *the death of someone loved.* It came true from the inside out.** She was not grieving someone else's death — **she was dying, watching, with the clearest eyes on that field, what her death was about to do to Ardo, and unable to reach him or stop it.**

**Pattern:** the two tomoe stretch and run together into a single sweep of black doubling back on itself twice — **a small bird's wing caught at the top of a beat.** *Suzume* means sparrow.

# PART TWO — SHE PAYS NO VISION

> ## ★ SUZUME HAS NO VISION TRACK AND WILL NEVER GO BLIND.
>
> **The eye is hers.** Native Uchiha tissue, in a native Uchiha body, awakened the way the clan's eyes are meant to awaken. **There is nothing in her for it to burn out.**
>
> **She can push it as hard as she likes, for as long as she likes, for the rest of her life.** The only limits are Chakra, Special, and how tired she is.
>
> **Ardo is the one paying.** The degradation is not a rule about the Mangekyō — it is a rule about **a non-Uchiha wearing one.** His track, his headband discipline, and his three roads out are in `ardo-transformations.md`. **Her tiers cost him nothing; only the shared Sōgan tree does, and he pays all of that too.**

## She knows

**Nobody told her. She worked it out** — Mitate, on her own best friend, month six of the Ryūchi year, because she was always going to eventually.

> *"Your eye is dying and mine isn't."* — *"…Yeah."* — *"And you were going to just not mention that."*

**Four hours. Nothing solved.** The three roads out discussed aloud for the first time, **including the one nobody was ever going to say.** She said ***"I'd give you the other one."*** He said ***"I know — that's exactly why I'm never asking."*** Neither moved.

**Permanently in the open between them. Unresolved.**

# PART THREE — 相眼 SŌGAN · "Paired Eyes"

**[GM — permanent, unprecedented. Full shared writeup in `ardo-transformations.md`.]**

The transplant ran **both directions.** **The circuit is closed, which has never been done.**

### Confirmed behaviour

- **Sight bleeds across in both directions, uncontrolled**, at any range, with no ability to refuse.
- **It functions while either party is unconscious.** First confirmed three days after the exchange, when her Mangekyō opened while she was still comatose and **tracked Ardo across a hospital room to look directly at itself in his socket.**
- **Ardo's end never closes**, because his eye cannot deactivate.
- **She attends everything that happens to him**, at any distance, with no ability to intervene.

### The cost

**Neither of them is private from the other again. Ever.**

She has spent thirteen years receiving another person's life in fragments — **and she is the reason the squad found him at all.** Sōgan Tier I is involuntary and ignores distance; it made her the compass across a continent, and she has been right about the difference between good quiet and bad quiet **every single time**, before anyone including Ardo could tell.

**Her eye reads *people*** — what is broken in them, what they can do, and what they are about to try. **Everything here is deliberate and activated, and hers switches off when she wants it to.**

**Unlock gating is story and training, not Vision.** Each tier needs a real reason — a teacher, a breakthrough, a fight that demands it.

# PART FIVE — 迎え火 MUKAEBI · "The Welcoming Fire"

**[GM — named Session Eight in a hospital. BUILT over nine months in Ryūchi Cave.]**

> *Mukaebi: the lantern lit at Obon so someone a long way out in the dark can find the road back to your door.*

## What it does

**It reaches a mind that cannot be reached** — unconscious, comatose, dissociated, genjutsu-locked, or lost inside its own vow — **and lights her up inside it** so they can find their own way back.

**Not a weapon. Not a genjutsu. No damage, no illusion, no mind-theft.** In a clan whose Mangekyō gifts are black flame and world-breaking illusion, **Suzume's awakened power is going to get somebody and bringing them home.**

## How it was discovered

She spent **three days comatose — awake the entire time**, alone, in a mind-built copy of her own hospital room **with no door**, methodically testing the walls in rows the way a medic works a body looking for the break. **She had stopped and sat down by the time he found her.**

Her Mangekyō, sitting in Ardo's skull with no owner and no instruction, spent those same seventy-two hours **reaching for him** on pure instinct. **Every uncontrolled Sōgan bleed was that. It needed someone to look back.**

Ardo took her hand, **moved Rai's headband to cover his *own* eye** so he was seeing only through hers, opened a closed chakra circuit between them, and looked. **Control check: 21 vs DC 15.** The wall opened. She woke.

> **She built the door with her last thirty seconds and could not open it from the inside.** The eye exchange was never about arming him — **it was a line back, laid by a medic who understood exactly how likely it was that she was about to be somewhere nobody could reach.**
>
> **It would have saved Souji from the vow in his own voice. It would have reached Ardo through the thing wearing him.** It awakened one second too late for either, and **the first person it ever saved was herself.**

## Mechanics **[BUILT — Ryūchi Cave, nine months]**

**Yoi did most of the teaching**, which surprised everyone and should not have — the owls find what is hidden **by listening**, and Mukaebi is the technique of finding someone lost inside themselves.

> *"You have been treating it as a rescue. **It is not. It is a lamp.** You do not go in and drag them out — you stand where you are and make yourself findable, and then you wait. **Waiting is the entire skill and you are terrible at it.**"*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | **Special.** **It exhausts her more than anything else she does** |
| **Upkeep** | **8 Chakra per round** while the lamp is lit |
| **Hard limit** | **She is a light, not a hand.** She cannot force, drag, or compel. **A hostile mind can refuse her** |
| **Vision** | **None to her, ever.** *(Mangekyō-grade on Ardo's side if it is ever routed through him)* |
| **Range** | Contact preferred; **Sōgan extends it to Ardo at any distance** |

# PART SIX — 無戸の間 MUTO NO MA · "The Doorless Room"

**[GM — Mangekyō genjutsu · A-RANK SIGNATURE]**

**3 slots · 40 Special · Genjutsu DC 16 · eye contact required**

> ## She built the worst thing in this campaign out of the worst thing that ever happened to her.

**迎え火 Mukaebi is a lamp lit so somebody lost can find their way home.**
**無戸の間 is the same room with the lamp taken out.**

## The technique

**Eye contact. CHA save vs her Genjutsu DC 16.** On a failure the target wakes somewhere they feel **completely safe** — their own home, a childhood room, a barracks bunk, whatever the eye finds. **It is perfect. It is warm. Every detail is correct.**

**And there is no door.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **No pain, no monsters, no torture** | **Nothing happens at all. That is the technique** |
| **Time runs wrong** | Seconds outside are **days** inside. **A three-round genjutsu is a week alone in a room** |
| **They stay lucid** | No merciful confusion. **They know exactly what is happening**, and they search, and they are methodical, and they find nothing — **because she built it out of a place she personally could not escape** |
| **Breaking it** | **Chakra disruption does not work. Physical shaking does not work.** Somebody has to **come and get them** — reach in from outside, deliberately. ***That is the only thing that ever worked for her*** |
| **Cost** | **Special, and the memory.** She is handing over her own three days |

> **She can end it whenever she wants, and does not always.** Ardo asked her once, in year −8, whether she was alright about that. **She said "no,"** did not elaborate, and it has not been raised since.
