## Why he plays the way he plays

**Two cells before this one.** Genzo's squad dissolved when Genzo died — a line on a report and no sensei left to run it. The second lasted **eleven days**, until the squad needed to absorb a clan heir mid-cycle for political reasons nobody explained to a genin, **and the easiest name to move off the roster was the clanless one.**

**Nobody told him it was personal. It wasn't. That's almost worse.**

> His response was never bitterness. It was **making sure nobody ever had an operational reason to move him again.** Be quietly, relentlessly *useful*, and the paperwork stops mattering.
>
> **That is the wound. Every single thing that has happened to him since runs through it.**

## Passives

**POWERFUL LIFE FORCE** `[SHEET — Body of Ashura]` — **Recover 5 Stamina at the start of each combat round.**

**MASTERY OF THE EARTH** `[SHEET — Senju]` — **+2 CON / +1 STR** (in the block above) · **+25% of maximum Health on a long rest** · **once per long rest**, a full round of genuine stillness restores **20 Stamina / 10 Chakra**.

**CLANLESS** `[SHEET]` — **+1 STR** (applied) · **one extra starting feat** (Unbroken Stance) and **one extra starting jutsu** (完結 Kanketsu) · **cannot naturally learn Kekkei Genkai or clan-exclusive jutsu.**

> **This is the build Naruto, Rock Lee and Might Gai run**, and the sheet is explicit that it is not a consolation prize.
>
> **⚠ And the restriction did not go away when the Senju blood surfaced.** He gained clan *vitality*, not clan *techniques*. **He still cannot learn Mokuton by any ordinary route** — see `souji-transformations.md`.

**READS THE ROOM BEFORE HE SPEAKS** `[GM]` — When he has a moment to watch someone before engaging, **advantage on the first Perception or Insight-equivalent check** about them that scene. *He spent a year being the new man dropped into rooms he didn't choose; he is good at fast, accurate reads because he has had to be.*

**UNBROKEN STANCE** `[GM — Clanless bonus feat]` — **Once per long rest**, when an effect that isn't outright unconsciousness would force him prone, **spend 5 Stamina to remain standing, fully able to act.** No save to earn it. **Does not work against direct unconsciousness, death effects, paralysis, or sealing.**
