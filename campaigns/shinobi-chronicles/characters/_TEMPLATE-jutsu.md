> ## FORMAT RULES
>
> 1. **Every technique states its slot cost**, not just its action type. Slot cost follows rank (Book IV §2 Part Two) unless an explicit `[GM]` override says otherwise **and gives a reason.**
> 2. **Every save states the actual DC**, not a stale one. **Recompute when the character levels.** Frozen DCs were the single most common error found by the architecture audit.
> 3. **Costs must sit inside the rank's band** (Book IV §1 Part Seven). If a technique's numbers fall outside it, either **rerank it** to match its output or **reprice it** to match its label — and **record which, and why, in the Corrections table.**
> 4. **Never duplicate a compendium technique's description here.** Reference the rank file.
> 5. **Mastery ladders describe what each tier *grants*.** The character's current position on the ladder lives in `campaign-state.md`.
