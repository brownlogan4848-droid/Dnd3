> ## FORMAT RULES
>
> 1. **Append at the end of every session**, after `session-log.md` and before reconciling `campaign-state.md`.
> 2. **Never move a fact into CONFIRMED without recording how they learned it.** The "how" is what stops knowledge leaking between characters.
> 3. **Keep BELIEVES populated.** A character who is right about everything has no arc.
> 4. **No mechanics here.** If you are writing a number, it belongs in another file.
