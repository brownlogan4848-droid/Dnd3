> ## FORMAT RULES
>
> 1. **A transformation's *cost* and its *risk* are different fields.** Activation cost is what you pay to get in; risk is what happens if you stay.
> 2. **Every GM-run form must say so at the top.** If the player loses control, that is the most important fact about it.
> 3. **If a form overrides a general rule** (rank ceiling, action economy, Reaction count), **cite the rule it overrides.** Character files may override Book IV only with an explicit reason — `ARCHITECTURE.md` §1.
> 4. **Obsolete forms are kept, not deleted**, with a note saying what superseded them and why using them is now a worse decision.
