# PART ONE — THE TURN

**Year 0, October 10th.** In roughly four minutes, in a clearing outside a burning Konoha:

1. **He came down the slope without being asked** and poured 息吹 Ibuki into Kushina Uzumaki — his own Stamina and Health, at the deliberately bad exchange rate — **buying Ardo the ninety seconds in which he learned his mother's name from another Uzumaki.**
2. He knelt there while Ardo accused him of having been absent, of clinging to Danzō, and of knowing exactly where to be.
3. **Minato defended him**, unprompted, from the ground, holding his dead wife. **Suzume defended him louder.**
4. Ardo moved to pull him into a hug and apologise.
5. **He put his sword through Ardo's stomach**, through live seal work, and pulled it out.
6. He gave a speech. He put Suzume into a treeline with a taijutsu barrage.
7. He swung to cut Ardo down. **Minato Namikaze stepped into it and took the blow instead.**
8. **He felt the wrong body take the hit** — and for one unguarded second the boy underneath was right there, staring at what his own hand had done.
9. **He left.**

## What he said

> *"Sorry, Ardo… **we must grow stronger.**"*
>
> *"Always chasing after your shadows… never strong enough to be recognised for my own merits and strengths… all these years spent trying to achieve our dreams only for me to realise that — **power is what accomplishes your dreams and aspirations.**"*
>
> *"**Danzō taught me that**, and maybe a few lessons on how to hide my true nature. But that was easy, Ardo. My whole life I've spent hiding from my true self. **I'm done denying that part of me.**"*
>
> ### *"When you're ready, truly ready, come find me."*

## What he said earlier, that nobody has weighed

Ten minutes before, accused of being Danzō's, he **flatly rejected the framing:**

> *"**It's not Danzō. It's never been Danzō.** Get that part right if you're going to say it in front of her body."*

And, on how he knew where to be:

> *"I've spent three months making sure none of you could find me. **I never once stopped finding you.** I know your chakra signature the way I know my own hands — I've known it for thirteen years. I felt it go wrong from four ridgelines away and I ran. **That's the whole terrible secret. I was never gone. I just made it look like I was.**"*
>
> *"Ask me about Orochimaru if you want. I'll tell you every ugly true thing about that, and there's plenty, and none of us are going to like all of it. **But don't you dare make this the thing that finally proves I was right to stop trusting people with it.**"*

> ## ⚠ [DECISION NEEDED] — the two statements do not reconcile.
> He denied Danzō by name, then credited Danzō by name, in the same scene. **Either it is a lie protecting a different handler** — Orochimaru remains the only person he had spoken to for three months, on every sheet — **or he is distinguishing *who employs him* from *who taught him*.**
>
> **The audit will not choose.** A table ruling decides it, and it changes what the party is actually chasing. Flagged in `campaign/campaign-state.md`.

# PART TWO — WHY

**Nothing about this was sudden, and all of it was documented.**

## The vow

**根の記憶 Ne no Kioku has delivered bloodline knowledge to Souji every single session since Session Eight — in his own furious voice.** That voice:

> *"Don't take his hand. **The second you need somebody is the second you can be left again.**"*

**It has been talking to him, uninterrupted, for thirteen years. Nobody built a counter to it.** Everyone assumed Ardo's hand on the slope was the answer — **and it was, for one afternoon, thirteen years ago.**

**And it has thirteen years of evidence now.** Souji gave everything he had, to everyone, constantly, for over a decade — **and people still died anyway.**

## The drift, in order

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Year −3** | **Suzume flagged it.** Shorter with people, sharper when tired, gone longer than missions needed, answering questions with the answer and nothing around it. **Ardo said *"he's always been like that"*** — true five years earlier, and by then it had stopped being true |
| **Last three years** | Slow, deniable withdrawal |
| **Last three months** | **He cut everyone off — administratively, not dramatically.** Stopped coming to things. Stopped being in the places he'd be. Answered when spoken to and started no conversations. **Has not slept in the squad's quarters since midsummer** |
| **Throughout** | **He spoke to exactly one person: Orochimaru.** Suzume asked twice and was told *"he's a colleague"* — **which is not a lie and is not an answer** |
| **Year 0** | Danzō, by his own account |

## The thing that was never built

**静かな部屋 the Quiet Room** — Suzume's dōjutsu Tier IV — **is the technique that reaches the vow underneath his ribs.** It was named, costed, and explicitly identified as *"the technique she needed on that training ground and did not have."*

**It is still locked.**

> **Thirteen years. One four-hour silence that worked, once. And the reliable counter, never built.**

# PART SIX — OPEN THREADS

1. **Where he is, and whose he is.** The Danzō/Orochimaru contradiction — Part One.
2. **Has 朽木 Kuchiki been taught to keep what it takes?** Flagged on three sheets for years as the corruption already sitting in his kit. `souji-transformations.md`.
3. **Does 息吹 Ibuki still answer him?** It exists only when he needs somebody else to be alright. **He used it an hour before he defected.**
4. **Does the Ishinuma contract still answer?** A four-century-old intelligence with a standing debt would have opinions. **And it is the party's most plausible route to finding him.**
5. **静かな部屋** — locked, and it is the door back.
6. **"When you're ready, truly ready, come find me."** **That is an invitation, not a threat.** He gave them a way to reach him, and he did not have to.
7. **What happened to the rest of Genzo's original squad.** Souji was the only one reassigned. **Never resolved.**
8. **Kanketsu's actual origin.** He knows Genzo taught it. **He does not know where *Genzo* learned it**, or whether it has a name anywhere outside the two of them who ever used it.
9. **The Hyūga heir who displaced him from his second cell.** Never met. Doesn't resent them personally. **Konoha isn't that big.**
