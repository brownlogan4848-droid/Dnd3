# PART ONE — WHO SHE IS RIGHT NOW

**Suzume · 27 · Jōnin · Konohagakure · 遊撃班 Yūgekihan · field medic**

Uchiha by blood and clanless on every document. Carries a Mangekyō that costs her nothing and a friend's ordinary eye in the other socket. **Built the kindest technique in this campaign and the cruellest one, out of the same three days.**

## The Three Questions

**1 — Who taught you?** **Rokka Uehara**, day to day. Then **宵 Yoi**, who taught her Mukaebi by telling her she was terrible at waiting. Then thirteen years of the war, which taught her the rest.

**2 — What did the war take from you?** **Rai Inuzuka**, in front of her, which opened her eyes. Then the habit of counting — she stopped in Year −8 and calls it the worst decision she ever made.

**3 — What would make you break the code?** *Unrecorded on the original sheet.* **What the record shows instead:** she has already broken the medic's second and third rules — *never stand on the front line*, *be the last to die on your squad* — by stepping in front of a detonation at zero range with no intention of surviving it. **She would do it again and has said so.**
