# SQUAD TWO — THE SEARCH FOR ARDO

**Running log of Roku, Souji, and Suzume while Ardo is in 龍地洞 Ryūchi Cave.**

The campaign splits at Session Nine. Ardo's arc runs underground with the snakes; this file tracks everyone else. **Cut to this squad periodically** — they are not on pause, they are walking, and things happen to them.

---

## THE SPLIT

**Session Nine, one day south-east of Konoha.** Ardo's uncontracted summoning attempt rolled a 24 and **reverse-summoned him** — no smoke, no flash, a fused glassy circle in the dirt and a pack left standing upright in the grass. Roku warned it was possible and warned he could not follow.

**What the summons told them.** Gensai (tortoises) and Yoi (owls) named the three sage realms:

| Realm | Species | Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mount Myōboku** | Toads | Southeast, past the mountains | Warm, green, loud |
| **Shikkotsu Forest** | Slugs | Far southwest | Old growth, wet, towering trees. They heal there |
| **龍地洞 Ryūchi Cave** | Snakes | **Far northeast, past the Land of Frost** | Under an unsurveyed mountain range. *"The surveyors did not come back"* |

**The one data point:** Suzume, through 相眼 Sōgan, felt him land — **"cold, and dark. Not night-dark. Under-dark."** Yoi's assessment: not proof, but not a forest and not a mountaintop, and it is the only data point on the continent.

**Decision: northeast.** Best case three months on foot, realistically four, across borders that will not cooperate — but walking away from every active front, which is the only part Roku likes.

---

## STANDING FACTS

- **Suzume is the compass.** Sōgan Tier I is involuntary and ignores distance. She has been receiving fragments continuously since he landed — cold, stone, something very large moving nearby. **She will know if he is hurt, if he is learning, or if he stops.** If the direction is wrong, she expects to know that too.
- **Souji is carrying two packs.** He picked up Ardo's without being asked and nobody has told him to put it down. His stated position: *"He didn't leave. Something took him. So we go get him. It's just a long walk."*
- **Roku's map runs out past the Land of Frost.** Beyond that they are navigating on summon-knowledge and a girl's half-eye.
- **They travel as 遊撃班 Yūgekihan** — papers at the bottom of the bag, no saying "village" out loud, four people on a road.
- **Both new contracts are one day old.** Gensai needs a full round of stillness to call. Yoi tells them everything she finds whether or not they asked.

---

## OPEN THREADS TRAVELLING WITH THEM

- The Uchiha have not yet learned that Suzume's eye left the clan. Every month outside the village buys time.
- Danzō withdrew rather than retreated. Root notices absences.
- Souji's 根の記憶 delivers one real piece of bloodline knowledge per session, unbidden, in his own furious voice — **including the stand of old-growth trees he saw three days before leaving and has still told nobody about.**
- Suzume's 迎え火 Mukaebi is named and unbuilt. Her dōjutsu tree has no tiers unlocked.
- Yoi reports what she finds. All of it. Including about each other.

| The will, read | *"...he's crying."* |
| **潮合 Shioai** | *"He's quiet — but quiet like the rooftops after the Shepherd, not quiet like a room with the door shut. **He's doing it.**"* Roku: *"Then let's not keep him waiting."* |

**She has been right about the difference between good and bad quiet every single time**, before anyone including Ardo could tell.

**Souji is still carrying two packs.** Nobody has told him to put one down.
