# Naruto Jutsu Compendium — C-RANK

Chūnin-level techniques. The tier where all five basic natures appear, summoning becomes available, and clan techniques take on real combat weight.

---

## Ninjutsu & General Skills

### Summoning Technique
**Japanese:** Kuchiyose no Jutsu (口寄せの術)
**Type:** Space–Time Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** After signing a blood contract with a species, the user marks their hand with blood and instantly calls that creature to their location from wherever it resides. The size and power of what arrives scales with the chakra invested — a modest offering brings a small scout, while a full commitment can summon a boss creature the size of a mountain. The contract is exclusive and binding; a shinobi generally serves one species for life. The foundation of the toad, snake, and slug contracts.

### Transparent Escape Technique
**Japanese:** Tōton no Jutsu (透遁の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Renders the user effectively invisible to the naked eye, allowing them to slip away from pursuit or move into position for an ambush. Considerably more effective than the Academy-level Cloak of Invisibility, since it conceals the user themselves rather than draping them in camouflaged cloth, though it remains vulnerable to sensory perception and ocular techniques.

### Adamantine Chain
**Japanese:** Kongō no Kusari (金剛の鎖)
**Type:** Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user manifests chains of solidified chakra from their body to bind, restrain, or ensnare a target. An Uzumaki-associated ability, the chains possess extraordinary tensile strength and can hold creatures far larger and stronger than the user. In their more developed applications the same chains serve as barrier and sealing implements rather than simple restraints.

### Ink Mist
**Japanese:** Sumigasumi no Jutsu (墨霞の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user spins a cloud of ink around their body to blot out the enemy's vision and cover a withdrawal. Rarely used alone — paired with the Body Flicker Technique it produces a near-guaranteed escape, since the opponent loses sight of the user precisely at the moment they move.

### Toad Subjugation
**Japanese:** Kaeru Kaeru no Jutsu
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user places a hand on a target's forehead protector and transforms them into a toad. Its purpose is coercion rather than combat — a captive turned into an animal in front of their comrades is a powerful interrogation lever, and the threat of permanence loosens tongues quickly.

### Toad Hiding Technique
**Japanese:** Gamagakure no Jutsu (ガマ隠れの術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user conceals themselves inside a summoned diving toad capable of descending to considerable depths underwater. The toad's interior functions as a spatial barrier that masks the user's chakra signature entirely, making this one of the few concealment methods effective against sensor-type opponents.

### Snake Authority Spell
**Japanese:** Jagei Jubaku (蛇威縛)
**Type:** Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Two large snakes erupt from the user's sleeves and coil around a target to pin them in place. The technique is deliberately flexible in outcome — the snakes can hold a target alive for capture, constrict until bones break, or deliver venom, all at the user's discretion.

### Thousand Crows
**Japanese:** Sanzengarasu no Jutsu (三千烏の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user summons an enormous flock of chakra-controlled crows that replicate themselves mid-flight until they blot out the sky. The swarm blinds and disorients everyone caught inside it while the user moves freely, and the constant motion and noise make tracking by sight or sound nearly impossible.

## Clone Techniques

### Water Clone Technique
**Japanese:** Mizu Bunshin no Jutsu (水分身の術)
**Type:** Water Release / Clone Technique
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Creates a tangible clone formed from water, possessing roughly a tenth of the original's power. Unlike illusory clones it can strike, block, and be struck, reverting to water when destroyed. Its limitation is range — the clone cannot travel far from its source water before losing cohesion — which makes it excellent for holding ground and terrible for pursuit.

### Ink Clone Technique
**Japanese:** Sumi Bunshin no Jutsu (墨分身の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Clone Technique
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user draws a clone into existence with ink and brings it to life. It carries substantially more mass than a standard clone, which makes it far harder to identify as a fake, and it can be dispatched independently for infiltration or long-range reconnaissance without the user leaving cover.

### Crow Clone Technique
**Japanese:** Karasu Bunshin no Jutsu (烏分身の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Clone Technique
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user forms a body out of a flock of crows, which scatters into birds when struck. It requires dramatically less chakra than a shadow clone while still absorbing an attack, and the dispersal itself is disorienting — an opponent who lands a killing blow watches the corpse explode into wings.

## Fire Release

### Fire Release: Great Fireball Technique
**Japanese:** Katon: Gōkakyū no Jutsu (火遁・豪火球の術)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user kneads chakra in the chest and expels it through the mouth as an enormous roaring sphere of flame, or as a sustained stream by maintaining the exhale. The Uchiha clan's signature technique and their traditional coming-of-age benchmark — a child who can perform it is considered a true member of the clan. Chakra-hungry enough that a novice may be left winded after a single use.

### Fire Release: Phoenix Sage Fire Technique
**Japanese:** Katon: Hōsenka no Jutsu (火遁・鳳仙火の術)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Rather than one large mass of flame, the user spits a volley of smaller fireballs that can each be aimed independently, saturating an area and denying the target a clean dodge. Shuriken or other weapons can be concealed inside the individual flames, so an opponent who successfully evades the fire may still take steel.

### Fire Release: Flame Bullet
**Japanese:** Katon: Endan (火遁・炎弾)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user expels chakra-infused oil from the mouth and ignites it, producing a large and sustained gout of fire. Because it burns fuel rather than raw chakra, the flame persists far longer than a standard fire technique and clings to what it touches. It can be shaped into one massive column or split into multiple smaller bullets.

### Fire Release: Dragon Fire Technique
**Japanese:** Katon: Ryūka no Jutsu (火遁・龍火の術)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Sends a compact, dragon-headed flame streaking at the target, most often guided along a pre-attached wire so it cannot be evaded. When combined with wire-binding it becomes a finisher — the target is already immobilized when the fire arrives and travels down the wire directly into them.

### Fire Release: Fire Dragon Bullet
**Japanese:** Katon: Karyūdan (火遁・火龍弾)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Expels a dragon-shaped bullet of concentrated fire at the target. More focused and directional than the Great Fireball, trading area coverage for penetrating force along a single line.

### Toad Oil Bullet
**Japanese:** Gama Yudan (ガマ油弾)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user spits a mass of thick, sticky chakra-oil that adheres to whatever it strikes and bogs the target down. On its own it is a restraint technique, but its intended role is as a setup — a follow-up fire technique turns the coated target into a torch.

### Fireball Fists
**Japanese:** —
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user sheathes both fists in flame, adding searing damage to ordinary strikes. It converts a close-quarters exchange into a punishing one, since blocking a burning punch with bare arms is nearly as costly as taking it.

## Water Release

### Water Prison Technique
**Japanese:** Suirō no Jutsu (水牢の術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Encases a target in a sphere of impossibly dense water that resists all movement and denies them air. The prison is effectively inescapable from the inside for anyone weaker than the caster. Its critical flaw is that the user must keep one arm inserted into the sphere to maintain it, anchoring them in place and leaving them as immobilized as their captive.

### Water Release: Hiding in Water Technique
**Japanese:** Suiton: Suichūgakure no Jutsu (水遁・水中隠れの術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user submerges into a body of water and suppresses their presence within it, becoming undetectable from the surface. Combined with the ability to breathe and move freely underwater, it turns any lake, river, or flooded battlefield into an ambush position.

### Water Release: Water Trumpet
**Japanese:** Suiton: Mizurappa (水遁・水喇叭)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user cups a hand into a funnel at the mouth and blasts out a concentrated jet of high-pressure water. The hand-funnel narrows the stream to increase both range and penetrating force, making it a precise strike rather than an area attack.

### Water Release: Gunshot
**Japanese:** Suiton: Teppōdama (水遁・鉄砲玉)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Fires one or more large compressed spheres of water at tremendous velocity. The projectiles hit with the blunt force of cannon shot, punching through stone and levelling structures. Famously used by the toad boss Gamabunta, whose scale turns each shot into a demolition round.

### Water Release: Wild Water Wave
**Japanese:** Suiton: Mizuranha (水遁・水乱波)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Expels a waterfall-like torrent from the mouth that batters and sweeps away everything in its path. Less precise than the Water Trumpet but far broader, it is used to clear groups, extinguish fire techniques, and flood terrain for follow-up water attacks.

### Water Release: Starch Syrup Capturing Field
**Japanese:** Suiton: Mizuame Nabara (水遁・水飴捕縛の場)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user spits a viscous, syrup-thick water across the ground, creating a field that traps and slows anyone standing in it. Rather than damaging the target it strips them of mobility — a devastating handicap in a fight decided by speed.

### Water Release: Waterfall Basin Technique
**Japanese:** Suiton: Takitsubo no Jutsu (水遁・滝壺の術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Generates a large volume of falling water from a comparatively small source, producing an artificial waterfall. Primarily a utility technique — it supplies the water other Water Release techniques require in environments that have none, and doubles as a training apparatus.

### Water Release: Bubble Trumpet
**Japanese:** Suiton: Hōmatsu Rappa (水遁・泡沫喇叭)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Sprays a dense volume of bubbles that whiten and fill an area, obscuring vision and masking movement. Beyond concealment it neutralizes oil-based and fire-based techniques by washing away their fuel before they can ignite.

## Earth Release

### Earth Release: Hiding Like a Mole Technique
**Japanese:** Doton: Moguragakure no Jutsu (土遁・土竜隠れの術)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Softens the earth ahead of the user so they can burrow into solid ground and move through it freely. Serves equally as evasion — dropping out of the path of an attack — and as an approach, allowing the user to surface directly beneath an unsuspecting target.

### Earth Release: Underground Projection Fish Technique
**Japanese:** Doton: Dochū Eigyo no Jutsu (土遁・土中映魚の術)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user swims through the earth as a fish moves through water, travelling at speed while completely concealed. Faster and more maneuverable than simple burrowing, it is built for closing distance and striking from below before the target registers an attack.

### Earth Release: Underground Submarine Voyage
**Japanese:** Doton: Dochū Senkō (土遁・土中潜行)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Liquefies the ground so the user can move through it at high speed, then erupts upward into the target. The violence of the surfacing is the point — the attack arrives with the full momentum of the underground charge behind it.

## Wind Release

### Wind Release: Great Breakthrough
**Japanese:** Fūton: Daitoppa (風遁・大突破)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user exhales a powerful blast of wind capable of hurling enemies backward, stripping cover, and scattering projectiles mid-flight. Its most tactical use is combinational: it fans allied fire techniques to enormous size, or blows an enemy's flames back into them.

### Sickle Weasel Technique
**Japanese:** Kamaitachi no Jutsu (カマイタチの術)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Generates a whirlwind carrying invisible blades of compressed air that slice everything caught inside it. Because the cutting edges cannot be seen, defending against it means guarding against the entire vortex rather than a visible attack line. Performed with a swing of a battle fan by its best-known users.

### Wind Release: Gale Palm
**Japanese:** Fūton: Reppūshō (風遁・烈風掌)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user claps their hands to compress and release a sudden gust of wind. On its own it staggers a target, but its real function is as an accelerant — thrown weapons launched into the current gain drastic speed and force, turning ordinary shuriken into armor-piercing projectiles.

## Lightning Release

### Lightning Release: Dance of 100 Volts
**Japanese:** —
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** A lightning-natured offensive technique that discharges current through a target on contact, causing muscular seizure and burns. The electrical shock disrupts the nervous system, leaving the victim unable to coordinate movement even after the current stops.

### Lightning Release: Electromagnetic Murder
**Japanese:** —
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Channels a lethal electromagnetic discharge into the target. Lightning-natured attacks of this kind bypass much of the protection physical toughness provides, since the damage propagates through the body's own conductivity rather than through the surface.

### Lightning Release: Lightning Beast Tracking Fang
**Japanese:** Raiton: Raijū Hashiri (雷遁・雷獣走り)
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user shapes lightning chakra into the form of a beast that races along the ground and pursues its target rather than travelling in a straight line. The homing behavior makes simple evasion ineffective, forcing the target to block, disperse, or ground the attack.

### Lightning Release: Spider Web
**Japanese:** —
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Spreads an electrified web of lightning across an area to snare and shock anything that touches it. Functions as both trap and area denial — a target driven into the web is held in place by the current while it burns them.

## Sound-Based Ninjutsu

### Extreme Decapitating Airwaves
**Japanese:** Zankūkyokuha (斬空極波)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (sound / air pressure)
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The maximum-output form of the Decapitating Airwaves, releasing supersonic waves and compressed air at full pressure through both arms at once. The blast pulverizes stone and tears apart the terrain in a wide cone. Pushed to this intensity the technique strains the user's own arms severely.

### Resonating Echo Drill
**Japanese:** Kyōmeisen (共鳴穿)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (sound)
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user amplifies sound through a device worn on the arm and directs it into the target's ear, where it wrecks the inner ear and destroys their sense of balance. The victim is left unable to stand or aim, and because sound travels faster than a person can move, the attack pursues them regardless of evasion.

## Genjutsu

### Demonic Illusion: False Surroundings Technique
**Japanese:** Magen: Kokoni Arazu no Jutsu (魔幻・此処非の術)
**Type:** Genjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Overlays an illusion across an entire location so that it appears to be somewhere else entirely — a forest where there is a building, an empty road where there is an ambush. Because it blankets an area rather than targeting an individual, anyone who walks into the affected zone is caught automatically without the caster needing to engage them.

### Demonic Illusion: Flower Mist
**Japanese:** Magen: Hanagasumi (魔幻・花霞)
**Type:** Genjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Envelops the target in an illusory mist of drifting flower petals that fogs their perception and misdirects their sense of where the caster is. The beauty of the imagery is functional — it holds attention, and a target watching petals is not watching the real attack.

### Genjutsu: Flower Petal Escape
**Japanese:** Genjutsu: Hanafubuki (幻術・花吹雪)
**Type:** Genjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** A swirling storm of illusory petals fills the air, providing visual cover for the caster to vanish, reposition, or strike from an unexpected angle. Built for disengagement and repositioning rather than damage.

## Taijutsu

### Lion Combo
**Japanese:** Shishi Rendan (獅子連弾)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user launches an opponent into the air, shadows them upward, and delivers a chain of strikes that adapts in real time to the target's attempts to counter — each attempted defense is read and turned into the setup for the next blow. The sequence finishes with a driving kick that slams the target into the ground from height. Its execution effectively requires the Sharingan to track and predict the target's mid-air responses.

### Shadow of the Dancing Leaf
**Japanese:** Konoha Kage Buyō (木ノ葉舞う陰)
**Type:** Taijutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user positions themselves directly beneath an airborne opponent, matching their movement so precisely that they remain locked in the target's blind spot for the entire ascent. It inflicts nothing by itself — it is the positioning step that makes an aerial finisher possible, and the target cannot escape it while committed to the air.

### Human Bullet Tank
**Japanese:** Nikudan Sensha (肉弾戦車)
**Type:** Taijutsu — Akimichi clan
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user expands their body, tucks in their limbs, and rolls forward as an enormous spinning sphere of destructive mass. The momentum flattens obstacles, structures, and anyone who fails to clear the path. Its weakness is the same as its strength: once rolling, the user has limited control over direction.

### Spiked Human Bullet Tank
**Japanese:** Nikudan Hari Sensha (肉弾針戦車)
**Type:** Taijutsu — Akimichi clan
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user wraps kunai-studded rope around their enlarged body before rolling, so the spinning sphere now shreds as well as crushes. The protruding blades also grant traction, letting the user climb walls and change direction mid-roll — solving the base technique's steering problem. Among the highest single-impact destructive taijutsu at this tier.

### Leaf Great Whirlwind
**Japanese:** Konoha Daisenpū (木ノ葉大旋風)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** An escalating chain of spinning kicks delivered in sequence — low, mid, high, then a dropping heel — each faster than the last. The stacking rotation builds momentum so that the final kick lands with vastly more force than the first, and the shifting heights make a consistent guard impossible.

### Leaf Rising Wind
**Japanese:** Konoha Shōfū (木ノ葉昇風)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user drops into a crouch and springs upward with a straight vertical kick. The recoil from the impact launches the struck opponent into the air, making it both a strike in its own right and the opening move of an aerial combination.

### Drunken Fist
**Japanese:** Suiken (酔拳)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** An inebriated fighting style in which the user's stumbling, unbalanced movement conceals genuine attacks. The unpredictability is the weapon — because the user is not consciously planning their strikes, an opponent cannot read intent, telegraphing, or rhythm, and even skilled combatants struggle to anticipate what comes next.

### Heavenly Foot of Pain
**Japanese:** Tsūten Kyaku (通天脚)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user concentrates an enormous volume of chakra into the leg and delivers a downward axe kick or heel drop. The impact pulverizes stone, splits ground, and demolishes structures outright; against a person it is simply fatal. Requires the precise chakra control needed to release all that force at the instant of contact.

### Cherry Blossom Impact
**Japanese:** Ōkashō (桜花衝)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user gathers chakra into a fist and releases it at the exact moment of impact, producing concussive force far beyond what the strike's physical size suggests. The technique shatters ground and armor alike, and the timing of the chakra release — not muscular strength — determines its power.

## Clan & Hiden Techniques

### Mind Body Switch Technique
**Japanese:** Shintenshin no Jutsu (心転身の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu — Yamanaka clan
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user projects their consciousness out of their body and into a target's, seizing control of the target's body and speaking and acting through them. The user's own body collapses defenseless for the duration, and the projected spirit travels in a straight line at fixed speed, so a moving or forewarned target can simply step aside. Invaluable for interrogation, infiltration, and turning an enemy against their own allies.

### Fang Passing Fang
**Japanese:** Gatsūga (牙通牙)
**Type:** Taijutsu — Inuzuka clan
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The paired evolution of Passing Fang: the user and their ninken partner both spin into high-velocity drilling projectiles and tear into the target from two angles at once. The dual approach eliminates the single-user version's core weakness, since an opponent who dodges one drill moves directly into the path of the other.

### Hidden Shadow Snake Hands
**Japanese:** Sen'ei Jashu (潜影蛇手)
**Type:** Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** Snakes shoot out from inside the user's sleeve or wrist to strike, bind, or disarm a target from a distance. The launch is nearly instantaneous and comes from an unexpected line, and the snakes can constrict, envenom, or simply hold a target still for a follow-up.

### Shadow Imitation Technique
**Japanese:** Kagemane no Jutsu (影真似の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu — Nara clan
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user stretches their own shadow across the ground until it connects with the target's, at which point the target's body is locked into mirroring the user's every movement. Range and duration are bounded by available light and the user's chakra, and a target strong enough can strain against the hold. Its true value is that it is a total immobilization — a captured opponent can be walked into position for an ally to finish.

## Fūinjutsu & Barrier Techniques

### Five Seal Barrier
**Japanese:** Gofū Kekkai (五封結界)
**Type:** Barrier Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user places five seal tags around a location, generating a spatial barrier that cannot be broken by force. All five tags must be located and removed simultaneously to bring it down, meaning an intruding squad must split up to breach it — which is precisely the vulnerability the barrier is designed to create.

### Enlightened Fire Seal
**Japanese:** Shikoku Fūin
**Type:** Fūinjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user concentrates chakra to a fingertip finely enough to inscribe seal formulae directly onto skin, iron plates, or other surfaces without ink. Demands exceptionally delicate chakra control, since too much force damages the surface and too little fails to mark it at all.

## Puppet Techniques

### Puppet Technique
**Japanese:** Kugutsu no Jutsu (傀儡の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The foundational art of Sunagakure's puppeteers. The user extends threads of chakra from their fingertips to a puppet and manipulates it as an armed extension of themselves, fighting entirely at range while the puppet absorbs any damage. Mastery is measured by how many puppets can be controlled at once, since each additional body demands separate finger control and divided attention.

## Shurikenjutsu & Weapon Techniques

### Manipulating Windmill Triple Blades
**Japanese:** Sōfūshasan no Tachi (操風車三の太刀)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Shurikenjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user throws a large shuriken and kunai controlled by near-invisible strings, using the visible weapons as decoys while a third blade doubles back on a concealed line to strike from behind. The deception depends on the target reacting correctly to the obvious threats, and its execution demands the ability to read the enemy's movement precisely enough to time the reversal.

### Senbon Shower
**Japanese:** Joro Senbon (如雨露千本)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Shurikenjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** A barrage of chakra-guided senbon needles rains down on the target from spinning umbrellas suspended overhead. The needles arrive from a wide spread of angles at once, leaving no safe direction to dodge toward, and the user directs the volley from cover.

### Prepared Needle Shot
**Japanese:** Shikomishindan (仕込針弾)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Weapon Technique
**Rank:** C
**Description:** A concealed arm-mounted mechanism fires a spread of poisoned senbon at the target, with chakra threads adjusting their trajectories in flight. Because the launcher is hidden and requires no throwing motion, the attack gives almost no warning — and the poison means a single graze is enough.

### Manipulating Attack Blades
**Japanese:** Sōshūjin (操集刃)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Shurikenjutsu
**Rank:** C
**Description:** The user suspends a mass of kunai in a chakra field surrounding the target, then drives them inward from every direction simultaneously. Because the blades converge from a full sphere rather than a single line, there is no direction in which the target can retreat.
