# Naruto Jutsu Compendium — D-RANK

The genin working band. Everything a graduate is expected to actually fight with — movement, basic elemental work, the first real taijutsu forms, and the utility techniques that keep a squad alive.

> **Created by the architecture audit.** This file was referenced by `book-0-index.md` (`02_D-Rank`) and by `book-4-the-game.md` §1 (which names *"Decapitating Airwaves, Leaf Gale, Dynamic Entry"* as living here) but **did not exist**. Every entry below is either a technique already in play on a character sheet or one the Books explicitly point at. Costs and mechanics are **not** duplicated here — see Book IV §1 for the cost band and the character files for a given PC's version.

**D-rank cost band (Book IV §1):** 8–12 Chakra · 5–8 Stamina · **1 combo slot.**

## Movement & Body Techniques

### Body Flicker Technique
**Japanese:** Shunshin no Jutsu (瞬身の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Supplementary
**Rank:** D
**Description:** The user channels chakra to move at a speed that outpaces the eye, appearing to vanish and reappear elsewhere — often accompanied by a swirl of leaves, smoke, or water depending on the practitioner's affinity and vanity. It is a burst of genuine physical movement rather than teleportation, so it follows terrain, can be intercepted mid-path by a fast enough opponent, and cannot cross a barrier the user could not otherwise cross. The signature travel technique of the entire shinobi world; nearly every jōnin uses it constantly and few bother to announce it.

### Tree Climbing Practice
**Japanese:** Ki Nobori no Shugyō (木登りの修行)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Chakra Control
**Rank:** D
**Description:** Emitting a fixed, continuous quantity of chakra from the soles of the feet to adhere to vertical and inverted surfaces. Too little chakra and the user falls; too much and the surface splinters away. Taught as a control exercise rather than a combat technique, and its real function is diagnostic — a shinobi who cannot hold a tree cannot hold anything more demanding.

### Water Surface Walking
**Japanese:** Suimen Hokō no Gyō (水面歩行の行)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Chakra Control
**Rank:** D
**Description:** The advanced form of the above. Because water yields, the user must continuously modulate their chakra output rather than hold it steady, making this substantially harder than climbing. Standard prerequisite for any Water Release practitioner and for fighting anywhere near a coast, a river, or a rice field.

## Taijutsu

### Leaf Whirlwind
**Japanese:** Konoha Senpū (木ノ葉旋風)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** D
**Description:** Konoha's foundational kick combination — a low sweep aimed at the ankles that forces the target to hop or block downward, immediately followed by a high kick to the head that arrives while their guard is still in the wrong place. The technique is not the kicks; it is the ordering. Taught at the Academy and never outgrown, because the tempo trick works on opponents who know exactly what is coming.

### Leaf Gale
**Japanese:** Konoha Reppū (木ノ葉烈風)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** D
**Description:** A single low spinning kick delivered to the shins or ankles with the full rotation of the hips behind it, intended to take an opponent's legs out from under them rather than to injure. A setup technique in the truest sense — its value is entirely in what the user does to a target who is suddenly on the ground.

### Dynamic Entry
**Japanese:** Dainamikku Entorī (ダイナミック・エントリー)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** D
**Description:** A flying kick delivered to the face at the end of a full-speed approach, ideally from a direction the target was not watching. Crude, entirely without subtlety, and effective in direct proportion to how little the recipient expected it. Might Gai's calling card and, by inheritance, Rock Lee's.

### Leaf Great Whirlwind
**Japanese:** Konoha Daisenpū (木ノ葉大旋風)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** D
**Description:** An escalation of Leaf Whirlwind into a four-kick chain — low, mid, high, then a spinning heel — with the user rotating continuously so each strike carries the momentum of the last. Demanding on the legs and on balance; a missed link leaves the user turned away from a live opponent.

## Elemental & Nature Techniques

### Fire Release: Ember Technique
**Japanese:** Katon: Endan (火遁・炎弾)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** D
**Description:** A compact burst of flame spat at a target at short range. Not the great walls of fire the Uchiha are known for — this is the entry-level form every Fire-natured genin learns first, useful for lighting things, forcing a dodge, and proving to an instructor that the nature transformation has taken.

### Water Release: Wild Water Wave
**Japanese:** Suiton: Mizurappa (水遁・水乱波)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** D
**Description:** A pressurised jet of water expelled from the mouth, aimed to knock a target off their feet or wash away footing. Requires no external water source at this rank, though a nearby one makes it substantially cheaper. Kiri's standard opener.

### Earth Release: Hiding in Rock
**Japanese:** Doton: Iwa Gakure (土遁・岩隠れ)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** D
**Description:** The user merges with stone or packed earth, becoming effectively part of the terrain until they choose to emerge. Concealment rather than travel — movement while merged is slow and shallow. Defeated by chakra sensing and by anyone who thinks to attack the ground.

### Lightning Release: Static Touch
**Japanese:** Raiton: Seiden (雷遁・静電)
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** D
**Description:** A short discharge delivered through the hand or a held metal weapon, producing a brief muscular seizure rather than serious injury. Lightning's characteristic advantage in miniature: it does not need to do damage to end an exchange, because a body that will not obey its owner for a half-second is already losing.

### Decapitating Airwaves
**Japanese:** Kubikiri Kūchō (首斬り空跳)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Offensive — **not** a nature transformation **[see note]**
**Rank:** D
**Description:** The user drives a compressed blade of air outward along the line of a swung weapon, extending the cut well past the reach of the blade itself. Devastating against an opponent who has correctly measured the weapon's range and stepped to exactly the wrong distance. Named for what it does to an unarmoured neck at the outer edge of that range.

> **⚠ Placement note — this technique is why the file's Wind rule needs stating carefully.** Book IV §1 names Decapitating Airwaves as a *"genuine D-rank alternative"* living in this file, and it is commonly assumed to be Wind Release. **The databooks rank it D and list no nature.** It is therefore recorded here as **shape manipulation of ordinary air, not Wind Release chakra** — which is what makes it available to a genin of any affinity and what keeps it from contradicting the rule immediately below. A Wind-natured user gets nothing extra from it. **Do not treat this as a Wind Release entry.**

> **⚠ THERE IS NO WIND RELEASE TECHNIQUE AT E- OR D-RANK.**
> This is deliberate and load-bearing, not an omission. **Wind Release begins at C-rank** (`03-c-rank.md`), which requires Chūnin under Book IV §3 Part Five. It is the single largest constraint on any Wind-natured genin, and it is the documented reason `characters/ardo.md` had to invent his own D-rank Wind techniques rather than learn any. **Do not add a Wind Release entry to this file.**

## Concealment, Escape & Utility

### Haze Clone Technique
**Japanese:** Kasumi Bunshin no Jutsu (霞分身の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Clone Technique
**Rank:** D
**Description:** Creates several mist-like clones with no physical substance whatsoever. Attacks pass straight through them, which is the entire point — the technique is a repositioning tool disguised as a threat, and a competent user spends the confusion rather than the clones.
*Promoted from `jutsu-compendium/expansions/` by the systems check — it existed only in a research file.*


### Hiding in Mist Technique
**Japanese:** Kirigakure no Jutsu (霧隠れの術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Supplementary — Kirigakure hiden
**Rank:** D
**Description:** The user converts chakra into a dense, clinging fog that fills the surrounding area, defeating ordinary sight entirely. Kiri's silent-killing doctrine is built on top of it: the mist is not the weapon, it is the condition under which the actual weapon becomes unanswerable. Requires a water source or sufficient ambient moisture. Dōjutsu and chakra sensing see through it; ordinary eyes do not.

### Hydrification Technique
**Japanese:** Suika no Jutsu (水化の術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu — Hōzuki clan
**Rank:** D **[CONFLICT — see below]**
**Description:** The user liquefies part or all of their body, allowing physical attacks to pass through without effect. Blades, blunt force, and projectiles are all defeated; Lightning Release is not, and conducts through the liquefied body with substantially increased effect. Absorbing water restores the user's mass.

> **[CONFLICT — preserved, not resolved.]** This file ranks Hydrification as a **D-rank hiden technique**, which is how the Hōzuki treat it internally. **Book II §1** notes that by the compendium's own logic a genuine kekkei genkai carries **no rank at all**, which would move it to `07-outside-the-ranks.md`. Both readings are kept. Table ruling required if a Hōzuki PC appears.

### Genjutsu Release
**Japanese:** Genjutsu Kai (幻術解)
**Type:** Genjutsu / Supplementary
**Rank:** D **[GM — placement ruling]**
**Description:** The user halts their own chakra flow and then forcibly surges it, disrupting the foreign chakra that a genjutsu is using to hold their nervous system. Self-application is a contest against the caster; applying it to an afflicted ally is easier, and physical pain administered by a third party achieves the same result more crudely. The universal counter to illusion, and the reason a genjutsu specialist works hardest at *not being noticed casting.*

> **[RESOLVED — was a tracked gap.]** Book 0's conflict log recorded that **Genjutsu Release appears in none of the seven rank files**, which left the setting's single most important defensive technique with no home. **Ruling: it lives here, at D-rank**, available to anyone from graduation. This is a placement decision, not a canon claim — the databooks do not consistently rank it. Overturn it at session zero if the table prefers it unranked.

### Chakra Suppression Technique
**Japanese:** Chakra Yokusei (チャクラ抑制)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Supplementary
**Rank:** D
**Description:** Deliberately minimising one's own chakra signature to slip beneath a sensor's notice. Effective against sensing at range and largely useless at conversational distance against a good one. Standard ANBU and infiltration training; the reason a hidden squad can sit inside a patrol radius for a day.

### Rope Escape — Advanced Application
**Japanese:** Nawanuke no Jutsu, ōyō (縄抜けの術・応用)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / General Skill
**Rank:** D
**Description:** The escalation of the E-rank basic to chakra-reinforced wire, restraint cuffs, and knotwork designed by someone who expected the attempt. **Does not defeat chakra-based seals or fūinjutsu bindings** — those need a sealer.

## Fūinjutsu

> ### ⚠ These two were missing, and their absence was a functional hole.
> The compendium carries **eight Fūinjutsu entries across all ranks and had no technique for putting anything into a scroll or taking it back out.** Storage scrolls are standard equipment (Book II §4, 1,500 ryō) and nothing in the compendium operated one.

### Enclosing Technique
**Japanese:** Fūin no Jutsu (封印の術)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu
**Rank:** D
**Description:** Seals a wide variety of objects — and, with sufficient skill, living things — inside a scroll, to be released when the scroll is opened or a set condition is met. Other techniques can be stored the same way and let loose later. This is the technique behind every storage scroll a shinobi carries, and the reason a squad can walk into a village with nothing visible and unpack a siege ten seconds after they need one.
*(Some fan-RPG sources rank a scaled-up version at B; the base technique is D.)*
*Promoted from `jutsu-compendium/expansions/` by the systems check — it existed only in a research file.*

### Unsealing Technique
**Japanese:** Kaifūin no Jutsu (解封印の術)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu
**Rank:** D
**Description:** The release counterpart to the Enclosing Technique — draws a stored item or technique back out of a seal, most often in the middle of a fight, most often faster than the opponent expects. A weapon appearing in an empty hand is this, and so is a wall of water that was in someone's sleeve a half-second ago.
*Promoted from `jutsu-compendium/expansions/` by the systems check — it existed only in a research file.*
