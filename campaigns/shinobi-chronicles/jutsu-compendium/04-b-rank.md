# Naruto Jutsu Compendium — B-RANK

Elite jōnin-level techniques. The tier where large-scale elemental ninjutsu, sealing, barrier arts, and clan ultimate techniques come into their own.

---

## Ninjutsu & General Skills

### Poison Mist
**Japanese:** Dokugiri (毒霧)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user exhales a spreading cloud of corrosive poisonous gas that sickens, blinds, or incapacitates everyone caught inside it. Because it fills a volume rather than travelling along a line, there is no dodging it — only holding one's breath, fleeing the cloud, or dispersing it with wind. The user must be immune to their own toxin.

### Dead Soul Technique
**Japanese:** Shikon no Jutsu (死魂の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user restarts a corpse's heart and animates it with their own chakra, puppeteering the body as a convincing double. Surgical traces are erased so the corpse appears whole and alive, allowing the user to present a "living" person who is anything but — ideal for faking a survivor, a hostage, or the user's own death.

### Vanishing Facial Copy Technique
**Japanese:** Shōshagan no Jutsu (消写顔の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user peels the face from a victim and wears it as a flawless disguise. Unlike a chakra-maintained transformation there is nothing to dispel and no chakra signature to detect, making it effectively impossible to see through by conventional means. The victim is left a faceless corpse.

### Mandala Formation
**Japanese:** Mandara no Jin (曼荼羅の陣)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Offensive / Defensive)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user expels a writhing wall of countless snakes that either swarms forward to overwhelm a target or rises as a living shield to absorb an incoming attack. Sheer mass is the mechanism — an opponent must cut through an effectively endless volume of bodies to reach the user.

### Multiple Striking Snakes
**Japanese:** Sen'ei Tajashu (潜影多蛇手)
**Type:** Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The expanded form of Hidden Shadow Snake Hands, launching a great many snakes at once rather than a few. The swarm divides its labor — some snakes feint, some cut off escape routes, and others bind the target — so that a single technique performs threat, containment, and capture simultaneously.

### Paper Person of the Dance
**Japanese:** Shikigami no Mai (式神の舞)
**Type:** Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user converts their body into thousands of sheets of paper that can separate, drift, reassemble, and take flight. In this state physical attacks accomplish nothing, the user can scout or infiltrate through any gap, and reassembly can occur directly behind an opponent for an unanswerable surprise attack.

### Super Beast Imitating Drawing: God
**Japanese:** Chōshin Giga (超神偽画)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (ink)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user paints figures of gods and buddhas and brings them to life at full scale, sending them into close-quarters combat with strikes that shatter stone. The technique's power scales with the emotion the artist invests in the drawing, making it one of the rare ninjutsu whose strength depends on the user's state of mind.

### Dustless Bewildering Cover
**Japanese:** Mujin Meisai (無塵迷彩)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (optical)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user cloaks themselves in a film of fine water droplets that refracts light around the body, rendering them genuinely invisible rather than merely camouflaged. Since it manipulates light itself rather than matching a background, it holds up while the user is moving.

### Needle Jizō
**Japanese:** Hari Jizō (針地蔵)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (hair)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user grows and hardens their hair into a dense shell of steel-rigid needles enclosing the entire body. It functions as an omnidirectional guard that also punishes anyone who strikes it, since attacking the shell means driving a limb into a bed of spikes.

### Lion's Mane Technique
**Japanese:** Ranjishigami no Jutsu (乱獅子髪の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (hair)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user rapidly grows their hair to enormous length and controls it freely, hardening it to the strength of steel. The hair can be bundled into a battering ram, split into whipping strands, or used to bind a target — the greater the bundling, the more destructive force it carries.

## Clone & Transformation Techniques

### Shadow Clone Technique
**Japanese:** Kage Bunshin no Jutsu (影分身の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Clone Technique
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Creates fully corporeal, independently thinking clones by dividing the user's chakra evenly among them — one clone takes half, two take a third each, and so on. The clones can fight, use techniques, and act on their own judgment, and when dispersed they return everything they experienced to the original, making the technique a peerless tool for reconnaissance and accelerated training. The chakra cost is severe enough that it is classified as forbidden, since an inexperienced user can drain themselves to death.

### Combination Transformation
**Japanese:** Konbi Henge (コンビ変化)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Transformation
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Two people transform together into a single larger creature or object, coordinating movement and chakra as one entity. The difficulty lies entirely in the synchronization — the pair must act with a unity of intent that most partnerships never achieve.

### Human Beast Combination Transformation: Two-Headed Wolf
**Japanese:** Jinjū Konbi Henge: Sōtōrō (人獣混合変化・双頭狼)
**Type:** Ninjutsu — Inuzuka clan
**Rank:** B
**Description:** An Inuzuka and their ninken fuse into a single enormous two-headed wolf, combining their chakra and physical power into one body. The resulting form is dramatically faster and stronger than either half, and its two heads mean it cannot be flanked or blindsided.

## Fire Release

### Fire Release: Great Fire Annihilation
**Japanese:** Katon: Gōka Mekkyaku (火遁・豪火滅却)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user exhales a colossal, sustained wall of flame that sweeps across a wide front and scorches everything in its path to ash. Where the Great Fireball concentrates fire into a sphere, this spreads it across the battlefield, making it an area-denial technique as much as an attack. It combines devastatingly with Wind Release, which multiplies its scale.

### Fire Release: Great Dragon Fire Technique
**Japanese:** Katon: Gōryūka no Jutsu (火遁・豪龍火の術)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user expels one or more enormous dragon-shaped blasts of flame powerful enough to punch through structures. Beyond direct damage, the heat it dumps into the atmosphere generates violent updrafts and storm clouds — making it a deliberate setup for lightning techniques that need a charged sky.

### Fire Release: Fire Dragon Flame Bullet
**Japanese:** Katon: Karyū Endan (火遁・火龍炎弾)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user shapes exhaled flame into a dragon that splits mid-flight to strike forward, left, and right simultaneously. The three-way division covers every direction a target might dodge toward, converting a single attack into an inescapable spread.

### Fire Release: Great Flame Bullet
**Japanese:** Katon: Daiendan (火遁・大炎弾)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** A vastly amplified Flame Bullet — roughly ten times the size, speed, and heat of the standard version. The scale is sufficient to engulf a summoned creature whole, and the oil-fueled flame continues burning long after impact.

### Fire Release: Toad Oil Flame Bullet
**Japanese:** Katon: Gamayu Endan (火遁・ガマ油炎弾)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu (cooperation)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** A two-party technique in which a summoned toad sprays a torrent of flammable oil while its summoner ignites it in the air. The division of labor allows a volume of fuel no single user could produce, resulting in a firestorm on a scale ordinary fire ninjutsu cannot approach.

### Fire Release: Phoenix Sage Flower Nail Crimson
**Japanese:** Katon: Hōsenka Tsumabeni (火遁・鳳仙火の術 紅蓮)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** An enhanced Phoenix Sage Fire Technique in which each launched fireball conceals a chakra-augmented blade at its core. The target must contend with burning and cutting at once, and anyone who shields against the flames still takes steel through the guard.

### Ash Pile Burning
**Japanese:** Katon: Haisekishō (火遁・灰積焼)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user exhales a cloud of superheated chakra-infused ash that settles over a target, then ignites it with a spark struck from their own teeth. The delay is the weapon — the victim is already coated before detonation, and the resulting explosion originates on their body rather than in front of it. Effective use demands reading the wind.

### Searing Migraine
**Japanese:** Katon: Zukokku (火遁・頭黒)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** A small flame is flash-expanded by a burst of wind into a rolling wave of fire that washes over a wide area. The wind component gives it far greater reach and speed than the modest initial flame would suggest.

### Fire Release: Blast Wave Wild Dance
**Japanese:** Katon: Bakufū Ranbu (火遁・爆風乱舞)
**Type:** Fire Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user whips exhaled flame into a spiraling serpent of fire driven by a vortex of wind, which coils around the target and burns them within the funnel. The rotation prevents the fire from dissipating, sustaining lethal temperatures far longer than an ordinary flame technique.

## Water Release

### Water Release: Water Dragon Bullet Technique
**Japanese:** Suiton: Suiryūdan no Jutsu (水遁・水龍弾の術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Draws a great volume of water into the form of an enormous serpentine dragon that slams into the target with crushing force. Skilled users can generate the water from their own chakra rather than requiring a nearby source, though the technique is famously demanding in the number of hand seals it requires.

### Water Release: Water Formation Wall
**Japanese:** Suiton: Suijinheki (水遁・水陣壁)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user expels a circling wall of water around themselves, forming an omnidirectional barrier that blocks incoming attacks and drives back anything that strikes it. Because the water is in motion rather than static, it disperses force instead of absorbing it, and can be released outward as an attack.

### Thousand Flying Water Needles of Death
**Japanese:** Sensatsu Suishō (千殺水翔)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user draws surrounding water into precisely one thousand needles suspended in the air around the target, then drives them inward all at once. Because the needles converge from every direction simultaneously, there is no gap to move through, and the sheer count means blocking a portion accomplishes nothing.

### Water Release: Water Fang Bullet
**Japanese:** Suiton: Suigadan (水遁・水牙弾)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Compressed, rapidly spinning columns of water erupt from below and drill into the target. Because the attack originates underwater or underfoot rather than from the user's position, it strikes from a blind angle the target cannot easily guard.

### Water Release: Water Shark Bullet Technique
**Japanese:** Suiton: Suikōdan no Jutsu (水遁・水鮫弾の術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Fires a large shark-shaped mass of water that tracks toward the target and hits with tremendous crushing momentum. The shark form is not decorative — it holds the water together over distance so the projectile does not disperse before impact.

### Water Release: Great Water Arm
**Japanese:** Suiton: Gōsuiwan no Jutsu (水遁・豪水腕の術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu — Hōzuki clan
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user redistributes the water making up their liquefied body into a single limb, swelling it to enormous size and strength. The resulting arm delivers blows on a scale no human frame could produce, at the cost of thinning the rest of the body.

### Water Release: Great Exploding Water Shockwave
**Japanese:** Suiton: Bakusui Shōha (水遁・爆水衝波)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user expels an ocean's worth of water from their mouth, flooding the entire battlefield and riding the resulting current. Its true purpose is terrain conversion — it transforms dry ground into an aquatic environment where the user's remaining water techniques operate at full strength and the enemy is at a permanent disadvantage.

### Water Release: Five Feeding Sharks
**Japanese:** Suiton: Goshokuzame (水遁・五食鮫)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user channels chakra from each of five fingers into surrounding water to form five sharks that circle the target and attack in sequence from different angles. The staggered assault means a target who repels one is immediately hit by the next, with no window to counter the user directly.

### Water Gun Technique
**Japanese:** Mizudeppō no Jutsu (水鉄砲の術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu — Hōzuki clan
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user fires a single droplet of water from a fingertip at supersonic speed, punching clean through a human body. The volume of water is trivial; the velocity is everything. Restricted to those who can convert their body to water, since the technique draws its ammunition from the user themselves.

## Earth Release

### Earth Release: Earth-Style Wall
**Japanese:** Doton: Doryūheki (土遁・土流壁)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user raises a thick wall of packed earth, often by spitting mud that hardens instantly, to intercept an incoming attack. Fast to deploy and easily scaled to the size of the threat, it is the most widely used defensive Earth Release technique.

### Earth Release: Earth Rampart
**Japanese:** Doton: Doryū Jōheki (土遁・土流城壁)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Raises a massive formation of stone shaped either as a broad plateau or a steep needle-like spire. The plateau form shelters an entire squad; the spire form lifts the user above the battlefield or impales what stands on the ground it erupts from.

### Earth Release: Split Earth
**Japanese:** Doton: Doryū Katsu (土遁・土流豁)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user tears open a deep fissure along a chosen line in the ground. Used offensively it swallows whatever stands above it; used strategically it severs an advancing force in two, isolating one half from the other.

### Earth Release: Light-Weight Rock Technique
**Japanese:** Doton: Karuomogan no Jutsu (土遁・軽重岩の術)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Drastically reduces the weight of an object or of the user's own body, to the point of enabling flight and sustained levitation. Beyond mobility it allows a user to shift masses that should be immovable. The paired counterpart to the Added-Weight Rock Technique.

### Earth Release: Added-Weight Rock Technique
**Japanese:** Doton: Kajūgan no Jutsu (土遁・加重岩の術)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Multiplies the weight of whatever the user touches, pinning an opponent to the ground under their own mass. Pushed far enough the accumulated weight crushes the target outright, and a struck object becomes a siege weapon by weight alone.

### Earth Release: Earth Spear
**Japanese:** Doton: Domu (土遁・土矛)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user hardens their own skin to the density of stone, reaching a defense that turns aside blades and blunt force alike while simultaneously adding crushing weight to their strikes. Its one significant vulnerability is Lightning Release, which cuts through the hardened earth as though it were not there.

### Earth Release: Rock Lodging Destruction
**Japanese:** Doton: Iwayado Kuzushi (土遁・岩宿崩し)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user identifies the load-bearing point of a cave or rock formation and destroys it, collapsing the entire structure on whoever is inside. An ambush technique that requires no direct engagement — the terrain does the killing.

### Earth Release: Earth Mausoleum Dumpling
**Japanese:** Doton: Doryō Dango (土遁・土陵団子)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user rips a colossal mass of earth from the ground, hoists it overhead, and hurls it at the target. Crude in concept and devastating in practice, since the projectile is large enough that dodging the impact does not save the surrounding area.

### Earth Release Barrier: Dome of Magnificent Nothingness
**Japanese:** Doton Kekkai: Dorō Dōmu (土遁結界・土牢堂夢)
**Type:** Earth Release / Barrier Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Encloses the target inside a self-repairing dome of earth that drains chakra from anyone trapped within. Because damage to the walls is continuously repaired using the stolen chakra, prisoners who try to smash their way out feed the prison holding them.

## Wind Release

### Great Sickle Weasel Technique
**Japanese:** Ō-Kamaitachi (大鎌鼬)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** A vastly amplified Sickle Weasel — an enormous cyclone of invisible wind blades that shreds terrain, strips cover, and levels structures across a wide radius. Its scale makes it as much an area-clearing technique as a strike against any single target.

### Wind Release: Vacuum Wave
**Japanese:** Fūton: Shinkūha (風遁・真空波)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user exhales a compressed blade of vacuum-sharp wind that slices cleanly through whatever it meets. Because the cutting edge is air rather than matter, it passes through gaps that would stop a physical weapon.

### Wind Release: Vacuum Serial Waves
**Japanese:** Fūton: Shinkū Renpa (風遁・真空連波)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** A rapid succession of vacuum blades fired one after another across a spread. Where the single Vacuum Wave can be sidestepped, the barrage covers the escape routes, leaving no clean angle to move through.

### Wind Release: Vacuum Sphere
**Japanese:** Fūton: Shinkūgyoku (風遁・真空玉)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user exhales a scattering of small, densely compressed spheres of vacuum wind that perforate everything in the impact zone. Functions like buckshot — individually modest, collectively impossible to fully evade.

### Wind Release: Vacuum Great Sphere
**Japanese:** Fūton: Shinkū Taigyoku (風遁・真空大玉)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** A single enormous sphere of concentrated vacuum wind fired as one devastating mass, trading the scattering pattern of the smaller version for overwhelming force delivered to one point.

### Wind Release: Pressure Damage
**Japanese:** Fūton: Atsugai (風遁・圧害)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Releases an immense wide-area gust that shreds everything in its path through sheer pressure. It scours the battlefield rather than striking a target, and its scale makes it effective against groups and structures alike.

### Typhoon Water Vortex Technique
**Japanese:** Gufūsuika no Jutsu (颶風水渦の術)
**Type:** Wind & Water Cooperation Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Two users combine a wind technique with a water technique to generate a towering vortex of spinning water. The rotation batters and drowns anything drawn into it, and the spray thrown off blinds the surrounding area, covering an allied approach.

## Genjutsu

### Demonic Illusion: Tree Binding Death
**Japanese:** Magen: Jubaku Satsu (魔幻・樹縛殺)
**Type:** Genjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The target is bound by an illusory tree that coils around them and pins them in place. Uniquely among restraint illusions the victim remains fully conscious and lucid, which makes it as useful for interrogation as for combat — and leaves them helplessly aware as the user closes in to finish them.

### Demonic Flute: Phantom Sound Chains
**Japanese:** Mateki: Mugen Onsa (魔笛・夢幻音鎖)
**Type:** Genjutsu (sound)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user plays a flute whose melody convinces victims that they are bound by chains while feeding them waking nightmares. Because the illusion is carried by sound it covers a wide area and requires no line of sight — the user need not know where their targets are, only that they can hear.

### Revolt of the Demon World
**Japanese:** Makyō no Ran (魔境の乱)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (sound-controlled)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Flute music summons and directs three materialized spirit creatures that pass through physical defenses to devour the target's stamina directly. Because the entities attack vitality rather than the body, ordinary blocking and armor provide no protection whatsoever.

## Taijutsu

### Front Lotus
**Japanese:** Omote Renge (表蓮華)
**Type:** Taijutsu (forbidden)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** After opening the first of the Eight Inner Gates, the user launches an opponent into the air, binds them in bandages, and drives them headfirst into the ground in a spinning suplex at terminal velocity. It is classified as forbidden because opening a gate removes the body's natural limiters, and the strain tears the user's own muscles in the process.

### Leaf Strong Whirlwind
**Japanese:** Konoha Gōriki Senpū (木ノ葉剛力旋風)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** An ultra-fast spinning back kick delivered with the full rotational force of the body behind it. Its power is such that it can hurl a full-grown opponent across a battlefield, and its speed makes it functionally unreadable to anyone who cannot track the turn.

### Uzumaki Naruto Combo
**Japanese:** Uzumaki Naruto Rendan (うずまきナルト連弾)
**Type:** Taijutsu / Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Shadow clones surround an opponent and knock them skyward, then converge on them in mid-air for a group assault capped by a driving heel drop that slams them into the ground. Since the airborne target cannot maneuver or retreat, every strike in the sequence lands.

### Flying Swallow
**Japanese:** Hien (飛燕)
**Type:** Taijutsu / Wind Release (chakra flow)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user channels wind-natured chakra along a bladed weapon, extending its cutting edge invisibly beyond the physical blade and multiplying its sharpness. Opponents misjudge the weapon's true reach and are cut from what appears to be a safe distance.

### Liger Bomb
**Japanese:** Raigā Bomu (ライガーボム)
**Type:** Taijutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user seizes an opponent, lifts them overhead, and drives them down with a slam violent enough to fracture the ground on impact. A pure display of overwhelming physical strength, with the crushing weight of the descent doing the damage rather than any technique.

## Clan & Hiden Techniques

### Mind Body Disturbance Technique
**Japanese:** Shinranshin no Jutsu (心乱身の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu — Yamanaka clan
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user pours chakra into a target's nervous system to seize control of their body while retaining their own consciousness and remaining fully mobile. Unlike the Mind Body Switch it leaves the user unexposed, and its ideal application is turning a captured enemy against their own allies.

### Fang Wolf Fang
**Japanese:** Garōga (牙狼牙)
**Type:** Taijutsu — Inuzuka clan
**Rank:** B
**Description:** An Inuzuka and their ninken fuse into a two-headed wolf and spin at extreme velocity, generating a tearing vortex that shreds anything drawn into it. The transformed pair tracks by a scent marker placed on the target beforehand, so the attack pursues relentlessly regardless of evasion.

### Butterfly Bullet Bombing
**Japanese:** Chōdan Bakugeki (蝶弾・爆撃)
**Type:** Taijutsu — Akimichi clan
**Rank:** B
**Description:** After consuming the clan's most dangerous pill, the user manifests wings of raw chakra and channels the entire converted output of their body into a single punch. The blow is annihilating, but the technique burns the user's own life force to fuel it, making it a last resort in the truest sense.

### Super Slap
**Japanese:** Chō Harite (超張り手)
**Type:** Taijutsu — Akimichi clan
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user enlarges their body, concentrates chakra into the palms, and brings them down in a sweeping slam. The impact caves in solid ground across a wide radius, making it as much an area attack as a strike against a single opponent.

## Medical Ninjutsu

### Delicate Illness Extraction
**Japanese:** Saikan Chūshutsu no Jutsu (細患抽出の術)
**Type:** Medical Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user reads disturbances in a patient's chakra to locate poison or hidden internal damage, then draws the contaminant out with a chakra scalpel while simultaneously repairing the tissue around it. ## Fūinjutsu

### Fire Sealing Method
**Japanese:** Fūka Hōin (封火法印)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user unfurls a scroll and seals an incoming fire technique into it, absorbing the flames entirely rather than blocking them. It is effective against fire of virtually any scale, and demands mastery of both sealing arts and ninjutsu to perform under combat conditions.

### Dark Sealing Method
**Japanese:** Fūkoku Hōin (封黒法印)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user binds a formation shut with blood-inscribed talismans placed at each corner. The resulting seal is invisible even to ocular techniques capable of perceiving chakra, making it impossible to identify — let alone dismantle — without knowing it is there.

### Iron Wall Sealing
**Japanese:** Fūin Teppeki (封印鉄壁)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu / Barrier
**Rank:** B
**Description:** A sealed gate barrier that can only be opened by users possessing a specific inherited chakra signature. Force accomplishes nothing against it; without the correct chakra there is no way through at all.

### Sealing Technique: Octopus Hold
**Japanese:** Fūinjutsu: Okutopasu Hōrudo
**Type:** Fūinjutsu (cooperation)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** A large-scale sealing technique in which clones envelop an entire enemy force in a spreading mass and seal them en masse. Built for battlefield scale rather than single targets, capable of taking hundreds of combatants out of a fight at once.

## Barrier Ninjutsu

### Barrier Method Formation
**Japanese:** Kekkai Hōjin (結界法陣)
**Type:** Barrier Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Four users position themselves at the corners of an area and raise an enormous barrier sealing it off from outside interference. Nothing enters or leaves while it stands, and it is designed to hold long enough for a decisive event to conclude uninterrupted inside.

### Barrier: Canopy Method Formation
**Japanese:** Kekkai: Tengai Hōjin (結界・天蓋法陣)
**Type:** Barrier / Sensory Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Creates a resizable dome-shaped barrier that registers any movement occurring within it. Rather than blocking intruders it detects them, giving the user complete awareness of everything moving inside the covered area.

### Toad Gourd Prison
**Japanese:** Kekkai: Gama Hyōrō (結界・蝦蟇瓢牢)
**Type:** Barrier Ninjutsu / Summoning

## Summoning & Space–Time Techniques

### Summoning: Food Cart Destroyer Technique
**Japanese:** Kuchiyose: Yatai Kuzushi no Jutsu (口寄せ・屋台崩しの術)
**Type:** Summoning
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user summons an enormous toad directly above a target so that it crushes them beneath its weight on landing. Crude in execution and effectively unblockable — the mass arriving from above is far beyond what any guard can absorb.

### Summoning: Earth Release: Tracking Fang Technique
**Japanese:** Kuchiyose: Doton: Tsuiga no Jutsu (口寄せ・土遁・追牙の術)
**Type:** Earth Release / Summoning
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user summons ninken that travel underground, following a target by scent, and erupt simultaneously to bite and pin them from every side. The target is immobilized without ever seeing the approach, since the entire pursuit occurs beneath the surface.

### Summoning: Rashōmon
**Japanese:** Kuchiyose: Rashōmon (口寄せ・羅生門)
**Type:** Summoning (Defensive)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Summons a colossal demon-faced gate of extraordinary durability to intercept an incoming attack. Multiple gates can be summoned at once or in series, each absorbing and weakening the force before it reaches the next.

### Summoning: Quick Beheading Dance
**Japanese:** Kuchiyose: Kirikiri Mai (口寄せ・キリキリ舞)
**Type:** Wind Release / Summoning
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user summons a scythe-wielding wind weasel that sweeps an entire area with a single stroke, flattening the terrain and everything standing on it. A finishing technique, used to end an engagement rather than continue it.

### Augmenting Summoning
**Japanese:** Zōfuku Kuchiyose no Jutsu (増幅口寄せの術)
**Type:** Summoning
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Summons a creature bound to a specific condition — most notably one that grows larger and stronger each time it is struck. Conventional attacks therefore make the problem worse, forcing an opponent to find a solution other than force.

### Toad Shop Technique
**Japanese:** Kuchiyose: Gamamise no Jutsu (口寄せ・ガマ店の術)
**Type:** Summoning
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user summons a toad disguised as an ordinary shop front to lure targets inside, whereupon the building reveals itself as the creature's stomach and traps them. A trap technique built entirely on the target's own curiosity.

### Summoning: Lightning Blade Creation
**Japanese:** Kuchiyose: Raikōkenka (口寄せ・雷光剣化)
**Type:** Bukijutsu / Summoning
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user stores weapons of any size within a scroll or their clothing and summons them instantly to hand mid-combat. It allows an arsenal to be carried invisibly and deployed without the delay of drawing.

### Flying Thunder God Slash
**Japanese:** Hiraishingiri (飛雷神斬り)
**Type:** Taijutsu / Space–Time
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user teleports directly into an opponent's blind spot and delivers a single fatal thrust in the same motion. Because the movement and the strike are simultaneous, the target has no interval in which to react to the arrival.

## Puppet Techniques

### Black Secret Technique: Machine One Shot
**Japanese:** Kurohigi: Kiki Ippatsu (黒秘技・機喜一発)
**Type:** Puppet Technique
**Rank:** B
**Description:** A two-puppet combination in which the first traps the target inside its hollow body while the second drives blades through the shell from outside. Once the target is sealed in the box there is no escape and no defense against the skewering that follows.

### Three Treasure Suction Crush
**Japanese:** Sanbō Kyūkai (三宝吸潰)
**Type:** Puppet Technique
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Three puppets take position in a triangle and generate a vortex between them that draws enemies toward the center, compresses them under mounting pressure, and then expels the remains. The pull means resisting requires overcoming the vortex before the crushing even begins.

### Chakra Shield
**Japanese:** Kikōjunpu (気孔循歩)
**Type:** Puppet Technique
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user channels chakra through an opened puppet arm to project a thin, near-invisible defensive barrier. Despite its slightness it turns aside attacks that would destroy a physical shield, and it can be angled instantly to meet a threat from any direction.

## Kenjutsu & Weapon Techniques

### Sword of Kusanagi: Longsword of the Sky
**Japanese:** Kusanagi no Tsurugi: Kū no Tachi (草薙の剣・空の太刀)
**Type:** Kenjutsu / Ninjutsu
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user summons the legendary Kusanagi blade and controls it remotely in mid-air, attacking from angles no hand-held sword could reach. The blade is sharp enough to wound even diamond-hard defenses, and the user never has to close to striking range.

### Sword of Kusanagi: Chidori Katana
**Japanese:** Kusanagi no Tsurugi: Chidori-gatana (草薙の剣・千鳥刀)
**Type:** Kenjutsu / Chakra Flow
**Rank:** B
**Description:** The user channels lightning chakra through the blade, sharpening it enough to cut through steel and adding a paralyzing shock to every cut. Even a shallow wound numbs the limb it lands on, disabling opponents who survive the strike itself.

### Uchiha Return
**Japanese:** Uchiwa Gaeshi (うちわ返し)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (deflection)
**Rank:** B
**Description:** Using a war fan, the user raises a barrier of chakra that catches an incoming attack and reflects it directly back at whoever launched it. Rather than absorbing force it redirects it, which means the stronger the attack, the more punishing the return.
