# Naruto Jutsu Compendium — OUTSIDE THE RANKS

Everything that falls outside the E–S system. Part One covers canon abilities that were never assigned a letter rank. Part Two covers material that isn't canon at all.

---
---

# PART ONE — UNRANKED CANON ABILITIES

These are real, canonical techniques that carry **no letter rank**. Most are classified by kekkei genkai, kekkei mōra, or dōjutsu instead, and their rank field is simply blank. They are not S-rank by default — they sit outside the scale entirely.

---

## Sharingan & Mangekyō Sharingan

### Sharingan
**Japanese:** Sharingan (写輪眼)
**Type:** Dōjutsu / Kekkei Genkai
**Description:** The Uchiha clan's inherited eye, granting the ability to perceive chakra, read and predict movement with near-precognitive accuracy, and copy nearly any technique it observes. It also enables the user to cast illusions through eye contact alone. Awakening and maturation are tied to emotional upheaval, with each additional tomoe marking a deepening of the eye's power.

### Mangekyō Sharingan
**Japanese:** Mangekyō Sharingan (万華鏡写輪眼)
**Type:** Dōjutsu / Kekkei Genkai
**Description:** An advanced evolution of the Sharingan, awakened through the trauma of losing someone deeply important to the user. It unlocks a unique set of techniques individual to each wielder, but every use degrades the user's eyesight until they eventually go blind.

### Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan
**Japanese:** Eien no Mangekyō Sharingan (永遠の万華鏡写輪眼)
**Type:** Dōjutsu / Kekkei Genkai
**Description:** Created by transplanting a close relative's Mangekyō eyes into a user who already possesses their own. The merged eyes restore lost vision permanently and remove the blindness penalty, allowing unlimited use of Mangekyō techniques.

### Amaterasu
**Japanese:** Amaterasu (天照)
**Type:** Mangekyō Sharingan / Fire
**Description:** Black flames ignite at the exact point the user's gaze focuses. The fire is said to burn at the heat of the sun, consumes anything it touches — including other flames — and continues burning for seven days and nights without fuel. It cannot be extinguished by conventional means, and only the caster can put it out.

### Blaze Release: Kagutsuchi
**Japanese:** Enton: Kagutsuchi (炎遁・加具土命)
**Type:** Mangekyō Sharingan / Blaze Release
**Description:** Applies shape manipulation to the black flames of Amaterasu, letting the user mold them into blades, arrows, lattices, or a controllable shield rather than a spreading fire. It converts an uncontrollable inferno into a directed weapon.

### Tsukuyomi
**Japanese:** Tsukuyomi (月読)
**Type:** Mangekyō Sharingan / Genjutsu
**Description:** Traps the victim in an illusory world where the caster controls time, space, and physical mass entirely. Days or years of torture can be inflicted in what is, outside the illusion, a fraction of a second. Victims typically emerge catatonic, and only another Sharingan or extraordinary mental fortitude can resist it.

### Susanoo
**Japanese:** Susanoo (須佐能乎)
**Type:** Mangekyō Sharingan
**Description:** Manifests a colossal spectral warrior of chakra around the user, growing from a skeletal frame through layers of musculature and armor to a complete titanic form. It serves as both an effectively impenetrable defense and an overwhelming offensive platform, wielding weapons drawn from the user's own techniques. Its use is physically ruinous, straining the wielder's body severely.

### Kamui
**Japanese:** Kamui (神威)
**Type:** Mangekyō Sharingan / Space–Time
**Description:** Transports whatever the user focuses on into a separate pocket dimension, or transports the user themselves. Its defensive application allows attacks to pass through the user harmlessly by phasing the targeted portion of their body out of reality; its offensive application removes an opponent from the battlefield entirely.

### Kotoamatsukami
**Japanese:** Kotoamatsukami (別天神)
**Type:** Mangekyō Sharingan / Genjutsu
**Description:** The most insidious illusion in existence — it alters a target's memories and convictions without them ever realizing they were placed under a genjutsu, so they act on manipulated beliefs they experience as entirely their own. It requires no eye contact and leaves no evidence, but can only be used once every several years.

### Izanagi
**Japanese:** Izanagi (伊邪那岐)
**Type:** Mangekyō Sharingan / Kinjutsu
**Description:** For a brief window the user blurs the line between reality and illusion, allowing them to reject any event that occurs — including their own death — and rewrite it as though it never happened. The cost is permanent: the eye used to cast it goes blind forever.

### Izanami
**Japanese:** Izanami (伊邪那美)
**Type:** Mangekyō Sharingan / Kinjutsu
**Description:** Locks the target into an infinitely repeating loop of a moment they themselves created, which only ends when they accept the truth of who they are and stop trying to change the outcome. Designed as a counter and a punishment for Izanagi's abuse, it likewise costs the caster the sight in the eye that used it.

### Yasaka Magatama
**Japanese:** Yasaka no Magatama (八坂ノ勾玉)
**Type:** Mangekyō Sharingan
**Description:** The user hurls linked comma-shaped projectiles of condensed Susanoo chakra at high speed. Because they are formed from Susanoo itself, they carry enough force to break through defenses that would stop ordinary ninjutsu.

### Demonic Illusion: Mirror Heaven and Earth Change
**Japanese:** Magen: Kyōten Chiten (魔幻・鏡天地転)
**Type:** Mangekyō Sharingan / Genjutsu
**Description:** Reflects an incoming genjutsu back at whoever cast it, turning an opponent's illusion into their own trap. It requires the user to recognize the illusion at the instant it takes hold.

## Byakugan & Gentle Fist

### Byakugan
**Japanese:** Byakugan (白眼)
**Type:** Dōjutsu / Kekkei Genkai
**Description:** The Hyūga clan's inherited eye, granting near-360-degree vision, penetrating sight through solid objects and over great distances, and the ability to perceive the chakra pathway system and its individual points directly. A single small blind spot behind the neck is its only structural weakness.

### Gentle Fist
**Japanese:** Jūken (柔拳)
**Type:** Taijutsu / Fighting Style — Hyūga clan
**Description:** A fighting style that uses Byakugan vision to strike the chakra pathway system rather than the body. Blows land as light taps externally while devastating the organs and chakra network beneath, and damage inflicted this way cannot be conditioned against or armored away.

### Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms
**Japanese:** Hakke Rokujūyon Shō (八卦六十四掌)
**Type:** Taijutsu — Hyūga clan
**Description:** The user strikes sixty-four of the target's chakra points in a rapidly accelerating sequence — two, four, eight, sixteen, thirty-two, sixty-four. Once complete, the target's chakra flow is sealed entirely and they can no longer mold any technique at all.

### Eight Trigrams Palms Revolving Heaven
**Japanese:** Hakkeshō Kaiten (八卦掌回天)
**Type:** Taijutsu — Hyūga clan
**Description:** The user releases chakra from every point on their body while spinning rapidly, generating a rotating dome that deflects attacks from all directions. It compensates for the Byakugan's single blind spot and is regarded as an absolute defense.

### Gentle Step Twin Lion Fists
**Japanese:** Jūho Sōshiken (柔歩双獅拳)
**Type:** Taijutsu — Hyūga clan
**Description:** Chakra is shaped around each hand into the head of a lion, which devours the chakra of anything it strikes. It transforms Gentle Fist from a system of precise taps into a technique that drains an opponent's reserves with every connection.

## Rinnegan & the Six Paths

### Rinnegan
**Japanese:** Rinnegan (輪廻眼)
**Type:** Dōjutsu / Kekkei Mōra
**Description:** The most exalted of the three great dōjutsu, granting mastery of all five basic natures, the ability to perceive and manipulate chakra at its most fundamental level, and access to the Six Paths techniques. Its wielders are historically regarded as gods.

### Almighty Push
**Japanese:** Shinra Tensei (神羅天征)
**Type:** Rinnegan / Deva Path
**Description:** Repels everything within a radius away from the user with overwhelming gravitational force. At small scale it deflects an attack; at full power it flattens an entire village in a single release. A fixed cooldown between uses is its only exploitable weakness.

### Universal Pull
**Japanese:** Banshō Ten'in (万象天引)
**Type:** Rinnegan / Deva Path
**Description:** The inverse of Almighty Push, dragging targets and objects toward the user. Typically used to yank an opponent out of position and into a waiting attack they cannot avoid.

### Planetary Devastation
**Japanese:** Chibaku Tensei (地爆天星)
**Type:** Rinnegan / Deva Path
**Description:** The user creates a small black sphere that generates immense gravitational attraction, tearing earth and rock upward to form an artificial moon around whatever is sealed at its core. Used as an inescapable prison for targets too powerful to kill.

### The Six Paths of Pain
**Japanese:** Rikudō no Pein (六道ノペイン)
**Type:** Rinnegan
**Description:** A network of six bodies controlled remotely through shared vision, each specializing in a distinct function — the Deva Path's gravity manipulation, the Asura Path's mechanized weaponry, the Human Path's soul reading and extraction, the Animal Path's endless summons, the Preta Path's absorption of any chakra-based attack, and the Naraka Path's interrogation and restoration of the other bodies. Together they form a single combatant with six simultaneous perspectives and no exploitable blind spot.

### Outer Path — Samsara of Heavenly Life
**Japanese:** Gedō: Rinne Tensei no Jutsu (外道・輪廻天生の術)
**Type:** Rinnegan / Outer Path
**Description:** Returns life to everyone the user has killed, resurrecting the dead fully restored rather than as bound corpses. Unlike other reanimation techniques there is no puppetry and no cost to the revived — the entire price is paid by the caster, who dies performing it.

### Limbo: Border Jail
**Japanese:** Rinbo: Hengoku (輪墓・辺獄)
**Type:** Rinnegan
**Description:** The user creates invisible shadow-selves existing in a plane adjacent to the physical world. They can interfere with the real world while remaining completely undetectable to anyone without a Rinnegan or comparable perception, making them effectively unanswerable.

### Truth-Seeking Ball
**Japanese:** Gudōdama (求道玉)
**Type:** Six Paths Ninjutsu
**Description:** Spheres of black chakra composed of all five natures plus Yin and Yang, which obliterate anything they touch on contact. They can be shaped into weapons or armor at will, and only senjutsu-infused chakra can resist their erasing effect.

### Six Paths Sage Mode
**Japanese:** Rikudō Sennin Mōdo (六道仙人モード)
**Type:** Senjutsu
**Description:** A transcendent state granting vastly amplified physical ability, the perception to track opponents moving faster than the eye can follow, and the capacity to sense and interact with chakra on a level ordinary sensory technique cannot reach.

## Kekkei Mōra & Ōtsutsuki Abilities

### Infinite Tsukuyomi
**Japanese:** Mugen Tsukuyomi (無限月読)
**Type:** Kekkei Mōra / Genjutsu
**Description:** Cast by reflecting light from a Rinnegan off the moon's surface, this illusion captures every living being on the planet simultaneously, binding them into dream worlds of their own ideal lives while their bodies are drained as a resource. It is the single largest-scale technique in existence.

### God: Nativity of a World of Trees
**Japanese:** Shinsū Senju (神・樹界降誕)
**Type:** Kekkei Mōra / Wood Release
**Description:** Generates an enormous divine tree that spreads across the landscape, draining the chakra of everyone it reaches to feed itself. Beyond its scale as an attack it functions as an instrument of mass harvest.

### All-Killing Ash Bones
**Japanese:** Yubi Tobasu no Jutsu / Kyōsatsu no Haikotsu (共殺の灰骨)
**Type:** Kekkei Mōra
**Description:** The user fires bones from their fingertips that reduce anything struck to ash on contact. There is no defense that reliably stops them and no recovery for anyone hit — a graze is fatal.

### Eighty Gods Vacuum Attack
**Japanese:** Yasogami Kūdan (八十神空撃)
**Type:** Kekkei Mōra
**Description:** The user hurls a massive volley of truth-seeking spheres in every direction at once, saturating the surrounding space with projectiles that erase what they touch.

### Yomotsu Hirasaka
**Japanese:** Yomotsu Hirasaka (黄泉比良坂)
**Type:** Kekkei Mōra / Space–Time
**Description:** Opens portals allowing instant travel between separate dimensions. It grants the ability to withdraw from any engagement at will and to relocate an entire battle to a world of the user's choosing.

## Kekkei Genkai Nature Releases

### Wood Release
**Japanese:** Mokuton (木遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai (Earth + Water)
**Description:** Generates and controls living wood from nothing, growing forests, structures, restraints, and clones at will. It is uniquely capable of suppressing tailed beast chakra, which made its original wielder the only shinobi able to subdue the biju directly.

### Ice Release
**Japanese:** Hyōton (氷遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai (Wind + Water)
**Description:** Creates and manipulates ice, most famously as arrays of floating mirrors that allow the user to travel between them at speeds the eye cannot follow, attacking from every reflection simultaneously.

### Lava Release
**Japanese:** Yōton (溶遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai (Earth + Fire)
**Description:** Produces molten rock, corrosive quicklime, and rubber-like substances, used to burn through defenses, immobilize targets in hardening flows, or absorb impacts.

### Boil Release
**Japanese:** Futton (沸遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai (Water + Fire)
**Description:** Generates superheated vapor and highly corrosive mist that dissolves what it settles on. It can also be used internally to drastically increase the user's own speed and power.

### Storm Release
**Japanese:** Ranton (嵐遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai (Water + Lightning)
**Description:** Creates beams of electrified energy that can be guided in flight, curving to pursue a target and striking from angles that make evasion futile.

### Scorch Release
**Japanese:** Shakuton (灼遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai (Fire + Wind)
**Description:** Produces spheres of superheated air that desiccate a target completely, evaporating all moisture from the body and leaving a dried husk behind.

### Explosion Release
**Japanese:** Bakuton (爆遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai (Earth + Lightning)
**Description:** Imbues matter with explosive chakra, allowing the user to detonate objects, terrain, or applied material at a chosen moment.

### Magnet Release
**Japanese:** Jiton (磁遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai (Earth + Wind)
**Description:** Magnetizes and manipulates metals and metallic particles, most notably iron sand and gold dust, shaping them into weapons, restraints, and shifting defensive barriers.

### Dust Release
**Japanese:** Jinton (塵遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Tōta (Earth + Wind + Fire)
**Description:** Forms an expanding three-dimensional field that disintegrates everything inside it at the molecular level. As a combination of three natures rather than two, it is among the most destructive techniques any shinobi can perform.

### Crystal Release
**Japanese:** Shōton (晶遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai
**Description:** Crystallizes matter — including moisture in the air and living tissue — and shapes it into blades, barriers, and projectiles. Crystallized opponents can be shattered outright.

### Steel Release
**Japanese:** Kōton (鋼遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai
**Description:** Hardens the user's body to the density of steel, granting a defense that turns aside blades and blunt impact alike.

### Swift Release
**Japanese:** Jinton (迅遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai
**Description:** Grants movement so fast the user cannot be tracked by any conventional eye, allowing them to cross distances and land strikes before an opponent registers that they have moved.

### Dark Release
**Japanese:** Meiton (冥遁)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai
**Description:** Absorbs chakra through a marked palm and releases it back as a blue flame-like energy, converting an opponent's own techniques into fuel for a counterattack.

### Dead Bone Pulse
**Japanese:** Shikotsumyaku (屍骨脈)
**Type:** Kekkei Genkai — Kaguya clan
**Description:** Grants complete control over the user's own skeleton, allowing bones to be grown, hardened beyond steel, extruded through the skin as blades, and regenerated instantly. The user's entire body becomes an inexhaustible armory.

## Other Notable Unranked Abilities

### Sage Mode
**Japanese:** Sennin Mōdo (仙人モード)
**Type:** Senjutsu
**Description:** The user draws in natural energy from the surrounding world and balances it against their own physical and spiritual energy. It dramatically amplifies strength, speed, durability, and technique power, and grants a form of perception that senses everything in the surrounding area. Failure to balance the energy correctly petrifies the user.

### Chakra Enhanced Strength
**Japanese:** —
**Type:** Taijutsu / Chakra Control
**Description:** The user builds chakra at a specific point and releases it precisely at the instant of impact, producing force wildly disproportionate to their physical size. Mastery of it allows a person of ordinary build to shatter stone and level structures with a single strike.

### Multi-Size Technique
**Japanese:** Baika no Jutsu (倍化の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu — Akimichi clan
**Description:** Converts stored calories into chakra to enlarge any part of the body or the body as a whole. It is the foundation on which every Akimichi combat technique is built, with power scaling directly to the size achieved.

### Shadow Imitation Shuriken
**Japanese:** Kagemane Shuriken (影真似手裏剣)
**Type:** Ninjutsu — Nara clan
**Description:** The user's shadow launches shuriken from within itself, attacking from the ground where no guard is oriented.

### Shadow Sewing
**Japanese:** Kagenui no Jutsu (影縫いの術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu — Nara clan
**Description:** The shadow splits into needle-tipped tendrils that pin a target in place by impaling them, functioning as both restraint and attack simultaneously.

### Shadow Gathering
**Japanese:** Kageyose no Jutsu (影寄せの術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu — Nara clan
**Description:** Extends shadow tendrils to grasp and retrieve objects at a distance, letting the user disarm an opponent or seize a weapon without exposing themselves.

### Sand Coffin
**Japanese:** Sabaku Kyū (砂縛柩)
**Type:** Magnet Release Ninjutsu
**Description:** Encases a target completely in a mass of chakra-infused sand, immobilizing them entirely.

### Sand Burial
**Japanese:** Sabaku Sōsō (砂瀑送葬)
**Type:** Magnet Release Ninjutsu
**Description:** The follow-up to Sand Coffin — the encasing sand contracts with crushing pressure, killing whatever it holds instantly.

### Shield of Sand / Armour of Sand
**Japanese:** Suna no Tate / Suna no Yoroi (砂の盾 / 砂の鎧)
**Type:** Magnet Release Ninjutsu
**Description:** Chakra-controlled sand rises automatically to intercept incoming attacks without conscious command, and a secondary layer worn against the skin absorbs anything that penetrates the first.

### Explosive Clay
**Japanese:** Kibaku Nendo (起爆粘土)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Kekkei Genkai
**Description:** The user infuses clay with chakra through mouths in their palms, sculpting it into creatures that fly, crawl, and hunt before detonating. Its escalating grades run from small self-detonating figures up to city-destroying blasts.

### Earth Grudge Fear
**Japanese:** Jiongu (地怨虞)
**Type:** Kinjutsu
**Description:** The user's body is held together by thousands of black threads, allowing them to stitch stolen hearts into themselves. Each additional heart provides a separate life and a separate elemental nature, and the threads can be used to repair injuries or extend limbs across a battlefield.

### Curse Technique: Death Controlling Possessed Blood
**Japanese:** Jujutsu: Shiji Hyōketsu (呪術・死司憑血)
**Type:** Kinjutsu
**Description:** After ingesting a target's blood and standing within a drawn ritual circle, the user links their body to the victim's. Any wound the user inflicts on themselves is transferred to the target, letting them kill from a distance while suffering the same injuries without dying from them.

# PART TWO — NON-CANONICAL MATERIAL

---

## Anime-Only Techniques

Filler arcs introduced a substantial number of techniques never present in the manga. Where these appear with a letter rank, that rank was assigned by wiki editors rather than by any databook — no official source ranks them. Several Lightning Release entries in the C-rank file (Dance of 100 Volts, Electromagnetic Murder, Spider Web) originate this way.

**Status:** treat any anime-only rank as an editorial estimate, not an official classification.

---

## Movie-Only Techniques

The feature films introduced their own techniques, villains, and abilities, none of which appear in the databooks. Where the wikis list them with ranks, those ranks are again editorial. Movie continuity also frequently contradicts the manga outright, so movie-only techniques are excluded from the ranked files entirely.

---

## Video Game Techniques

Official Naruto games **do not use the E–S technique ranking system at all.**

- **Ultimate Ninja and Ultimate Ninja Storm series** categorize moves as Jutsu, Ultimate Jutsu, and Awakening. The S/A/B/C/D grades that appear in these games rate *battle and mission performance*, not the techniques themselves — a common source of confusion.
- **Naruto Online, Shinobi Striker, Gekitō Ninja Taisen, Path of the Ninja, and mobile titles** use skill slots, cooldowns, and gacha rarity tiers such as N/R/SR/SSR. None of these map onto the canonical ranks.
- Games also introduce entirely original techniques for playable characters, which have no canonical existence whatsoever.

**Status:** no video game technique carries a canonical rank, because the games do not assign them.

---

## Fan & Tabletop RPG Rank Systems

Fan-built game systems assign their own D–S ranks for balance purposes. These ranks are invented by the system's designers and **vary between systems for the same technique** — the same jutsu is frequently C-rank in one ruleset and B-rank in another.

- **Naruto 5e** ties ranks to chakra-point costs and character level, and scales a technique's effect based on the rank it is cast at — a design mechanic with no canonical parallel.
- **Forum d20 systems** (Shinobi Fables, The Naruto Way, NinRp and similar) attach stat blocks covering cost, upkeep, speed, and power to each technique alongside a self-assigned rank.

### Representative fan-created techniques
These do not exist in canon in any form:

**Stun Technique** (*Mahi no Jutsu*) — a fan offshoot of the Temporary Paralysis Technique targeting the mind rather than the body.
**Fire Style Seal / Water Style Seal** — fan-designed sealing tags that launch fireballs or flood an area.
**Fire Style: Flamestrike / Pyroblast Jutsu** — fan-designed stronger variants of the Fireball Technique.

**Status:** fan-assigned ranks are non-canonical and system-specific.

---

## Trading Card Game Ranks

The *Naruto Kayō* card game assigns its own S/A/B/C ranks to cards. These are balance decisions for the card game and do not reflect databook classifications — notably, several abilities that carry **no** canonical rank are given letter ranks on their cards, including Kotoamatsukami and Izanami as S, and Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms and Revolving Heaven as B.

**Status:** card ranks are a separate system and do not override or supply canonical ranks.
