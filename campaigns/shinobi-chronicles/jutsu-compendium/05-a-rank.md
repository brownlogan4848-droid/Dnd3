# Naruto Jutsu Compendium — A-RANK

Kage and elite-jōnin territory. Signature techniques, forbidden arts, and the medical and sealing disciplines at their highest practical level.

---

## Ninjutsu & General Skills

### Rasengan
**Japanese:** Rasengan (螺旋丸)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Offensive)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** A sphere of chakra compressed and set spinning in every direction simultaneously, held in the palm and driven into a target where it grinds and blasts outward. It requires no hand seals, making it impossible to anticipate from movement alone, and its self-sustaining rotation means the user need only maintain contact. Developed over three years by studying the Tailed Beast Ball, it represents the pinnacle of pure shape transformation without any nature added to it.

### Big Ball Rasengan
**Japanese:** Ōdama Rasengan (大玉螺旋丸)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Offensive)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** A Rasengan scaled up to several times normal size, typically formed with clone assistance since one pair of hands cannot compress that volume alone. The larger sphere carries proportionally greater destructive force and a much wider point of contact, at a steep increase in chakra cost.

### Hiding with Camouflage Technique
**Japanese:** Meisaigakure no Jutsu (迷彩隠れの術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user bends light around their body to achieve near-total invisibility while moving freely. Unlike lower-tier concealment it does not rely on backgrounds, stillness, or cover — only ocular techniques capable of perceiving chakra directly can detect the user at all.

### Telescope Technique
**Japanese:** Tōmegane no Jutsu (遠眼鏡の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Using a crystal ball as a focus, the user observes any individual anywhere, provided they already know that person's chakra signature. Range is effectively unlimited, making it the premier surveillance technique — though it cannot see through barrier ninjutsu designed to block it.

### Dance of the Crescent Moon
**Japanese:** Mikazuki no Mai (三日月の舞)
**Type:** Kenjutsu / Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user splits into three bodies that assault a target from three separate blind spots at once. Because all three are real and all three attack simultaneously, defending against one guarantees exposure to the other two.

### Brainwashing Technique
**Japanese:** Sennō Sōsa no Jutsu (洗脳操作の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Supplementary)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user inserts a specially prepared needle into a target's brain, suppressing selected memories and installing directives the target is unaware of. The victim continues to behave normally and believes their choices are their own, making this the ideal method for creating a long-term sleeper agent inside an enemy village.

### Mirrored Doppelgänger
**Japanese:** Kyōmen Shūsha no Jutsu (鏡面写者の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Triggered when a seal barrier is dismantled, this technique produces exact duplicates of whoever removed the seal — identical in appearance, technique, and skill level. The intruder is forced to fight themselves, though the copies possess limited stamina and degrade over a prolonged engagement.

## Clone Techniques

### Multiple Shadow Clone Technique
**Japanese:** Tajū Kage Bunshin no Jutsu (多重影分身の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Clone Technique (forbidden)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Creates hundreds or even thousands of fully corporeal shadow clones at once, each capable of independent thought and combat. It is classified forbidden because the chakra is divided among every clone produced, meaning an ordinary user who attempts it distributes themselves to the point of death. Only those with vastly abnormal chakra reserves can survive its use, and it converts one shinobi into an army.

### Clone Body Blow
**Japanese:** Bunshin Tai Atari (分身体当たり)
**Type:** Taijutsu / Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user and their clones use their numbers to scatter the opponent's attention, then converge into a single ranged aerial body slam. The confusion phase is what makes the strike land — the target cannot identify which body is committing until impact.

### Clone Great Explosion
**Japanese:** Bunshin Daibakuha (分身大爆破)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Offensive)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user sends a shadow clone forward as bait and detonates it once the enemy commits to attacking it. Since the clone is indistinguishable from the original, the opponent's correct instinct — to strike the exposed target — is exactly what triggers the blast at point-blank range.

## Transformation Variants

### Harem Technique
**Japanese:** Hāremu no Jutsu (ハーレムの術)
**Type:** Transformation Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The Sexy Technique combined with the Multiple Shadow Clone Technique, producing dozens of transformed figures that surround and overwhelm a susceptible target's composure entirely. The escalation from a single figure to a crowd is the whole mechanism — where one can be resisted, a swarm generally cannot.

## Water Release

### Water Release: Great Waterfall Technique
**Japanese:** Suiton: Daibakufu no Jutsu (水遁・大瀑布の術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user raises an enormous body of water tens of meters into the air and drops it as a colossal waterfall, hollowing out the ground beneath and sweeping away everything caught underneath. The chakra cost is heavy enough that it is a committed strike rather than an opening move.

### Water Release: Great Shark Bullet Technique
**Japanese:** Suiton: Daikōdan no Jutsu (水遁・大鮫弾の術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Fires an immense shark-shaped mass of chakra-laden water that absorbs the chakra of any opposing water technique it meets, growing stronger as it advances. Countering it with water therefore feeds it — the correct response is to avoid it entirely or answer with a different nature.

## Earth Release

### Earth Release: Swamp of the Underworld
**Japanese:** Doton: Yomi Numa (土遁・黄泉沼)
**Type:** Earth Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Converts the ground across a wide area into a bottomless, adhesive swamp that swallows whatever stands on it. Its depth and area scale with the chakra invested, and at full strength it is capable of engulfing summoned creatures the size of buildings. Nothing that sinks into it climbs back out.

## Wind Release

### Blade of Wind
**Japanese:** Kaze no Yaiba (風の刃)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user materializes an invisible longsword of wind extending from their fingertips. Because there is nothing to see, the weapon's reach and angle cannot be judged, and its edge cannot be blocked by an ordinary blade — the sword simply passes through whatever is put in its way.

## Lightning Release

### Chidori
**Japanese:** Chidori (千鳥)
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user concentrates lightning chakra into one hand until it screams like a flock of birds, then charges forward and drives it through the target in a piercing thrust. The concentration required for the strike creates tunnel vision so severe that the user cannot see a counterattack coming — which is why its creator considered it incomplete without the Sharingan to compensate.

### Chidori Sharp Spear
**Japanese:** Chidori Eisō (千鳥鋭槍)
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user extends the Chidori into a long lightning spear through shape manipulation, allowing the technique to strike at mid-range without closing distance. It sacrifices raw concentrated power for reach, and can be lengthened, whipped, or split as the situation demands.

### Chidori Current
**Japanese:** Chidori Nagashi (千鳥流し)
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user discharges Chidori outward in every direction at once, striking and paralyzing everyone in the surrounding area. It doubles as a defensive measure, since anything that touches the user — including a weapon held by an attacker — conducts the current straight back.

### Chidori Senbon
**Japanese:** Chidori Senbon (千鳥千本)
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The Chidori is divided into a thousand fine lightning needles fired simultaneously at the target. Paired with the precision to aim at vital points, the barrage can incapacitate a target without killing them, making it the rare lightning technique suited to taking someone alive.

### Lightning Release: Shadow Clone Technique
**Japanese:** Raiton: Kage Bunshin no Jutsu (雷遁・影分身の術)
**Type:** Lightning Release / Clone Technique
**Rank:** A
**Description:** A shadow clone infused with lightning nature that releases a paralyzing electrical discharge at the moment it is destroyed. Because destroying a clone is the natural response to it, the technique punishes the correct reaction — the opponent stuns themselves by winning.

## Genjutsu

### Temple of Nirvana Technique
**Japanese:** Nehan Shōja no Jutsu (涅槃精舎の術)
**Type:** Genjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Illusory white feathers drift down over a wide area, and everyone who sees them falls into an irresistible sleep. Its value is scale — a single caster can incapacitate an entire crowd, battlefield, or village district without a fight. Only those aware enough to dispel it before succumbing can resist.

### Bringer-of-Darkness Technique
**Japanese:** Kokuangyō no Jutsu (黒暗行の術)
**Type:** Genjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Steals all light from the victim's perception, plunging them into absolute darkness while the world around them continues normally. The target is left blind in the middle of a fight they can still hear, and cannot orient toward an attacker they have no way to see.

## Taijutsu

### Morning Peacock
**Japanese:** Asakujaku (朝孔雀)
**Type:** Taijutsu (Eight Gates)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** With six of the Eight Inner Gates opened, the user punches so rapidly that air friction ignites their fists, and the accumulated trail of flame spreads behind the strikes in the shape of a peacock's tail. The barrage is delivered against an airborne target who has no ground to push off and no way to escape the sequence.

### Daytime Tiger
**Japanese:** Hirudora (昼虎)
**Type:** Taijutsu (Eight Gates, Seventh Gate)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user compresses air to an extreme degree between their fists and releases it as a tiger-shaped cannon blast of pure pressure. Because it uses no chakra whatsoever, it passes straight through chakra-absorbing defenses that would neutralize any ninjutsu, and the detonation levels the surrounding terrain.

### Evening Elephant
**Japanese:** Sekizō (夕象)
**Type:** Taijutsu (Eighth Gate)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** From the Eight Gates Released Formation, the user delivers a series of escalating air-cannon punches, each strike sending a compressed shockwave shaped like an elephant's foot crashing down on the target. Successive blows land harder than the last, and the technique's sheer force overwhelmed opponents thought untouchable.

### Eight Gates Released Formation
**Japanese:** Hachimon Tonkō no Jin (八門遁甲の陣)
**Type:** Taijutsu (Eight Gates)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user opens all eight of the body's chakra gates, removing every natural limiter the body imposes to protect itself. Strength, speed, and chakra output surge past Kage level entirely, the user's skin reddens and steams as their body burns itself up, and the cost is absolute — opening the eighth gate kills the user without exception.

### Acrobat
**Japanese:** Akurobatto (アクロバット)
**Type:** Kenjutsu / Taijutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** A seven-sword fighting style in which the user holds blades in unorthodox positions across the body and moves in a loose, dance-like flow. Because the swords come from angles no conventional swordsman would use and even the wielder does not plan the sequence, the style is effectively impossible to read or counter.

### Uzumaki Naruto Two Thousand Combo
**Japanese:** Uzumaki Naruto Nisen Rendan (うずまきナルト二千連弾)
**Type:** Taijutsu / Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** A mass of shadow clones launches the opponent skyward and delivers roughly two thousand consecutive strikes before the target reaches the ground. The airborne victim has no means of defense, guard, or escape for the entire duration of the barrage.

## Clan & Hiden Techniques

### Shadow Clutch Technique
**Japanese:** Kage Zukami no Jutsu (影掴みの術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu — Nara clan
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user shapes their shadow into a solid cable with the tensile strength of steel, capable of gripping, lifting, and hurling objects weighing hundreds of kilograms. It converts the clan's traditionally restraint-focused shadow techniques into a tool of direct physical force.

## Medical Ninjutsu

### Mystical Palm Technique
**Japanese:** Shōsen Jutsu (掌仙術)
**Type:** Medical Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user channels chakra into a wound to accelerate cell division and repair damaged tissue directly, healing injuries in moments that would otherwise take weeks. It demands precise chakra control and extensive anatomical knowledge, and the same principle applied offensively lets a medic disrupt an opponent's circulatory system from within.

### Yin Healing Wound Destruction
**Japanese:** In'yu Shōmetsu (陰癒傷滅)
**Type:** Medical Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user anticipates where an attack will land and pre-emptively floods that area with healing chakra, so the tissue regenerates as fast as it is damaged. The result is an opponent landing clean hits that produce no lasting injury — the wound closes as it opens.

### Healing Resuscitation Regeneration Technique
**Japanese:** Chikatsu Saisei no Jutsu (治活再生の術)
**Type:** Medical Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** A team of medical-nin regenerates missing or destroyed portions of a body from a surviving cell sample. The procedure is slow, requires perfect coordination among the participants, and has a low success rate, but it can restore damage no other medical technique can address.

### Body Pathway Derangement
**Japanese:** Ranshinshō (乱神掌)
**Type:** Medical Ninjutsu (Offensive)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user injects electrical chakra into a target's nervous system, scrambling the signals between brain and body so that every command produces the wrong movement. The victim remains conscious and physically unharmed but cannot make their own limbs obey them.

### Katsuyu: Immense Network Healing
**Japanese:** Katsuyu: Mōryōjika
**Type:** Medical Ninjutsu / Summoning
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user summons a slug that divides into countless smaller pieces, each attaching to a different ally and channeling healing chakra to them simultaneously. It allows one medic to treat an entire army spread across a battlefield without moving from where they stand.

## Fūinjutsu

### Five Elements Seal
**Japanese:** Gogyō Fūin (五行封印)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** An odd-numbered seal applied over an existing even-numbered seal, thrown out of alignment so that it disrupts rather than reinforces. Applied to a jinchūriki it severs their access to the sealed beast's chakra and destabilizes their own chakra flow, leaving them unable to mold techniques properly.

### Five Elements Unseal
**Japanese:** Gogyō Kaiin (五行解印)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Removes a Five Elements Seal with a single precise fingertip strike. Correcting a deliberately misaligned seal without damaging the seal beneath it requires a level of sealing mastery few shinobi ever attain.

### Evil Sealing Method
**Japanese:** Fūja Hōin (封邪法印)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Using the user's own blood as ink, this seal encircles and suppresses a cursed seal. It does not remove the curse — it contains it, and the containment holds only as long as the bearer's own willpower resists. A seal applied to someone who wants the power will fail.

### Four Red Yang Formation
**Japanese:** Shisekiyōjin (四赤陽陣)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu / Barrier
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Four elite shinobi take positions at the corners of an area and raise a red barrier of extraordinary durability. It is strong enough to withstand a direct Tailed Beast Ball, making it one of the few defenses capable of holding against a full-power biju attack.

### Mutually Multiplying Explosive Tags
**Japanese:** Gojō Kibakufuda (互乗起爆札)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu (Offensive)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** A single explosive tag that replicates itself repeatedly, multiplying into an ever-growing mass of tags before detonating all at once. The resulting blast is far beyond survivable — the technique is only practical for a user who cannot be killed by their own explosion.

### Sealing Technique: Lion Closing
**Japanese:** Fūinjutsu: Shishi Heikō
**Type:** Fūinjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user inscribes a seal that completely suppresses chakra within its radius. Anything relying on chakra to function — including puppets, animated weapons, and active techniques — simply stops working inside the sealed zone.

## Kinjutsu

### Twin Snakes Mutual Death Technique
**Japanese:** Sōja Sōsai no Jutsu (双蛇相殺の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Kinjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user grips an enemy's hand, forces their fingers into shared hand seals, and kills them both simultaneously. It is a suicide technique with no version in which the user survives — it exists solely for a shinobi willing to trade their own life to guarantee an enemy's death.

### Cursed Seal Technique
**Japanese:** Juinjutsu (呪印術)
**Type:** Juinjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** A mark applied to a subject's body that grants access to a foreign, corrupting source of chakra in exchange for gradual submission to its bearer's will. The application itself is lethal to most recipients — only a minority survive receiving it at all. Other applications of the same principle bind the marked to obedience or kill them if they betray their masters.

## Summoning & Space–Time Techniques

### Summoning: Toad Mouth Bind
**Japanese:** Kuchiyose: Gamaguchi Shibari (口寄せ・蝦蟇口縛り)
**Type:** Summoning / Space–Time Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user summons only the esophagus of an enormous toad, which spreads across the surrounding area and converts the entire location into a living digestive interior. Everyone inside is trapped in flesh that constricts and consumes them, with no exterior to escape toward.

### Summoning: Triple Rashōmon
**Japanese:** Kuchiyose: Sanjū Rashōmon (口寄せ・三重羅生門)
**Type:** Summoning (Defensive)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Three colossal demon gates are summoned in sequence, one behind another. An incoming attack is absorbed and weakened by the first, further diminished by the second, and dispersed entirely by the third — a layered defense capable of stopping attacks a single barrier could not.

### Flying Thunder God — Second Step
**Japanese:** Hiraishin: Ni no Dan (飛雷神・二の段)
**Type:** Space–Time Ninjutsu
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user throws a marked kunai past an opponent, then teleports to it, arriving behind them in the same instant the blade passes. The enemy's attention follows the thrown weapon while the actual attack materializes at their unguarded back.

### Flying Thunder God: Mutually Instantaneous Revolving Technique
**Japanese:** Hiraishin Goshun Mawashi no Jutsu
**Type:** Space–Time Ninjutsu (cooperation)
**Rank:** A
**Description:** Two users of the Flying Thunder God chain their teleportations together, appearing and disappearing in coordinated succession to strike a target from continuously shifting positions. The margin for error is severe — a mistimed jump puts one user directly in the path of the other's attack.

## Puppet Techniques

### Human Puppet Manipulation
**Japanese:** Sōen: Hitomi Gokū
**Type:** Puppet Technique
**Rank:** A
**Description:** The user attaches chakra strings to a living person's head, torso, and limbs and controls them as a puppet. The victim retains their own body and abilities but none of their agency, and the puppeteer can channel their own techniques through the captured body — turning a captured ally into a weapon against their own comrades.
