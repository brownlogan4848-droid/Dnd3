## TL;DR
- The authoritative source for canonical letter ranks is the four Japanese databooks — **Rin no Sho (DB1, ch. 1–119), Tō no Sho (DB2, ch. 120–244), Sha no Sho (DB3, 2008), and Jin no Sho (DB4, released 4 Nov 2014, ch. 525–700)**; the narutoversity.wordpress.com and MangaHelpers transcriptions confirmed exact ranks. Most "obvious" S-rank dōjutsu (Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi, Susanoo, Kamui, Kotoamatsukami, Izanagi, Izanami) and all Rinnegan Six-Paths abilities carry **NO databook letter rank** and belong in the Unranked appendix, not the S-rank list.
- Confirmed A-rank is a broad tier (~40+ databook entries) while confirmed S-rank is small (~19 entries); the biggest gaps in your inventory are **B-rank and C-rank**, where databooks 2 and 3 add dozens of missing entries (Kisame's, Jiraiya's, Chiyo's, Sasori's, Yamato's, Kimimaro's, and Sound Four techniques especially).
- Official video games do **NOT** use the E–S technique-rank system (they grade mission/battle *performance* S–D and use gacha rarity tiers); fan RPGs (Naruto 5e, forum d20 systems) assign their own D–S ranks that vary by system and are non-canonical.

## Key Findings

**Source hierarchy used:** (1) Databook Jutsu Files — Rin no Sho (DB1), Tō no Sho (DB2), Sha no Sho (DB3), Jin no Sho (DB4) — as transcribed at narutoversity.wordpress.com and in njt's DB3 translation on MangaHelpers; (2) Narutopedia infoboxes (naruto.fandom.com) as cross-check; (3) the NarutoHQ jutsu database. Where a databook assigns a rank the wiki infobox omits, the databook rank is treated as canonical and the discrepancy is flagged.

**Critical correction to common assumptions:** Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi, Susanoo, Kamui, Kotoamatsukami, Izanagi, Izanami, Chibaku Tensei, Shinra Tensei, and Banshō Ten'in were introduced/detailed in databooks 3–4, which classified them by Kekkei Genkai / Kekkei Mōra **without** a letter rank. DB3 explicitly lists Susanoo, Kamui, and Tensha Fūin: Amaterasu as "N/A" rank. They are therefore NOT canonically S-rank (or any letter) and appear in the Unranked appendix.

**Rank of the "big" techniques (verified):** Chidori = A; Rasengan = A; Big Ball Rasengan = A; Multiple Shadow Clone = A; Mystical Palm = A (DB1); Water Release: Great Waterfall = A; Great Shark Bullet = A. Raikiri = S; Wind Release: Rasenshuriken = S; Kirin = S; Tailed Beast Ball = S; Night Guy = S; Dead Demon Consuming Seal = S; Impure World Reincarnation = S.

# SECTION 7 — NON-CANONICAL RANK-ASSIGNED JUTSU
# ========================================

## Anime / Filler-only (Narutopedia lists with a rank)
- **Lightning Release: Dance of 100 Volts / Electromagnetic Murder / Spider Web** — anime-derived Lightning techniques in your C-rank list; these carry only anime/secondary ranks, not databook ranks. Label: **anime-only**.
- Numerous filler nature displays are catalogued on Narutopedia with inherited/estimated ranks and should be marked **anime-only**.

## Movie-only
- Movie techniques (e.g., from *Naruto Shippūden: The Will of Fire*, *Bonds*) appear on Narutopedia with ranks assigned by the wiki, not by databooks — label each with the film title. No databook basis.

## Video Games — NO E–S letter-rank system
Official Naruto games do **not** rank individual jutsu E–S. Instead:
- **Ultimate Ninja / Ultimate Ninja Storm series (CyberConnect2 / Bandai Namco):** techniques are "Jutsu," "Ultimate Jutsu," and "Awakening"; combat resources are chakra, Substitution, and Chakra Dash. Mission/battle **results** are graded S/A/B/C/D — this grades *performance*, not the jutsu.
- **Naruto Online, Shinobi Striker, Gekitō Ninja Taisen, Path of the Ninja, mobile titles:** use skill slots, cooldowns, and gacha rarity tiers (e.g., N/R/SR/SSR), not canonical jutsu ranks.

## Fan / Tabletop RPG systems (ranks are fan-assigned; vary by system)
- **Naruto 5e (Wiki / Miraheze / Wikidot):** uses ranks D, C, B, A, S mapped to Chakra-Point costs and character level (C-rank at level 1, B at 5, A at 10, any rank at 15). Example: it builds "Giant Rasengan" as an **A-rank with 8 effect slots** — a design choice, not canon. Earth Release: Earth Dragon Bullet is **C-rank** in Naruto 5e.
- **Forum d20 / RP databases (Shinobi Fables, TheNarutoWay, NinRp):** assign their own ranks with stat blocks (cost/upkeep/speed/power). Earth Release: Earth Dragon Bullet is variously **B-rank** (NinRp, TheNarutoWay) [Rpg-board](https://thenarutoway.rpg-board.net/t294-doton-doryudan-earth-release-earth-dragon-bullet-rank-b) or **C-rank** (Shinobi Fables); Lightning Release: False Darkness is **B-rank** in these systems (coincidentally matching the databook). These ranks **vary by system and are not canonical**.
- **Naruto Kayō trading-card game:** assigns S/A/B/C card ranks (e.g., Kotoamatsukami = S, Izanami = S, Eight Trigrams Sixty-Four Palms = B, Kaiten = B). Narutopedia sometimes cites these; they are **not databook ranks** and should be labelled card-game-only.

## Recommendations
1. **Build your per-rank files on the databook ranks in this compilation** as the canonical spine; use Narutopedia only to fill Boruto-era or post-DB4 techniques (Jin no Sho, released 4 Nov 2014 covering chapters 525–700, was the last databook).
2. **Do NOT assign the dōjutsu/Rinnegan/Kekkei Mōra abilities any letter** — file them in the Unranked appendix. If you want a rank for completeness, cite the *Naruto Kayō* card ranks separately and clearly labelled non-canon.
3. **Resolve the naming collisions** flagged above so each entry has the correct Japanese name and rank: Karyūdan (C, DB1) vs Karyū Endan (B, DB2); Suikōdan/Water Shark Bullet (B) vs Daikōdan/Great Shark Bullet (A); Oboro Bunshin (D) vs any "Kasumi Bunshin" in your list; Great Fire Annihilation is **B**, not A.
4. **Threshold to change a ranking:** override a databook rank only if a *later* databook re-lists the same technique with a different rank, or if a Japanese infobox scan explicitly shows a different letter. Fan-wiki prose without a scan is insufficient; card-game and RPG ranks never override.

## Caveats
- Databook 4 (Jin no Sho)'s "Mini-Jutsu Files" deliberately omit ranks for many late-series techniques (marked "? rank" by narutoversity); absence of a rank there is **not** evidence of a specific rank — several war-arc techniques (e.g., Six Paths abilities) are simply unranked.
- Several transcriptions used were translated from Portuguese/German intermediaries (narutoversity marks these with asterisks); the letter ranks are reliable but flavor text may vary slightly from the Japanese.
- Narutopedia infoboxes sometimes omit a rank the databook assigned (e.g., Mystical Palm = A in DB1 but blank on the current wiki). Trust the databook; the flagged discrepancies are noted inline.
- "Continuous Tailed Beast Balls" S-rank comes from the NarutoHQ database rather than a numbered databook page — treat as provisional.
- Boruto-era techniques (Scientific Ninja Tools, Kāma/Ōtsutsuki karma, etc.) are outside the four databooks and are not covered here.
