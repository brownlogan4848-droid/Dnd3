- The canonical **E-rank** category is essentially exhausted by your existing 7 entries; the highest-value expansion is in **D-rank** (7 new verified techniques) and especially the large **C-rank** and **B-rank** categories compiled below from Narutopedia and its derived databases (the NarutoHQ Jutsu Finder confirms it indexes **3,024** total jutsu across all ranks).

### D-RANK (gap-fill additions — NEW only)

### Haze Clone Technique
**Japanese:** 霞分身の術 (Kasumi Bunshin no Jutsu)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Clone Technique
**Rank:** D
**Description:** Creates several intangible mist-like clones of the user. Because the clones have no physical substance, attacks pass straight through them, [wordpress](https://narutoversity.wordpress.com/first-databook-jutsu-files/) making the technique useful for confusing an opponent and dodging while the real user repositions.

### Blinding Technique
**Japanese:** 目潰し (Metsubushi)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (supplementary)
**Rank:** D
**Description:** A simple sensory-disruption technique used to rob an opponent of their sight momentarily, creating an opening. Listed on Narutopedia's D-rank technique roster (mirrored by the Naruto: New Generation wiki). [Fandom](https://naruto-new-generation.fandom.com/wiki/D-rank_techniques)

### Enclosing Technique
**Japanese:** 封印の術 (Fūin no Jutsu)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu (sealing)
**Rank:** D
**Description:** Allows a wide variety of objects — and even living things — to be sealed inside a scroll and released when the scroll is opened or a set condition is met; other techniques can also be stored for later use. [Narutopedia](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Enclosing_Technique) It is the storage counterpart to the Unsealing Technique. [Narutopedia](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Talk:Enclosing_Technique) (Note: some fan-RPG sources rank a scaled-up version B-rank; Narutopedia lists the base technique as D.)

### Unsealing Technique
**Japanese:** 解封印の術 (Kaifūin no Jutsu)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu (sealing)
**Rank:** D
**Description:** The release counterpart to the Enclosing Technique — draws an item or technique back out of a storage seal/scroll, allowing e.g. a weapon to be summoned mid-battle. [Narutopedia](https://naruto.fandom.com/wiki/Talk:Enclosing_Technique)

### Hovering Hummingbird Technique
**Japanese:** ホバリング・ハミングバード (Hovering Hummingbird)
**Type:** Bukijutsu (nunchaku weapon technique)
**Rank:** D
**Description:** The user swings nunchaku at high speed in front of the body while moving, forming a defensive guard that deflects incoming attacks while keeping the target in view. [Fandom](https://naruto-new-generation.fandom.com/wiki/Hovering_Hummingbird_Technique)

### Hydrification Technique
**Japanese:** 水化の術 (Suika no Jutsu)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Hōzuki clan hiden) / Water
**Rank:** D
**Description:** Liquefies part or all of the user's body into water, letting physical strikes pass through harmlessly and enabling fluid, evasive movement. (Rank per Narutopedia's D-rank list; most associated with the Hōzuki clan.)

### Water Release: Submergence
**Japanese:** 水遁・水没 (Suiton: Suibotsu)
**Type:** Genjutsu-type water technique
**Rank:** D
**Description:** Afflicts a target in the water with the illusion/sensation of drowning, incapacitating them and leaving them open to attack; relatively easy to detect for a composed opponent. [Fandom](https://naruto-new-generation.fandom.com/wiki/Water_Release:_Submergence)

### NON-CANONICAL / VIDEO GAME / SPINOFF E–D RANK JUTSU

**Important context:** The E–S letter-rank system is a **databook** convention. Official console/handheld games (Naruto: Ultimate Ninja, Ultimate Ninja Storm 1–4, Naruto Online, Naruto to Boruto: Shinobi Striker) categorize moves as "Jutsu / Ultimate Jutsu / Awakening," **not** by E–D–C–B letters, and the *Naruto SD: Rock Lee & His Ninja Pals* gag spinoff does not assign combat ranks to its comedic techniques. As a result, genuine "non-canonical but rank-assigned" jutsu come almost entirely from **fan tabletop/RPG systems and fan wikis**. For example, the Naruto 5e Wiki assigns its own tiered ranks and even scales them — it ranks the Shadow Clone Technique "C-Rank" and notes "If this jutsu is cast at B-Rank or higher, increase the number of clones… by 2." ## Caveats
- The E–S letter ranks are a databook convention; a large share of named jutsu (dōjutsu, many kekkei genkai, most tailed-beast techniques, formation/fighting-style skills) carry **no** assigned rank and were correctly excluded.
- Some Japanese names above are the standard romanizations from the databases consulted; a few (e.g., certain Lightning Release C-rank entries) had no kanji surfaced in the sources and are marked with an em dash.
- Direct machine-fetching of naruto.fandom.com category pages was blocked during research, so the C/B lists were assembled from Narutopedia-derived mirrors (NarutoHQ, which indexes 3,024 jutsu; Naruto: New Generation wiki) plus databook transcriptions rather than a verbatim dump of Narutopedia's own category page. These sources agree with one another, but the compiled C/B lists represent the canonical high-usage core, not a guaranteed 100% enumeration of every entry in those large categories.
- The Non-Canonical section is intentionally short because official video games and the Naruto SD spinoff do not use the letter-rank system; the rank-bearing non-canon material that exists is fan-RPG/tabletop content (e.g., the Naruto 5e Wiki tiering system, which even scales a jutsu's effect by the rank it is cast at).
