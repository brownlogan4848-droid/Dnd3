# Naruto Jutsu Compendium — S-RANK

The highest ranked tier. Techniques that decide battles outright, break the natural order, or cost the user their life to perform.

---

## Ninjutsu & General Skills

### Eight Branches Technique
**Japanese:** Yamata no Jutsu (八岐の術)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Transformation)
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user transforms into a colossal white serpent bearing eight enormous heads and eight tails, each head large enough to swallow a building and capable of acting independently. The sheer scale of the form makes conventional combat meaningless against it, and the multiplicity of heads means destroying one accomplishes nothing. Considered the greatest technique in its user's arsenal.

## Wind Release

### Wind Release: Rasenshuriken
**Japanese:** Fūton: Rasenshuriken (風遁・螺旋手裏剣)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** Wind nature is added to the Rasengan, compressing thousands of microscopic wind blades into a spinning shuriken-shaped disc. On contact it expands into a sphere that shreds the target at the cellular level, severing chakra pathways so finely that the damage cannot be healed by any medical technique. Its original form inflicted the same irreparable damage on the user's own arm, making it functionally a self-crippling technique until shape control advanced enough to throw it.

### Big Ball Rasenshuriken
**Japanese:** Ōdama Rasenshuriken (大玉螺旋手裏剣)
**Type:** Wind Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** A vastly enlarged Rasenshuriken with a correspondingly wider blast radius, capable of engulfing an entire battlefield section rather than a single target. The scaling multiplies both the chakra demand and the destructive area, turning a precision kill into an area annihilation.

## Water Release

### Water Prison Shark Dance Technique
**Japanese:** Suirō Sameodori no Jutsu (水牢鮫踊りの術)
**Type:** Water Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user floods the battlefield and raises an enormous water prison in which only they can breathe, having merged with a chakra-devouring weapon to survive inside it. The trapped opponent drowns while their chakra is continuously drained away, and the user moves through the water like the shark the technique is named for.

## Lightning Release

### Lightning Cutter
**Japanese:** Raikiri (雷切)
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The perfected form of the Chidori, refined to the point that the lightning is dense enough to cut through a bolt of lightning itself — the feat from which it takes its name. It carries the same fatal flaw as its parent technique, the tunnel vision produced by the charge, which is why safe use effectively requires an ocular technique to watch for the counterattack.

### Kirin
**Japanese:** Kirin (麒麟)
**Type:** Lightning Release Ninjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user first heats the atmosphere with massive fire techniques to seed thunderclouds, then guides genuine natural lightning down from the storm in the shape of a beast. Because the lightning is real rather than chakra-generated, it arrives in a fraction of a second — far faster than any reaction, dodge, or defense — and delivers a level of destructive force no ninjutsu-produced lightning can approach. The preparation is the only vulnerability.

## Taijutsu

### Night Guy
**Japanese:** Yagai (夜ガイ)
**Type:** Taijutsu (Eighth Gate)
**Rank:** S
**Description:** Performable only with all Eight Gates open, this is the ultimate forbidden taijutsu. The user's chakra manifests as a burning red dragon coiling around their body, and they hurl themselves forward at a velocity that visibly distorts the space ahead of them, obliterating whatever the strike meets. Its power is such that even an opponent standing at the peak of the world feared for his life against it. The recoil shatters every bone in the striking leg and burns the user's life away entirely.

## Medical Ninjutsu

### Creation Rebirth
**Japanese:** Sōzō Saisei (創造再生)
**Type:** Medical Ninjutsu / Kinjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user forcibly accelerates the division of every cell in their body, regenerating any injury — including fatal ones — almost as fast as it is inflicted. It grants what amounts to temporary immortality in combat, but each use consumes a portion of the finite number of divisions the body's cells can undergo, permanently shortening the user's lifespan.

## Fūinjutsu

### Yin Seal: Release
**Japanese:** Infūin: Kai (陰封印・解)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** Over years the user stores surplus chakra into a seal on the forehead, then releases the entire accumulated reserve at once. The stored volume dwarfs what any body could hold naturally, and it exists specifically to fuel techniques whose chakra demands would otherwise be impossible to meet.

### Dead Demon Consuming Seal
**Japanese:** Shiki Fūjin (屍鬼封尽)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu / Kinjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user summons the Death God itself, which reaches through their body to seize a target's soul and drag it out. The sealed soul is imprisoned within the Death God's stomach for eternity, beyond any possibility of retrieval or resurrection. The price is fixed and non-negotiable: the user's own soul is taken as well.

### Sealing Technique: Nine Phantom Dragons
**Japanese:** Fūinjutsu: Genryū Kyū Fūjin (封印術・幻龍九封尽)
**Type:** Fūinjutsu / Summoning
**Rank:** S
**Description:** Up to nine users channel their chakra through a colossal statue, which extends spectral dragons to extract a tailed beast from its host and seal it away. The extraction takes several days of unbroken concentration, and the process invariably kills the jinchūriki it is performed on.

## Kinjutsu

### Living Corpse Reincarnation
**Japanese:** Fushi Tensei (不死転生)
**Type:** Ninjutsu / Kinjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user transfers their entire consciousness into another person's body, discarding their old form and taking full possession of the new one. It grants effective immortality through serial body theft, but the acquired body degrades within a few years and must be replaced, and a body with a strong enough will can resist the takeover.

### One's Own Life Reincarnation
**Japanese:** Kishō Tensei (機生転生)
**Type:** Medical Ninjutsu / Kinjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user transfers their own life force into another person to restore them to life. It works even on someone already dead, but the exchange is absolute — the recipient lives only because the user dies in their place. Forbidden precisely because there is no version of it the caster survives.

## Summoning & Space–Time Techniques

### Summoning: Impure World Reincarnation
**Japanese:** Kuchiyose: Edo Tensei (口寄せ・穢土転生)
**Type:** Summoning / Kinjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user calls a dead person's soul back from the afterlife and binds it into a living sacrifice, which reshapes itself into a perfect replica of the deceased. The resurrected retain all their abilities in life, cannot be permanently killed, and regenerate from any damage, while a control tag strips them of their own will. Forbidden as a fundamental violation of the natural order — it turns the graveyards of history into an inexhaustible army.

### Flying Thunder God Technique
**Japanese:** Hiraishin no Jutsu (飛雷神の術)
**Type:** Space–Time Ninjutsu
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user marks a location, object, or person with a special formula and can then teleport to that mark instantaneously from any distance. It operates on the same principle as summoning, except the user summons themselves. Once a battlefield has been seeded with marks, the user becomes effectively untouchable and can strike anywhere within it faster than perception allows — a capability that earned its most famous practitioner a village-wide flee-on-sight order.

## Puppet Techniques

### White Secret Technique: Chikamatsu Collection of Ten Puppets
**Japanese:** Shirohigi: Jikki Chikamatsu no Shū (白秘技・十機近松の集)
**Type:** Puppet Technique
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user controls ten master-crafted warrior puppets simultaneously, one assigned to each finger. Each puppet was built as a complete combatant in its own right, and directing all ten at once with independent tactics represents the absolute ceiling of conventional puppetry skill.

### Red Secret Technique: Performance of a Hundred Puppets
**Japanese:** Akahigi: Hyakki no Sōen (赤秘技・百機の操演)
**Type:** Puppet Technique
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user manipulates one hundred human puppets at once through sheer force of will, breaking past the ten-finger limitation that constrains every other puppeteer. The resulting force is an army answering to a single mind — a scale of control credited with destroying an entire nation.

## Tailed Beast Techniques

### Tailed Beast Ball
**Japanese:** Bijūdama (尾獣玉)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Offensive)
**Rank:** S
**Description:** A tailed beast gathers positive and negative chakra in a precise eight-to-two ratio, compressing the mixture into a dense black sphere and firing it. The detonation levels mountains and reshapes terrain permanently. Only tailed beasts and jinchūriki with sufficient control over their beast can form one, and there is no conventional defense — the technique must be intercepted, redirected, or avoided entirely.

### Continuous Tailed Beast Balls
**Japanese:** Renzoku Bijūdama (連続尾獣玉)
**Type:** Ninjutsu (Offensive)
**Rank:** S
**Description:** The user forms and fires Tailed Beast Balls in rapid succession rather than one at a time, saturating an area with repeated detonations. Since a single sphere is already capable of erasing a landscape, the sustained barrage is used to eliminate any possibility of survival across a wide front.
