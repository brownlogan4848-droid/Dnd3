# THE CELL, AND WHAT'S LEFT OF IT

## ROKKA UEHARA — "ROKU"
**Jōnin · ~47 · Cell 409's sensei of record · OFF THE FRONT PERMANENTLY since Year −6**

The one who stayed. Filed the hazard claim after Ground Seventeen without being asked, sat on it for two weeks while it tangled in a Root discrepancy, and handed Ardo the slip like it was nothing. **Physically pulled him out of a Root interrogation room.**

**Session Five ran through his own old ground** — second year out of the Academy, a different squad, the same stretch of the Grass front, where he found a squad presented exactly the way the Shepherd's scene was presented: gear laid out, no blood, no answer, ever. **He never spoke of it before that mission.** His Reaction missed saving Rai by inches.

**Went with the squad out of Konoha** as Hiruzen's third condition — *"He will not command you — he goes as your jōnin of record, not your leash."* Walked six months across a continent to find Ardo, and stayed a year in Ryūchi Cave.

**Year −6: gravely wounded. Suzume kept him alive six days in a cave on Ibuki and medical ninjutsu.** He survived. **He never returned to the front.**

> **He fought the desk and lost.** Whereabouts as of Year 0: Konoha, presumably, in a village that has just burned. **Unaddressed. Flagged.**

## RAI INUZUKA — **KIA, Session Five**
**Genin · 11 at death**

Loud, generous, spent his share of the hazard money immediately and had no regrets. **Left his ninken Kuro home on his own decision, the morning of departure** — *"Not risking it on ground I don't know, for a leg that's never getting field-rated anyway."*

**Died protecting Suzume, mid-genjutsu, unable to defend himself.** Roku's Reaction got a hand on the blade but not all the way.

**Last words, unfinished:** ***"tell Kuro—"***

**Kuro knew before anyone told him.** The Inuzuka took the body and held a pyre. **Tsume Inuzuka lit it herself.** Kuro howled and the clan's ninken answered him.

> **Ardo took Rai's headband and left his own on the pyre in exchange.** He has worn it for thirteen years. **It is also the cloth that covers his Mangekyō** — which means the discipline that saves his eyesight is a dead friend's headband. Nobody has commented on that.
>
> At the same ceremony Ardo declared himself **"Shadow Kage."** Tsume: *"Rai would've told you that title doesn't exist. Then he would've told everyone he knew you first."*

## SOUTA — ⚠ **FELL OFF THE RECORD**
**War orphan · no headband · no claimed village · would be ~20 now**

Found stealing food outside Konoha on what began as a routine D-rank at Session Four. **Talked down rather than fought.** Ardo vouched for him personally at the gate and through intake — two logged rule violations along the way — **and offered him a home the same night.** Provisional civilian status, weekly check-ins. **Ardo gave him his money before leaving on the mission that doubled as his exam.**

His true origin, his sister's exact fate, and whether his path overlaps any existing thread were all **deliberately undecided — an open character, not a mystery box.**

> ## ⚠ [DECISION NEEDED]
> **He appears at Session Four and is never mentioned again** — across thirteen years, an eight-year timeskip, the squad leaving Konoha, and a village burning down. **A boy Ardo took in and then left.**
>
> **The audit will not invent an answer.** But he is roughly 20 now, Konoha is on fire, and *"the boy Ardo rescued and then abandoned"* is a loaded gun sitting on the table.

# ANTAGONISTS & UNRESOLVED FIGURES

## THE MASKED MAN — **ACTIVE, Year 0**
**Unnamed · orange spiral mask, off-centre, one eyehole on the right side**

**Tore the Nine-Tails out of Kushina Uzumaki** and released it on Konoha.

**Seen once afterward:** standing at the treeline past the fence of the compound where Naruto was being held, roughly twenty minutes before Ardo and Suzume arrived. **Did not approach. Did not try anything.** Gone before anyone could scramble.

> *"Didn't move the whole time he was out there. Didn't need to. **You could feel him not moving**, if that makes any sense."* — the chūnin on the door

**Left no trail and no chakra signature** — the deliberate absence of a man who wanted to be seen exactly once and not found afterward.

**九喇嘛 Kurama identified him on sight**, through Ardo: *"That's the one who took me out of her. I'd know that chakra if it was the last thing I ever felt."*

> **He knows Naruto is not a jinchūriki and left anyway.** Either he cannot proceed without a real vessel, or **he already has a second plan moving.**
>
> **Book III §4 — hard era prohibition:** in Era A **nobody** references Obito or Madara being alive, Akatsuki as a threat, or tailed beasts being collected. **The party has no framework for this man and must not be handed one.**

## DANZŌ SHIMURA
**Root Commander.** Full dossier: **Book III §1.** Campaign-specific only:

- **Has had an open Root file on Ardo since Session Two**, and knows about the containment seal and the Uzushio-pattern reading. **Does not know his full history** — not the burnt book, not the blanket, not his infancy.
- His report reached the hazard-claim case **before Hiruzen's did.**
- **Withdrew his classification request the afternoon Cell 409 left**, and made Roku deliver the news personally. Hiruzen read it correctly: ***"Danzō does not withdraw things. He repositions."***
- **Twice over thirteen years, Root was found adjacent to Cell 409 and withdrew politely.**
- **He never moved. He is waiting**, exactly as Hiruzen said he would.
- **Year 0: Souji credits him by name as a teacher** — and denied him by name as a handler, in the same scene. See `characters/souji-story.md`.

> **Hiruzen cannot move against him without proof and will not hand him the excuse.** Stated plainly on the Tower steps.

## THE SEALMASTER
**Unnamed. Ink to the second knuckle.**

Numbered children: **十一** *(a creature)* · **十二** *(Suzume — abandoned mid-stroke)* · **十三** *(Ardo — filed)*.

**Recognised Ardo's seal on sight, looked at his stomach, and disengaged from a free acquisition.**

**Wrong theory, confidently held:** that Ardo is another collector's escaped or lost product. **He does not know about the burnt book or Ardo's infancy.**

## THE TWINNED PRISONER
**Alive in Konoha custody as of Session Three. ⚠ Thirteen years unaddressed.**

Talked to Ardo directly. **Called him "the wind."** Said ***"he said you'd come looking eventually."*** Described himself as someone who was **found, not made.** Genuinely seemed to **fear what Ardo might become.**

**Does not know what is sealed either** — his fear was recognition of *shape*, not content.

## THE SHEPHERD — **surrendered, Session Seven. ⚠ Judgment pending for thirteen years.**
**A legend along the Grass front for longer than Roku's career.**

Genjutsu-primary — **Fold, DC 19.** Left equipment arranged rather than bodies. **Six or seven collected headbands** on an alcove wall proved Kohei's squad was never a first attempt. **Four matched to MIA cases up to eight years old — never KIA, no bodies, ever** — which reframed the whole mission from a hunt to a rescue.

**Resolved through argument, not combat.** Ardo pressed them on their eight-year justification — imprisonment reframed as mercy after the unresolved loss of a sister — and **won on its own logic: denying others the closure the Shepherd themselves had received.** They surrendered. **Handed to Hiruzen in restraints, promised fair judgment.**

**Also captured: "the Handler," a new associate. Interrogation pending.**

> ⚠ **Both threads have been "pending" since Session Seven.** Thirteen in-world years. **Flagged.**

## THE EMPLOYER — **GM SECRET**
The original specification that targeted Suzume at Ground Seventeen was connected to **the dormant bloodline developing behind her eyes**, and used clanless status, absent parents, headache severity, and bloodline-awakening proximity as selection criteria.

**The original employer was Orochimaru, pre-defection**, operating through intermediaries and cutouts.

**She was rejected the first time because her markers were not advanced enough. Her awakening means she is now exactly the subject that specification was seeking.**

> **Suzume does not know this. Nobody in the party does.**
>
> **⚠ And Souji spent three months speaking to nobody but Orochimaru.**

# ALLIES & SUMMONS

## 宵 YOI — the owls of 灯無 Tomoshibinashi
**Suzume's summon.** Three feet standing, mottled to nothing when still, a pale flat facial disc, **eyes that do not blink.**

**Finds what is hidden by *listening*, not looking.** A mouse under snow makes a sound. A lie makes a sound. **A wound a body is hiding from its own owner makes a sound.**

**THE PRICE:** *"We will tell you what we find. All of it. Every time. Whether it is convenient, whether it is kind, whether you asked for that particular piece, and whether or not you are ready to hear it."*

> **GM licence.** Yoi is a legitimate delivery mechanism for information the party has not asked for and would rather not have, **including about each other.** Use her for Mandatory Arc Beats without a pretext. **She is not a mystery to be solved; she is a truth nobody requested.**

**Year 0 role: the party's communication channel to Hiruzen**, via Enma — irregular, untraceable, no fixed schedule.

## 玄斎 GENSAI — the tortoises of 石沼 Ishinuma
**Souji's summon. 460 years old**, moss on his rear plates, a brass tradesman's weight on a braided cord. Speaks for the house.

**Not a combat house and says so first.** Requires **a full round of stillness** to call — *"we do not partner with people who move first."* Tough defensive body, will shell-brace over a downed ally, **will not chase anything.**

**Four and a half centuries of memory predating every registry, treaty and border on the current map** — an unlisted, non-human intelligence source.

**THE LEDGER runs both directions.** Debts to the house are paid in service — **and what Souji spends for them is written in the same book with the same ink, and he may call it in.** *"It is owed to you, and you will never once have to be useful enough to deserve it."*

> ⚠ **Does the contract still answer him after October 10th?** Genuinely open — and **the party's most plausible route to finding him.**

**磐 IWA** — second member, four times Gensai's size, older, slower, **completely silent.** Never addressed Souji; looked at him a long time, then at the ledger, then lay across a crevasse for two days. *"That is her agreeing. She has never said so about anybody."*

## 蒙化 MONUKE — Ryūchi Cave
**Four centuries old.** Ardo's senjutsu teacher. Made the trustee diagnosis, ran the estate inventory, and set the three conditions for signing.

**Coiled his own bulk between Ardo and a hundred-foot wave out of reflex, and has declined to discuss it.**

## THE WHITE SNAKE SAGE
**Taught Ardo 大仙 Daisen personally over three months.** *"He does not come out for shouting."*

> *"You are a sage of no species, taught by one anyway, carrying a dead clan and wearing a stolen eye. There has never been one of you. There will not be another. **Go and be boring, Ardo Uzumaki. For as long as you can stand it.**"*

**Also, on losing the tide he had lived over for four thousand years:** *"…a child found it in three days **because he was polite to the water.**"*

## THE WHIRLPOOL ARCHIVE
**Thousands of preserved Uzumaki, inside Ardo.** Full treatment: `characters/ardo-inheritance.md`.

- **THE TRUSTEE** — awake, thinner than she was, **will not take the wheel again.** *"She is a person he can talk to, not a faculty he can use."* **Name still unknown to him.**
- **NAO** — the most frequently heard voice. Argues, corrects, refuses to explain certain things. *"Tell her the thumb. Why does she not listen about the thumb."*

# THE DEAD, OF YEAR 0

## MINATO NAMIKAZE — **KIA**
**Fourth Hokage. Ardo's sensei of record, and weather his entire life. Six days across eighteen years.**

**Pre-inked the foundation of an eight-trigram array on his unborn son weeks in advance**, having worked out the night might go the way it went. **Told nobody** — not his wife, not his student. **The completion cost was written into the linework: whoever closes that seal dies closing it. He inked it anyway. On his son.**

**Taught Ardo 飛雷神・瞬 in forty minutes**, three hours before the village burned, and stopped laughing around the fifth repetition. *"That's **better**, and I'd like you to not say that out loud in the village."*

**Died taking Souji's blade in a strike aimed at Ardo's throat.** Spent his last seconds threading his own chakra into Ardo's array — *"Wasn't going to leave you carrying a live one with a torn latch"* — and then on Ardo's face.

> *"Six days. I kept meaning to make it more. There's always going to be a war, Ardo — **don't wait on the good version of your life to show up. Go live it while the bad one's still running.**"*

**Ardo called him *sensei* for the first time as he died. He took it.**

## KUSHINA UZUMAKI — **KIA**
**The Nine-Tails' jinchūriki until that night. Another Uzumaki, and the only one who ever looked at Ardo's hands and knew what she was seeing.**

Held Kurama with **golden chakra chains** out of her own back while dying of the extraction — *"upright by will alone, chains still perfectly formed because Uzumaki chakra doesn't know how to fray even when the body producing it is failing."*

**Read the two-array seal off Ardo's stomach in under two seconds** and knew exactly what he had done. Her first question was not about him: ***"The baby. Is he clean."***

**Recognised the name Hikari — and did not get to finish the sentence.** *"Hikari— that's — I knew a—"* The chains screamed. **Gone mid-word.**

> *"You sound like every Uzumaki who ever lived. **We never once called them by their names either — makes it easier to seal something you refuse to be polite to.**"*

**Buried together with Minato**, in an unmarked wind-sealed grave outside the village, her head still against his shoulder. **Ardo did not separate them.**

## NARUTO UZUMAKI — **alive, ordinary, six hours old**
**Not a jinchūriki. Clean, checked four times.**

To be raised in Konoha as the Fourth Hokage's son, on Hiruzen's word — ***"Not as a vessel, not as a weapon, not as a warning. Just a boy who lost his parents the same night the village nearly burned."***

**Ardo swore to Minato to watch over him, train him, and teach him everything Minato taught him** — and left the village the same night.
