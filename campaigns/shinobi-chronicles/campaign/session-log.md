# ACT ONE — ERA A

## 一 · SESSION ONE — "GROUND SEVENTEEN"

**Roku's bell test became a real incident.** A staked, altered creature; a staged murder; **an abduction attempt on Suzume.**

Ardo tracked alone, invented **Gale Fist · Twinned**, and **綻び the Seam opened for the first time**, landing 虚 Hollow.

**Suzume was taken north to the Nakano outflow**, checked against a bingo book, **assigned 十二 — Twelve**, and abandoned when the sealmaster realised she was not the intended subject. **She survived.**

*Ardo ended at Health 20/36, Stamina 0 (Exhausted), and ~73 Chakra — which Roku noticed.*

## 二 · SESSION TWO — "THE PAPERWORK AND THE WARM MEAL"

Hospital wake-up. **Danzō arrived offering an exchange.** The first Root reading was destroyed when Ardo fed it chakra against instruction; **the second found the containment/Uzushio structure.** Ardo tried to force the Seam under interrogation and **it correctly refused.** Roku pulled him out physically.

**Danzō has had an open file since this session.**

## 三 · SESSION THREE — "PROOF"

Ardo snuck into the hospital to question **the Twinned prisoner** — learned he wasn't the sealmaster, and that the sealmaster's subordinate died protecting him.

**The calm Seam variant succeeded, twice, deliberately**, with Roku and Suzume present as a safety net. Two natural 20s. A real answer instead of a smear: ***"Not yet. But not never."***

Ardo told Suzume and Roku everything. **Suzume shared what she overheard on the riverbank and got a real specialist appointment out of it** — which confirmed the migraines were real, were not ordinary eye strain, and had no explanation.

**Fourteen-day training montage.** Clone Technique to Proficient. **Hazard claim cleared — 42,000 ryō, split three ways.** Splendor developed and proven across three light missions.

## 四 · SESSION FOUR — "THE EXAM THAT WASN'T ONE"

A D-rank chicken-and-grain theft turned into **recruiting Souta** — a starving war orphan talked down instead of fought, vouched for at the gate, offered a home the same night.

**Summoned by Hiruzen the next morning.** Ardo disclosed the Seam in full, in a cleared room, one on one. Won a **scaled-down exam path** judged personally by Hiruzen rather than a board, and got it explicitly separated from the still-pending personal search through the Land of Fire.

**Rai left Kuro home.**

## 五 · SESSION FIVE — "GRASS FRONT"

**The worst session of Act One's first half.**

A courier mission escalated immediately: a Konoha squad — Kohei's, Roku's own former student — **found gone, gear arranged rather than fought over.** The squad tracked them to a fifth figure, calm, holding four survivors bound but alive. **"The Shepherd."**

Combat opened with area genjutsu (**Fold, DC 19**). Ardo shrugged it off on a natural 20. **Rai went down unresponsive. The Shepherd turned on him. Roku's Reaction missed by inches.**

### **RAI INUZUKA DIED.**

**Suzume's Sharingan awakened on the spot — 2 tomoe.** Ardo's grief tore the Seam open involuntarily; **Hollow fired for the second time ever.** An improvised wind compression strike became **圧縮弾 Compression Shell.** A longer, deliberate stay in the mindscape followed — **the door didn't close on its own for the first time** — producing **借風 Borrowed Gale** and **紅風 Kōfū.**

**The fight resolved:** **迅雷・嵐雷神 Storming Thunder God** forced into existence mid-cast on a natural 20, staggering the Shepherd. **Suzume closed it with a three-action combo capped by a second natural 20.** The Shepherd escaped wounded and marked — **the first survived encounter with them on record.**

### Aftermath

**Ardo tried to force Kōfū a second time at Rai's body, on fury alone. It refused him outright** — establishing the standing **Grief/Rage Distinction.**

**The pyre.** Kuro knew before anyone told him. Tsume Inuzuka lit it. **Ardo took Rai's headband and left his own on the fire**, then declared himself **"Shadow Kage"** in front of the whole Inuzuka clan.

## 六 · SESSION SIX — "THE EVALUATION"

Hiruzen attended the funeral personally, then requested Ardo and Suzume privately. **Suzume gave a full account of her Sharingan; Hiruzen is containing it deliberately** — no Uchiha council disclosure unless she asks, Root kept out, medical file reclassified. **That door stays closed until she opens it. Her call, standing.**

Ardo pushed for rank reevaluation. Hiruzen refused to rubber-stamp it but **sanctioned a real evaluation on his own authority** — a full-contact match against Roku, judged on conduct under pressure, **deliberately delayed three days so neither would be tested while still running on grief.**

**Three days at Ground Six.** Storming Thunder God to Practiced. **Pulse Thread to Practiced, unlocking Split Current** on the tenth deliberate repetition.

**The match.** Roku proved exactly why the combo-limit warnings exist — Substituted out of Ardo's opener, then **Counter-Windowed a takedown that put Ardo on the ground before two of three actions happened.** Suzume answered alone in round two, landing all four actions clean.

**Round three: the campaign's first real Team Combo**, linked live outside the printed consecutive-initiative rule. Pulse Thread broke Roku's save and left him Open → Storming Thunder God at full momentum stunned him → Leaf Whirlwind drove him into the ground. **39 damage, zero interruption, +15 Team Special each.** **Roku conceded on the spot.**

> **One standing table ruling came out of this match**, logged to Book IV §2: **a lost-reach Displace no longer auto-ends the attacker's combo — the attacker gets one reroll to close back in.**

## 七 · SESSION SEVEN — "THE SHEPHERD, CLOSED"

**Chūnin vests taken.** Souji Terada joined the cell the same day, assigned by Hiruzen personally.

**Downtime:** Ardo's second natural 20 on the Wind/Lightning fusion — deliberate training this time — pushed Storming Thunder God to Proficient and **unlocked Thunder Gods Gale.** Suzume unlocked **Mirrored Opening.** **Souji's Clanless feat resolved as Unbroken Stance**, decided out loud in front of both of them rather than alone — new for him.

**Four headbands matched to MIA cases up to eight years old — never KIA, no bodies, ever.** The mission reframed from hunt to rescue.

**A fresh abduction put the squad on a live trail.** Souji's Root Grip locked the culprit; **Suzume's Nerve Lock missed on a natural 1**, forcing a hostage standoff. **Souji ended it with Kanketsu — and the Senju bloodline awakened live, witnessed by the entire squad.**

**The confrontation itself resolved through dialogue, not combat.** Ardo pressed the Shepherd on their eight-year justification and **won the argument on its own logic. The Shepherd surrendered.** Two rescued shinobi reunited with family at the gate; eight-year-old case files closed.

**Closed on the rooftops above the village, the whole squad, silent, together.**

## 八 · SESSION EIGHT — "TRAINING GROUND SIX"

> ## The worst day in the history of Cell 409, and it started as a friendly spar.

**Ardo and Souji, full contact, no stakes.** It escalated the way things escalate between two boys who like each other, and **every escalation was legitimate right up until it wasn't.**

**Ardo unlocked 渦風 UZUFŪ** — not by reaching. **Souji's Senju chakra surged nearby and the thing behind the seal reacted on its own**, frantic and protective. **Three escalating CON saves, all passed** — and passed by refusing to ride the rage and instead reaching back to *calm the thing panicking on the other side.* **Then, untaught, he turned the whirl-pattern seal into a channel instead of letting it rip.**

**Souji faced the same test and failed it by three points.** He roared *"Ardo! We control our future together!"* and meant it. **The vow underneath his ribs didn't believe him.** He took Ardo's hand anyway — 1 to a 13 — **and it wasn't enough. 不倒樹 FUTŌJU took him.**

### The machine

Ardo dropped out of Uzufū to help him up. **Their hands touched, and the thing behind the seal got Senju chakra delivered directly into its own hand. Uzufū inverted into 逆渦 SAKAUZU.**

**Eleven minutes** of perfect speed that couldn't be reacted to against perfect immovability that couldn't be chased.

**Suzume ran three kilometres for Roku**, hating it the whole way, because going down there alone would only have made her the third child in the crater. Roku got the Hokage.

**Hiruzen Sarutobi recognised both of them on sight** — Hashirama's ground-work in one, Uzushio whirl-pattern sealwork in the other, in two orphans with no clan on paper, in a cell he assigned himself. ***"I know what I'm looking at."*** He refused to fight Fudō's rule and **liquefied the ground instead**, then pinned Souji with Enma's staff.

**And in doing so removed the only thing suppressing Sakauzu's tripled speed.**

### What Suzume did

**Sakauzu went for the unconscious Souji. Nothing on that field could intercept it.**

**She flash-stepped into the gap and turned her back on the punch so her body would cover his completely.** She took a full-force 空爆 Kūbaku detonation at zero range — **99 damage against 80 maximum Health. Souji took nothing. It worked exactly as she intended.**

**万華鏡写輪眼 — the Mangekyō awakened as she died.**

Dying, with her last voluntary motion: ***"See what I see... carry my ninja way with you."*** **She removed her own left eye and put it in his hands.** Ardo dug out his own in the mud and **completed the exchange in both directions.**

**Sakauzu saw what Ardo's hand had done and let go of the aperture like it burned.**

Then Ardo reached inward with nothing left: ***"We did this to her. So please. Let's fix this."*** **It took the *we*.** *"Yes. We did."* It moved his untrained hands through textbook medical ninjutsu and **spent nearly all of itself buying four minutes** — the entire margin between the crater and the hospital.

Its last words before going quiet: ***"...She reminded me of somebody."***

### Three days later

**Ardo woke to a Mangekyō that will never switch off and an empty mindscape. 荒れ川 ARAKAWA** — and the retroactive gut-punch that **it was never his chakra control, it was the entity's, the whole time.**

**Suzume had been awake for all three days** — conscious and trapped in a mind-built copy of her hospital room **with no door**, testing the walls in rows until she stopped and sat down.

Ardo took her hand, **moved Rai's headband to cover his own eye**, opened a closed chakra circuit, and looked. **Control check 21 vs DC 15.** The wall opened. **She woke. 迎え火 MUKAEBI named itself in the doing.**

Her first words: *"You took your time."* Her second, refusing his apology: ***"I didn't step in front of it for you… Don't turn the best thing I ever did into something you did to me."***

**Souji did not speak for two days.** Ardo came to his room, pulled up the chair nobody had been sitting in, **and said nothing for four hours.** At sundown Souji spoke, to the window: ***"...Is she alive."***

### 遊撃班 YŪGEKIHAN — the departure

**Cell 409 left Konoha. All three, by choice, out loud, on the record, with the Hokage's seal.**

Hiruzen's four conditions: **designated, not wanderers** · **they report irregularly** · **Roku goes as jōnin of record, not leash** · **ask Suzume properly, awake, with a real door to refuse through.**

> **Souji set that fourth condition independently, four hours earlier, in a hospital room. Neither he nor Hiruzen knew the other had said it.**

**Suzume said yes, first, before any of her own three conditions.**

Hiruzen, privately, apologised — **not for the night, for the eight years before it.** *"Go and find out what you are. Come back and tell me. **And come back** — this village has produced exactly one boy who walked out past the walls looking for answers and never came home, and I have been paying for that particular failure for thirty years."*

**He did not say the name.**

## 九 · SESSION NINE — RYŪCHI CAVE, AND THE ROAD

**The campaign split.** Ardo's uncontracted summoning rolled a 24 and **reverse-summoned him** into 龍地洞 Ryūchi Cave — no smoke, no flash, a fused glassy circle in the dirt and a pack left standing in the grass.

**Roku, Souji and Suzume walked northeast for six months to find him**, navigating on Suzume's half of a paired eye past the end of Roku's map. *(Full log: `campaign/archive/squad-two-journey.md`, closed.)*

**Both squads signed their first contracts:** Souji to the **Ishinuma tortoises** (Gensai, on one true sentence), Suzume to the **Tomoshibinashi owls** (Yoi, who audited her on sight and was right about all of it).

### What Ardo did underground

**拮抗 Kikkō** — the counterweight, invented in two days, found by refusing to be still. Then, day two: **潮合 SHIOAI. Senjutsu, achieved, by his own method** — *you don't still a tide, you keep time with it.* **Contracted to no species, owing nobody.**

**Monuke's reframe: the entity was never a prisoner. It was a trustee, and the seal was a bequest.** 荒神 Aragami — the campaign's worst-diagnosed threat — **closed.**

**The will, read.** Four coils: **器 Utsuwa** the vessel · **手綱 Tazuna** the reins · **癒しの手** the healing hands, with a person still attached · and underneath them all, **潮風 SHIOKAZE** — the original form every transformation he ever built was a crude copy of.

**He took it.** *"I'm ready, mother."* **The first time he had ever used the word.**

### And then, under the sea floor

**渦の書庫 — the Whirlpool Archive.** A seal array the size of a coastline. **Thousands of preserved Uzumaki**, hidden as far from the Land of Whirlpools as it is possible to get, so the craft would survive if the island fell.

**The island fell twenty years ago and nobody ever came for them.**

**He did not take. He offered** — *"It's to complete the archive, and for us to be whole again"* — **and every one of them said yes.**

**He learned his mother's name — HIKARI — and claimed his own out loud.**

**The tide under Ryūchi Cave stopped, permanently.**

# 十二 · SESSION TWELVE — "THE TENTH OF OCTOBER"
## **Year 0, October 10th. The end of Act One.**

### Before

**Minato taught Ardo 飛雷神・瞬 Hiraishin no Shun in forty minutes**, rebuilt around the Mangekyō — *marks placed by looking, nothing written, nothing left behind.* He watched Ardo do it eight times and stopped laughing around the fifth. **Roughly three hours before the village burned.**

### The attack

**A masked man — orange spiral, off-centre, one eyehole on the right — tore the Nine-Tails out of Kushina Uzumaki** and released it on Konoha.

**Ardo and Suzume arrived by 音置き Otooki**, landing thirty feet from Kushina, who knelt with golden chains blazing out of her back into the base of Kurama's throat, **dying upright by will alone.**

**Souji was on the ridge above, watching, and had been for four seconds.**

### The clearing

Kushina read the seal off Ardo's stomach in under two seconds and knew exactly what he had done. **Her first question was about the baby.** Then his name. Then his mother's.

***"Hikari— that's — I knew a—"***

**The chains screamed. She never finished it.**

**Souji came down the slope without being asked and poured 息吹 Ibuki into her** — his own Health and Stamina — buying the ninety seconds in which any of that happened at all.

### The seal transfer

**Minato had pre-inked an eight-trigram array on his newborn son weeks in advance**, knowing the completion cost was the sealer's life. **He told nobody.**

**Ardo unspooled it off the infant stroke by stroke in reverse order, left Naruto completely clean, and wrote it onto his own stomach over his mother's containment array.** *Fūinjutsu check: 26 vs DC 20.*

**He shut the archive out first** so he could not be argued out of it. **The last thing through before the door closed:** *"—love, destination isn't the same as conduit, I was talking about the rout—"*

**Kushina died as the chains went dark.** Her last deliberate act was **opening her hand** — not dropping Ardo's jacket. Opening it.

### Inside

**九喇嘛 Kurama came in like weather**, and a gate built itself in real time to hold it. It stopped when it saw what was already in there.

> ***"Why do you already have this many ghosts in here."***

Ardo asked it, through the pain, whether that rage was what it felt all the time. **It answered honestly, and at length**, and said the thing that mattered:

> *"I do not hate the ones who only want to live… **I hate that it has never once mattered whether I did.**"*
>
> *"You ask if I hate Suzume. I do not know Suzume. **That is the wound** — I have never once been given long enough with anyone to find out whether I hate them or not before the leash tightens again."*

Ardo refused to call it a monster. **Kurama's answer:** *"Go on refusing, then. **See how long it survives contact with what's coming for both of us.**"*

### The turn

Ardo, coming up out of it, accused Souji of having been absent and of clinging to Danzō. **Minato defended him from the ground, holding his dead wife. Suzume defended him louder.** Souji said the smallest true thing and let the silence do the rest.

**Then Ardo moved to pull him into a hug and apologise.**

> ### *"Sorry, Ardo… we must grow stronger."*

**The sword went through Ardo's stomach, through live seal work, and came back out.** Souji gave a speech, credited **Danzō** by name — having flatly denied him ten minutes earlier — **put Suzume into a treeline**, and swung to cut Ardo down.

### **MINATO NAMIKAZE STEPPED INTO IT.**

Yellow light collapsing into a man-shaped space half a heartbeat too late to dodge and **exactly in time to choose.**

**Souji felt the wrong body take the hit**, and for one unguarded second the boy underneath was right there, staring at what his own hand had done. **Then he was gone**, with an invitation: ***"When you're ready, truly ready, come find me."***

**Minato used what was left threading his own chakra into Ardo's array** — *"Wasn't going to leave you carrying a live one with a torn latch"* — and then on Ardo's face.

> *"Six days. **Don't wait on the good version of your life to show up. Go live it while the bad one's still running.**"*
>
> *"You get him there. That's the whole job now. Not mine anymore."*

**Ardo called him *sensei*. He took it.**

### After

**Ardo buried them together in unmarked wind-sealed ground** — her head still against his shoulder, because they had not let go of each other and he was not going to be the one who did it for them. **No headstone; the Fourth Hokage's death has to stay a secret a little longer.**

*"I'll come back and do this properly. Both of you get a real one. I swear it."*

**Suzume was alive in the treeline.** Woke up and diagnosed him before asking about herself.

**Konoha: burning.** Naruto: safe, ordinary, screaming, **and watched twenty minutes earlier by a masked man at the treeline who left without doing anything.** Kurama identified him instantly.

**The Tower assembly.** Ardo told Hiruzen all three things standing on the steps. Hiruzen did not stagger — sixty years teaches you how to take that standing — and filled in *"Danzō"* himself before Ardo finished the sentence, **with dread rather than surprise.**

He announced the Fourth's death to the village himself, resumed the hat, and agreed to raise Naruto: ***"I'll raise him like I raised you. Better, if I can manage it."***

And set the terms for the hunt: **"whatever you and Suzume go do out there, the village cannot be seen doing it with you."**

> *"Go get your brother back, Ardo. Or find out for certain that there's nothing left of him to get back. Either way, **come home after** — that's not Hokage's orders. That's just an old man who's already burying enough people tonight."*

### The hospital — the last three hours of the tenth

**Suzume closed the wound** with 癒し眼 open, and found something she made him listen to: **Souji missed the artery by less than a centimetre**, on a stationary target, at contact range. *"Souji does not miss."*

**Ardo healed her back** — two ribs, a hyperextended joint, a scalp laceration — with his own hands, and 癒しの手 under them, and adult hands with two old burn scars adjusting a smaller pair somewhere far underneath.

He started a sentence and stopped it.

> *"Suzume, I—"* … *"…Nevermind."*
>
> ***"I know."*** *"I've known for eleven years… So don't. Not tonight. Not in a hospital. Not with him still out there."* — ***"But when we get him back? You're saying it properly. With your whole mouth. Somewhere with a door."***

### And then he lifted the headband and turned everything on.

**大仙・潮風 · 大仙・潮合 · 大仙・手綱**, inside a triage floor, in a village that had just burned. **Every sensor in the Land of Fire. Every sage alive.** The cost the White Snake Sage named — *"every time you enter it, you are choosing to be seen"* — **paid, deliberately, to work a hospital.**

Suzume's only comment: ***"Then be quick and be enormous."***

**What it actually did:** 満ち MICHI, the flood beat, extended past his skin. **Three floors of people held still** — bleeding slowed, shock loosened, bodies that had decided to stop deciding talked into waiting — while nine exhausted medics did the real work inside the space he bought them. **He was not treating four hundred people. He was holding a building.**

**The corridor filled up.**

| Who came | What happened |
|---|---|
| **A runner from the Tower** | Ardo's answer: *"I'm carrying forth mine and **Rai's** ninja way."* Hiruzen's reply, relayed exactly: *"Tell him I am not angry and I am not surprised, and that those are two different failures"* |
| **Four ANBU** | *"He sent us to find out what you are."* / *"And?"* / ***"You're a man doing triage."*** **Two of them went and worked the second floor east as medics** |
| **三島 Mishima Sōbei**, Tower sealing archivist, 51 years | Read the linework off Ardo's forearms, said what it was, said where he had seen it before — **the Tower vault** — and **left without asking a single further question.** A folded slip under a pen: ***"Ask about the alliance with the island."*** |
| **Two Uchiha police** | Came for the enormous thing, **found Suzume's right eye in a window reflection** at thirty metres. **Ardo moved to step in front of her and she stopped him** — *"If you move, you decide it for them."* **They left without saying anything. Deferred, not resolved** |

**Naruto was brought and fell asleep in Ardo's arms.** Kurama looked at the child it was supposed to be sealed into and said, eventually: ***"…He has her hair."***

**The arithmetic, from a nineteen-year-old with a clipboard:** *"We've lost eleven people since midnight. Before you started, we were losing about one every four minutes. **It's been a hundred and forty minutes.**"*

**Suzume, for the record:** ***"This is the ninja way. Not the fight. This."***

### What it cost

**−2 Max Vision** (one for the clearing, one for the choice to uncover and work). **A report in an ANBU captain's hand that says *a man doing triage*, and she knows how that will read.** A sealmaster deciding what he is *obliged* to file. **And two Uchiha who have not decided yet whether they saw anything.**

### What it bought

**癒しの手 stopped being something Ardo owns and became something he can do.** Four hundred patients in one night, and around the ninetieth his hands stopped asking the coil questions.

> **The thumb. Four times in a row. Without thinking.**
>
> *A voice he has never been able to put a face to, arriving over the sound of hands adjusting hands:* ***"—like that."***

# 十三 · SESSION THIRTEEN — "THE ALLIANCE WITH THE ISLAND"
## Year 0, October 11th · dawn · **IN PROGRESS**

### The vault

**Ardo walked into the Tower's lower archive at 05:40 and out again at 05:45.**

Two of the three doors were a bored chūnin and a lock. **The third has no handle and no hinges** — a slab cut with linework so fine it reads as grain until you're close, and then it is an inward-facing spiral with a place at the centre for a palm.

**He recognised the hand.** Not the style — *the hand.* The lean. The hurry at the line-ends. **It is the same hand that wrote the burnt book.**

He put his palm in the centre and did not push chakra. **He let the tide come up to his skin and keep time.** The spiral rolled over once and opened.

> **No alarm. There is no alarm to trip.** Whoever built that door never once considered that a thief might be Uzumaki. **It let him in because it thinks he's family.**

**Six items on four shelves. He took one and left the rest.**

> ### 渦 — 同盟 — 実施 — 保留 / 三
> **WHIRLPOOL — ALLIANCE — EXECUTION — HELD IN ABEYANCE / 3**
>
> Signed twice: an unknown hand, and beneath it **Sarutobi Hiruzen, Year −18. Two years before the island fell.**

**He did not open it.** It went in the satchel.

### The staircase

**三島 宗兵衛 Mishima Sōbei was sitting on the fourth step with cold tea**, having worked out how long the door would take.

He is an archivist. That is the entire answer and he was irritated to be asked for a better one — *"You keep waiting for the part where I turn out to be important. There isn't one. **That's the point of me.**"*

**What he gave, sideways, while complaining about other things:** he filed the alliance in **Year −38** and something else in **Year −41**, *"and they don't say the same thing, and I have known that for thirty-two years, and I have never once had anybody to say it to."* **He has never opened the case.** He filed the outside of it, dusted it twice a year, and learned the shape of the thing from handling it.

**保留**, in his gloss: *"Somebody decided. And then decided not to do it. And then never un-decided it, so it just… sat."*

**Why now:** *"Thought I'm seventy-one. Thought I've got maybe four good years and a bad one. Thought if I take it with me nobody ever asks."*

**What he will report:** that the third door opened. **Not who** — *"because I was on this staircase with my eyes shut like an old man who fell asleep where he sat. That's not a favour. That's the exact width of what I can do and stay a man who's never lied on a sheet."*

**His advice:** ***"Read it somewhere you can afford to be angry. Not in a room with people. Not in this village."***

**And on his way up the stairs, to a wall, not turning around:** *"Your mother's people were good to us. That's not in a file, that's just something I remember. They came, they built things with us, and the ones I met were **loud**, which I liked, and then it— Anyway."*

### The open road

They left by the north-east gate and did not use the road for the first hour. **No papers, no cell designation, no sanction** — Hiruzen's condition. Suzume rode most of the distance in a sling of Ardo's making because her left arm is unusable until midday and two of her ribs are still knitting.

They stopped roughly **twenty kilometres out**, in a fold of ground with a stream and no sightline to the village.

### The scroll, read where he could afford to be angry

**He did not open it first. He handed it to Suzume** — *"I don't know what this is. Maybe you should read it before I do."*

**渦 — 同盟 — 実施 — 保留 / 三.** The alliance between Konoha and the island was not a sentiment. It was **an instrument with an execution clause** — the obligation to reinforce, drafted, costed, and ready to be run. **It was drafted. It was never run.** 保留: *held in abeyance*, signed by **Sarutobi Hiruzen in Year −18**, and never once rescinded in the twenty-eight years since.

**Two years later the island fell**, and the thing that would have answered was sitting in a case behind a door with no handle, being dusted twice a year by a man who never opened it.

> **Ardo, standing, not crying, looking down at her:** *"My people gave their lives and sacrificed so much and Hiruzen couldn't even tell me."*
>
> And then, after a long time: *"It doesn't matter anymore. Together, we helped restore the archive of my people within me."* — **"Thank you, Suzume. You really are amazing."**

> ⚠ **He has not decided what to do with this.** He has not written to Hiruzen, not turned around, and not said the word *betrayal* out loud. **The scroll is still in the satchel.**

### The mindscape, first night — the trustee finishes her sentence

Ardo went in before sleeping and the **trustee** was waiting to complete the sentence the seal transfer cut in half.

> *"…**Route**,"* she said. *"I was talking about the **route**."*
>
> *"You heard **destination** because you were already looking for somewhere to put it, and you had decided the somewhere was going to be **you**."*

**What the Uzumaki craft actually was:** *"Everyone else's sealing puts a thing somewhere and holds it. **Ours moved.** We put enormous things into a rotation and passed them hand to hand."* Not vessels. **A relay.**

> *"You do it in your body already. Every day. You have never once thought to do it **with people**."*

**And, incidentally, devastatingly:** **Hikari was four years old and in Konoha** during the alliance delegation season. His mother stood in that village as a child, in the years the instrument was live.

**Her instruction:** ask the archive about finding Souji **in the morning, out loud, where Suzume can hear it.**

### The second descent — the rotation, seen

Ardo went back in the same night, deeper, and **lit everything at once — 潮風 Shiokaze, 潮合 Shioai, 大仙 Daisen** — then **lifted the headband inside his own mindscape** and turned the Mangekyō on the seal itself.

**What the eye showed him:** the whirl-mark is not a pattern, it is a **mechanism**, and it has been running for four hundred generations of corrections. **Minato's eight-trigram array is flawless and assumes an empty vessel.** Ardo can now point at the exact line where the two disagree.

**Kurama, civil for the second time:** *"You're not going deeper. You're already at the bottom. That's the problem."* — *"Try not shoving. Just once."*

**He stopped pushing, and the rotation came up.** The river off the floor as counterweight. Natural energy finding its speed. The estate turning on a beat and legible. **Nothing spent. Nothing burning.** He felt the whole of it, one arc from turning —

**— and the fourth arm was nearly empty.** Suzume alone, holding, asleep, ribs wired. The other three arcs swung into the gap three times, found nothing to hand off to, and **set themselves back down.**

> **Not locked. Not refused.** *It cannot turn on one person.*
>
> **Kurama:** *"Your gate's not locked, boy. Your **room's** empty."*

**Nothing in there named it.** The fox did not. The estate did not. **The name is still waiting.**

### The fire on a hill

He held 大仙 for **eleven minutes in the open**, at night, over open country, with no shielding — the exact thing the White Snake Sage warned could not be hidden. **Suzume woke up.**

**Something is in the treeline to the north-east.** It has been there a little while.

### The name

Ardo said it himself, unprompted, as **a description** — *"the whirling tide"* — reaching for the plainest words that fit what he had just stood inside.

**渦潮.** He did not say it as a name. He did not know it was one.

**The archive went silent for the first time since Session Nine** — four hundred generations of a dead village listening to the last of them work out what they were called, with nobody left to teach him.

**Suzume told him**, badly, sitting down, out of position, thirteen years of holding it coming out wrong: *"That's not a description. It's a **place.**"* And, when it still didn't land: *"**Uzushio.** It's the name of your village."*

> **Why nobody ever said it:** *"It was the last thing you had left that was yours to find. You have been given every single thing you are. By dead people, mostly. And there was exactly one piece left that you were going to have to **reach** for."*
>
> **Kurama, with nothing sharp in it:** *"I've been to that island."*

**⚠ THE CONSTRAINT IS DISCHARGED.** He named it first. He was told afterward. **No NPC supplied the word.**

### 牧野 ハナ Makino Hana — the watcher

Sixty metres out, stood up on her own, walked out with her hands open. **A civilian contract watcher, four days on post, kilometre 18–24 of the north-east trunk road.**

**Her sheet carried both their descriptions** — *red hair, headband over the left eye* · *a woman with a medical kit and a right eye she keeps covered in company* — and a second sheet: ***"Report immediately and by fastest means if the subject demonstrates a significant increase in capability."***

**She does not know who she reports to.** A stone, a folded sheet, money four days later, a broker in Tanzaku, no faces, ever. **The post is continuously staffed and the drop-stone is worn into a dip** — this is years of hands, not months.

**The line she never understood:** *"If the subject makes contact, do not run. Do not fight. **Answer what he asks.**"*

> **Somebody built a tripwire for the exact moment Ardo got stronger — and then instructed their asset to cooperate with him when it tripped.** That is not how Root writes an order. **Who holds the drop remains unresolved by design.**

### What he sent back up the chain

He did not kill her, did not press her, and did not take the ledger. **He dictated a line for the back page:**

> ***"Suzume and I are coming to bring you back home."***

And then gave her the thing four years of that job had never given her — **a name.** *"Souji Terada."*

**She did not put the sheet under the stone at dawn. Or the next day.**

### The road

**大仙 released after roughly twelve minutes.** No drain, no crash, everything returned — *which is the part Suzume was watching.*

He came out of it **loud and genuinely happy** for the first time since Training Ground Six: the world past the border, Monuke, the White Snake Sage, thirteen years of one war in one country. **Suzume recognised the presentation** — forty hours awake, two burials, a stabbing, a bijū and a village's name in one day — **and deliberately did not name it tonight.** She took a sleep commitment at the first waystation instead, and he agreed too fast, and she let that go too.

**They walked north-east into an hour before dawn, toward a border they have no papers for.**

**SESSION THIRTEEN ENDS.**
