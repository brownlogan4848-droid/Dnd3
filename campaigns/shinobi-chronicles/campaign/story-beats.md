# STORY BEATS — *The Long Shadow*

> ## ★ THIS IS THE GM'S PLANNING FILE. Nothing here is canon until it happens at the table.
>
> **`session-log.md` records what happened. This file holds what is *ready to* happen** — beats built, costed and wired to the characters who care about them, so a session never has to be invented from nothing.
>
> **Live mechanical state:** `campaign-state.md` · **What each character wants:** `characters/<name>-story.md` Part Two · **Who may know what:** Book III §4.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

1. **Open `campaign-state.md` first.** Where are they, what's spent, what's injured, what clock is running.
2. **Come here and pick ONE primary beat** from a character's *Ready* list, plus at most one secondary.
3. **Check the beat's `IF` conditions.** If they aren't met, it isn't ready — pick another.
4. **Run it.** Follow the `THEN` branches as the table actually goes.
5. **Afterwards:** move the beat to `FIRED`, write the outcome, and promote whatever it unlocked.

> **⚠ One primary beat per session.** Two competing emotional climaxes in one night is why sessions feel rushed. The rest of the night is travel, complication, and consequence.

### Beat status legend

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **READY** | Conditions met. Can fire tonight |
| **ARMED** | One named condition away |
| **HELD** | Deliberately not yet. The reason is written in |
| **FIRED** | Happened. Kept for the record and for callbacks |
| **⚠ LANDMINE** | A thing that must **not** happen by accident. Read before improvising |

# PART TWO — ARDO

**Arc:** the last librarian of a dead people learns that being the only one left is a problem to be *solved*, not a title to be worn.

**Ladder:** 借風 → 紅風 → 渦風 → 潮合 → 潮風 → 大仙 → **渦潮** *(named, not entered)*

## READY

### A1 — THE DECISION ABOUT SOUJI ★ *the arc's hinge*

**IF** Ardo is face to face with Souji, in any state, anywhere.

**THEN** he must decide whether the bond is **broken** or **holds forever**.

- **Either answer opens Gate 3 and unlocks 渦潮.** It is a decision gate, not a redemption gate.
- **⚠ Do not weight the scene.** No pleading NPC, no last-second reveal that Souji was controlled. If he is controlled, that is discovered *before* or *after*, never during.
- **A decision made in fury is not a decision** — the gate's own first clause. If Ardo is running on rage, the moment does not resolve and Souji leaves again.

**THEN → unlocks** B1 *(the rotation turns)* and closes the campaign's first question.

### A2 — THE SCROLL, ANSWERED

**IF** Ardo reaches any of: a Konoha channel (Yoi → Enma), Hiruzen in person, or a moment where he must explain himself to someone from the village.

**THEN** he has to say out loud what he thinks about **保留** — Konoha drafting an obligation to reinforce Uzushio and holding it.

| He chooses | THEN |
|---|---|
| **Confront Hiruzen** | Hiruzen does not deny it and does not defend it. He explains the room it was decided in. **He does not ask forgiveness** |
| **Say nothing, carry it** | It surfaces later at the worst possible moment, in front of someone who did not need to know |
| **Burn it** | The only copy. **Mishima finds out and it breaks something in him** |
| **Give it to the carriers** | They have wanted proof for twenty years. **It makes Ardo their instrument, not their ally** |

### A3 — THE FIRST TIME SOMEONE CALLS HIM AN UZUMAKI IN PUBLIC

**IF** he uses the name where a Konoha shinobi, a foreign sensor, or a bingo-book professional can hear.

**THEN** the name stops being private grief and becomes intelligence. **Kumo's unnamed entry gets a name.**

## ARMED

### A4 — THE STAND OF TREES

**ARMED — needs:** the party within travelling distance, and one session with no other primary beat.
Thirteen years. Twice within a week of it. Nine archive members placed it in the same region and **two went quiet in a way Nao refuses to explain.**

### A5 — THE LIVING UZUMAKI WHO WAS NOT A DEAD END

**ARMED — needs:** Ardo to raise it out loud with anyone but the archive. He never has.

### A6 — HIS MOTHER'S INTENDED NAME

**ARMED — needs:** a source that isn't the archive. They don't have it. **Kushina did.**
*"That's — I knew a—"* Cut off by the chains. **Hikari was four and in Konoha during the delegation season.**

## HELD

### A7 — THE THREE ROADS OUT OF BLINDNESS

**HELD until Vision reaches the 39–15 band.** That band *rewards* him — advantage on everything the eye does, Mangekyō costs halved — which is when the decision becomes genuinely hard instead of obvious.

**⚠ Road 3 is Suzume's other eye.** She has offered. He refused. **Nobody may raise it again except the two of them.**

### A8 — THE ARCHIVE DIES WITH HIM

**HELD.** Nobody has said out loud that if Ardo dies badly, four hundred generations go with him. **The right person to say it is Kurama, and the right time is when Ardo is being reckless with his own life for someone else's sake.**

## ⚠ LANDMINES

| Do not | Why |
|---|---|
| **Let an NPC hand him a technique** | Utsushi lets him take anything he sees. **The limit is knowledge and practice, not permission.** Earned or copied, never gifted |
| **Solve the Souji question for him** | Who holds Souji is unresolved by GM ruling. **Let the Danzō trail be *nearly* right** |
| **Make 渦潮 free** | It is the only form that spends nothing. Every other form keeps its costs |

# PART THREE — SUZUME

**Arc:** the girl who ran three kilometres for help becomes the person nobody can reach — and finds out that is also a wound.

> **⚠ She is not a support arc.** If a session has no Suzume beat in it, that session is under-built.

## READY

### S1 — THE QUIET ROOM, AND WHY IT WON'T OPEN ★ *her arc's hinge*

**静かな部屋 Tier IV is locked because she has not been alone in her own head since she was fourteen.** Sōgan cannot be refused, works while she sleeps, and worked while she was comatose.

**IF** Ardo and Suzume have a conversation about the circuit — which they have now had once.

**THEN** the road to unlocking it is one of:

| Route | Cost |
|---|---|
| **Ardo closes his end** | **He cannot.** His eye never deactivates. *Dead end, and he has to discover that* |
| **A third party gates the circuit** | Who? Monuke? The carriers? **Everyone who could is someone she'd have to trust with the eye** |
| **⚠ Rhythm** | **Ardo can put two things out of reach of each other by keeping time.** He proved it on his own seal. *Neither of them has connected this out loud. Do not connect it for them* |

**THEN → unlocks** the technique that would have reached Souji by more than three points, immediately before the Souji decision. **That timing is the whole design.**

### S2 — KUROBE

**IF** the party is anywhere Toki's crew can reach.

**THEN** Suzume works the problem: twelve years detached, index edited, **reversible in principle, not by her.**

- **THEN if she pursues it** → she needs the technique's author or someone who knows it. **That road leads to Mitsuki.**
- **THEN if she succeeds** → she has learned a memory technique. **She will have to decide whether she keeps it.**
- **THEN if she fails** → Toki's debt stands anyway, and Toki says so, and that is worse.

### S3 — SOMEBODY IS DESCRIBING HER

*"A woman with a medical kit and a right eye she keeps covered in company."* On Hana's watch sheet **and** in Tazawa's brief. **One channel.**

**IF** she pulls that thread → it runs to the same place as the Souji question. **Do not let it arrive early.**

## ARMED

### S4 — THE UCHIHA

**ARMED — needs:** the party in or near Konoha, or an Uchiha in the field.
Two policemen saw her right eye in a window reflection on the tenth and **went home without reporting it — for now.** She has never been claimed by the clan. Hiruzen has contained it for thirteen years **and that containment is her call to end, not his.**

### S5 — "NEVER AGAIN BE SOMETHING THINGS HAPPEN TO"

**ARMED — needs:** a moment where she is *carried, protected, or left behind* for her own good.
**Ardo has done all three in the last eight days.** She has let it go twice. **The third time should not be let go.**

## HELD

### S6 — THE COUNT SHE STOPPED KEEPING

**HELD.** She stopped counting the dead in Year −8 and later called it the worst decision she ever made. **This surfaces when she loses someone she could have saved, not before.**

# PART FOUR — SOUJI

**Run as:** Book IV §4 profile **Jōnin**, role **Rival**, escalating toward **Wall**. **Not a fight the party is meant to win yet, and he said so himself.**

## The evidence, and it does not reconcile

| For "something is left" | For "he is gone" |
|---|---|
| Poured 息吹 Ibuki into Kushina to buy Ardo ninety seconds | Ran Ardo through the stomach |
| **Missed the artery by under a centimetre**, on a stationary target, at contact range — *Souji does not miss* | Killed Minato Namikaze |
| Apologised **first** | *"Power is what accomplishes your dreams"* |
| *"When you're ready, truly ready, come find me"* | Named Danzō and denied Danzō in one conversation |

## READY

### J1 — THE GRAFT ON 朽木 KUCHIKI

**Somebody grafted a retention onto one of his techniques.** Visible to anyone who reads a technique at the level of its construction. **Nobody has looked yet.**

**IF** the party fights him, or recovers a recording, or gets a witness who saw it used.
**THEN** → hard evidence that Souji has been *worked on*, which is not the same as controlled, and Ardo will want it to be.

### J2 — THE ISHINUMA CONTRACT

**玄斎 Gensai is four centuries old, owes Souji a standing debt, and does not lie.**

**IF** Suzume summons through the Ishinuma line, or the party reaches the tortoises.
**THEN** Gensai answers honestly — **or refuses, which is its own information.** *Do not let him give the whole picture. Book III §4 applies to summons too.*

## 信念 HIS CONVICTION — **Resolve 8**

> ***"I have stood behind those two my whole life. Power is the only thing that has ever moved anyone forward, and I will not be held back again."***
>
> **Crack:** ***"Every step you take is measured against him. You left to stop being in his shadow and it made you a man who can only be described in relation to him."***
>
> **⚠ Neither PC gets shared-experience advantage. They ARE the shadow.**
>
> **⚠ He does not soften on a hit.** He loses his place in the sentence and then accelerates, because being seen accurately is not comfort to him. Full entry and the evidence required: `campaign/npcs.md` → CONVICTIONS. Rules: Book IV §4 Part Six-B.
>
> **⚠ Talking him down does not decide A1.** Resolve 0 makes him *stop*. **Whether the bond is broken or holds is still Ardo's call**, and it can go either way with a man standing there listening.

## ⚠ LANDMINE — WHO HOLDS HIM

**Unresolved by standing GM ruling.** Four live readings: **Danzō · Orochimaru with Danzō as cover · someone else entirely · nobody, and he is attributing it.**

**Never confirm or deny on the party's behalf. Let them chase Danzō and let the trail be *nearly* right.** When it resolves it should **cost something to learn**, not be handed over.

# PART SIX — THE TSUGITE CHAIN *(the engine)*

**継ぎ手 — fragments of the Uzumaki method for moving a sealed bijū between living vessels without killing either.** Why three nations erased Uzushio. Shattered into strangers, count destroyed on purpose.

## The mechanic that drives every beat

> **There is no list and there never was.** Each node **leans toward the next one.** The collector has one and is walking the chain in order. **So can the party — in both directions.**

## Chain state

| | |
|---|---|
| **Collected by Mitsuki** | **3** *(one since recovered — see below)* |
| **Held by Ardo** | **2** — 辰巳 Tatsumi *(living, consenting)* · 金城 徳 Kaneshiro Toku *(via the jar)* |
| **Known living carriers** | The five on the road, at least two of them marked. **Names not yet given** |
| **Total in existence** | **Unknown, and the archive genuinely cannot say.** Their own ancestors destroyed the count |

## READY

### T1 — THE MILL AT KANŌ CROSSING

**Fourth night of the month, from dusk. Under the wheel housing. Walk away, don't wait.** Tazawa ran it twice and never saw who collects.
**He is now past his window, so the collector already knows something went wrong.**

- **THEN if the party stakes it out** → they are watching a drop the collector *knows* is compromised. **Assume it is watched back.**
- **THEN if they make a delivery** → they choose what goes in the jar, and **whatever goes in gets walked to whoever collects.**

### T2 — FUCHINOMIYA

**A valley-sized piece of Uzumaki sealwork: seven or eight engineered wheels of water, each turning at its own rate.** Held by the Kaneshiro household under a Year −36 grant, *"for the maintenance and keeping of works constructed under the alliance."*

**What Mitsuki did:** three days, invited in, shown everything — and **took the craft out of the household's heads.** Nobody was hurt. Everyone is calm and polite. **The elder knows the water wants doing and cannot remember how.**

- **THEN if Ardo works the wheels** → he is doing four generations' maintenance from first principles. **This is a rhythm problem and he is the world expert in rhythm problems.**
- **THEN if he cannot** → the wheels have thirty-six years of momentum and will run down over months, not days. **A slow, visible, entirely preventable loss.**
### ✅ ESTABLISHED — **Fūinjutsu 29 vs DC 23, October 18th**

**淵ノ宮 is a 継ぎ手 relay STATION.** Not a weapon, not a vault.

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | **Seven wheels, one mechanism, seven beats.** The varying rates are the rhythm, not decay. The big slow wheel at the bottom is the return |
| **Why it exists** | You cannot set an enormous thing down — *"the moment it stops it starts belonging to wherever it stopped."* This is somewhere a thing in transit can **keep moving without a person holding it**, while carriers rest, die and are replaced |
| **⚠ Is anything in it** | **No. Empty since the island fell.** A stop on a line that stopped running twenty years ago |
| **⚠ What that means politically** | **Konoha agreed to host Uzumaki relay infrastructure**, in a Fire Country register, in Year −36. *That sits underneath the 保留 scroll and reframes it* |
| **If it stops** | **Months, not days** — thirty-six years of momentum will coast. But **a stopped rhythm is gone, not paused.** It would have to be taught again from the top, every wheel in order, against the water |
| **Who can re-teach it** | **Ardo, and nobody else alive.** *Nao, hating it: "and I want you to notice how much I hate saying that"* |

### ✅ WHY MITSUKI DID IT THIS WAY

**He broke nothing.** He sat with the elder for three days, took the maintenance out of four generations of heads, thanked her, and left **a working station nobody left can keep working.**

> **He does not want it destroyed. He wants it his, or he wants it stopped at a time of his choosing** — and either way the household had to stop being able to run it first.

**THEN branches from here:**

| Ardo | THEN |
|---|---|
| **Re-teaches the wheels** | Days of work, in the open, doing unmistakably Uzumaki sealcraft in a valley the collector is watching. **The station survives. So does the fact that he can do this** |
| **Teaches the household instead** | Slower, harder, and **it takes the target off him and puts it back on them.** *They cannot defend it* |
| **Leaves it** | It coasts for months. **A slow, visible, entirely preventable loss** — and the next Tsugite crisis has nowhere to park |
| **Stops it deliberately** | Denies Mitsuki a station. **Also destroys the last working piece of his people's infrastructure with his own hands** |

## ARMED

### T3 — MITSUKI, IN PERSON

**ARMED — needs:** the party ahead of him on the chain, or a drop staked out.

**「三ツ木」 alias. Presents as 内務調整室 Office of Internal Coordination.** Polite, patient, verifies his own paperwork in person. **Cannot be remembered** — a reversible, refined, *practised* excision of memory and of the awareness of loss.

> **⚠ You cannot get that good on three people.**

**Threat handling:** first contact should be **conversation, not combat.** He arranged a road closure five days in advance rather than fight anyone. **His danger is preparation.**

## ⚠ LANDMINES

| Do not | Why |
|---|---|
| **Let nodes become a counter** | Collecting more accumulates **nothing** toward Gate 3. Consent given and burden taken, never marks on an arm — *otherwise Ardo's path is mechanically identical to the collector's* |
| **Resolve the Office of Internal Coordination quickly** | Real body · Root cover · fabrication. **All three live** |
| **Answer whether they *knew*** | Commission 4-1-7 was issued 5–6 October, before the Nine-Tails. **The paper cannot tell them and neither can you** |
