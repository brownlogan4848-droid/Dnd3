### PART ONE — CHAKRA FUNDAMENTALS

#### What Chakra Is
Chakra is produced by combining two components:

| Component | Source | Depleted by |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical energy (身体エネルギー)** | The body's cells; built by training, conditioning, nutrition | Physical exertion |
| **Spiritual energy (精神エネルギー)** | The mind; built by experience, meditation, study | Mental strain, concentration |

Molding chakra means combining these in a ratio appropriate to the technique. Getting the ratio wrong produces a failed or degraded jutsu — this is why an exhausted shinobi's techniques come out weak and malformed rather than simply not working.

#### The Chakra Pathway System
A network analogous to the circulatory system, running throughout the body, carrying chakra to **361 tenketsu** (chakra points) — the nodes where chakra can be emitted or blocked.

**Implications:**
- **Gentle Fist** (Hyūga) targets tenketsu directly, closing them to shut down a target's chakra flow. Damage to the pathway system is far harder to heal than damage to flesh.
- **Chakra scalpels** and **Body Pathway Derangement** attack the system without external wounds.
- The **Rasenshuriken** severs chakra pathways at a cellular level — damage no medical ninjutsu can repair.

#### Chakra Control
The efficiency with which a shinobi converts chakra into effect. **Reserves and control are inversely correlated in practice**, because someone with vast reserves never has to be precise.

| Profile | Example | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Huge reserves, poor control | Naruto (early) | Wastes chakra; can't do delicate work; can afford to fail |
| Modest reserves, superb control | Sakura | Every point counts; excels at medical ninjutsu and precision |
| Both | Kage-tier | The actual definition of elite |

**Training exercises, in order of difficulty:**
1. **Leaf concentration** — hold a leaf to the forehead with chakra. Academy level.
2. **Tree climbing** — walk up a vertical surface with a *constant* output. Genin.
3. **Water walking** — walk on water with a *constantly adjusting* output. Chūnin.
4. **Waterfall climbing / underwater work** — advanced.
5. **Medical ninjutsu** — the highest common bar; healing a live fish is the standard drill.

#### Chakra Exhaustion
Depleting reserves causes progressive symptoms: fatigue, blurred vision, failed hand seals, collapse, and — at total depletion — **death**, since chakra is bound to life force. A shinobi who drains themselves fully does not survive it. This is the cost mechanism behind most kinjutsu.

#### Sensing
**Sensor-type shinobi** perceive chakra signatures at range, identifying individuals, numbers, and sometimes emotional state. Range varies from a few hundred metres to national scale (Karin, Ao, and the Kumo sensor corps at the high end; **Nagato's rain network** covers an entire country).

Sensors are the most strategically valuable non-combat asset in a war and should be the *first* target in any ambush.

### PART TWO — THE FOUR DISCIPLINES

#### Ninjutsu (忍術)
Techniques that manipulate chakra to produce an external effect. The broadest category. Includes:
- **Elemental ninjutsu** (nature transformation)
- **Space–Time ninjutsu** (Flying Thunder God, Kamui, Summoning)
- **Shape-manipulation ninjutsu** (Rasengan)
- **Clone techniques**
- **Barrier and sealing ninjutsu** (see Fūinjutsu)
- **Medical ninjutsu** (see below)

#### Genjutsu (幻術)
Techniques that disrupt the target's **chakra flow in the brain**, altering their sensory perception. The victim experiences things that are not happening.

**Key mechanics:**
- Requires a **delivery vector** — eye contact, sound, touch, or a chakra-carrying medium. Sharingan users deliver through the eyes; sound-based genjutsu bypasses visual defenses entirely.
- **Genjutsu does no direct physical harm** — but a target who believes they are drowning may still stop breathing, and a target frozen in illusion can be killed conventionally.
- **Release (Kai)** — the victim disrupts their own chakra flow with a surge. Requires recognizing you're in a genjutsu, which is the hard part.
- **External disruption** — an ally causing pain or channeling chakra into the victim breaks it. This is the standard field solution.
- **High chakra control is the defense.** Sakura, Kurenai, and Itachi all excel here.

#### Taijutsu (体術)
Hand-to-hand combat. Requires no chakra in its basic form, which makes it:
- **Immune to chakra sealing and suppression**
- **Unblockable by chakra-detection**
- **Instantly available with no seals**

Advanced taijutsu (**Eight Gates**, **Gentle Fist**, **Chakra Enhanced Strength**) does use chakra, but as an amplifier rather than a projectile. A taijutsu specialist is the correct counter to a genjutsu specialist and a nightmare for a slow ninjutsu user.

#### Fūinjutsu (封印術) — Sealing
Techniques that seal objects, creatures, chakra, or abstractions into a container — a scroll, an object, or a living person.

**Applications:**
- **Storage sealing** — weapons, supplies, entire structures into a scroll.
- **Jinchūriki sealing** — binding a tailed beast into a human host.
- **Chakra suppression** — the Five Elements Seal, the Hyūga caged bird seal, Root's tongue seal.
- **Barriers** — the Four Violet Flames Formation, the Five Seal Barrier.
- **Reanimation and soul work** — the Dead Demon Consuming Seal, Edo Tensei.

**The Uzumaki clan** were the world's foremost sealers; Uzushiogakure was destroyed largely because of it. Konoha's alliance with the Uzumaki is why Konoha shinobi wear their spiral.

Full technique lists in the Jutsu Compendium, primarily A-rank and S-rank.

### PART THREE — NATURE TRANSFORMATION

#### The Five Basic Natures

| Nature | Kanji | Character | Signature village |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire (Katon)** | 火遁 | Aggressive, wide-area, requires breath | Konoha (Uchiha, Sarutobi) |
| **Wind (Fūton)** | 風遁 | Cutting, piercing, sharp | Suna |
| **Lightning (Raiton)** | 雷遁 | Piercing, fast, high-penetration | Kumo |
| **Earth (Doton)** | 土遁 | Defensive, terrain-shaping, mass | Iwa |
| **Water (Suiton)** | 水遁 | Adaptive, versatile, needs a source (or great skill) | Kiri |

#### The Cycle

**Fire → Wind → Lightning → Earth → Water → Fire**
Each is strong against the one it points to.

| Nature | Strong vs. | Weak vs. |
|---|---|---|
| Fire | Wind | Water |
| Wind | Lightning | Fire |
| Lightning | Earth | Wind |
| Earth | Water | Lightning |
| Water | Fire | Earth |

**Crucial caveat:** the cycle is a tiebreaker, not a law. **Sufficient chakra volume overwhelms elemental advantage.** A Kage's Fire jutsu will blow through a genin's Water jutsu regardless of the chart. Play it as ±1 step of effectiveness, not as an auto-win.

#### Affinity
Every shinobi has a **natural affinity** for one nature, determined by their chakra's inherent character and detectable with **chakra-induction paper** (litmus paper that cuts for Wind, burns for Fire, crumples for Lightning, crumbles for Earth, dampens for Water).

**Acquisition rates:**
- **One nature** — genin/chūnin standard.
- **Two natures** — jōnin standard.
- **Three or more** — elite. Kage-tier commonly have three to five.
- **All five** — extraordinarily rare (Kakashi with the Sharingan; Hiruzen "the Professor"; Rinnegan users automatically).

Learning a non-affinity nature is dramatically harder and slower than the first — a good source of downtime activity and mentor plots.

#### Yin and Yang Release

| Release | Governs | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Yin (陰遁)** | Spiritual energy, imagination | Creates **form from nothing** — genjutsu, illusion, mental constructs |
| **Yang (陽遁)** | Physical energy, vitality | Breathes **life into form** — healing, vitality, animation |
| **Yin–Yang (陰陽遁)** | Both | Creation of true life and substance. Wood Release; Truth-Seeking Balls; Creation Rebirth's underlying principle |

These sit outside the five-nature cycle and are far less commonly wielded. See the Jutsu Compendium's *Outside the Ranks* file.

### PART FOUR — KEKKEI GENKAI & ADVANCED NATURES

#### Kekkei Genkai (血継限界) — "Bloodline Limit"
An inherited ability that cannot be learned, copied by the Sharingan, or taught. Two forms:

**1. Advanced Nature Kekkei Genkai** — simultaneous combination of two basic natures, producing a new one.

| Advanced Nature | Combination | Notable users / origin |
|---|---|---|
| **Wood (Mokuton)** | Earth + Water | Hashirama Senju; Yamato (artificial) |
| **Ice (Hyōton)** | Wind + Water | Yuki clan, Kiri; Haku |
| **Lava (Yōton)** | Earth + Fire | Kumo (Darui), Iwa (Kurotsuchi), Mei Terumī |
| **Boil (Futton)** | Water + Fire | Mei Terumī; Han (Five-Tails jinchūriki) |
| **Storm (Ranton)** | Water + Lightning | Kumo; Darui |
| **Scorch (Shakuton)** | Fire + Wind | Pakura of Suna |
| **Explosion (Bakuton)** | Earth + Lightning | Iwa lineage; Deidara |
| **Magnet (Jiton)** | Earth + Wind **[INF on exact combination]** | Rasa (Gold Dust), Third Kazekage (Iron Sand), Gaara **[see note]** |
| **Crystal (Shōton)** | — **[A]** | Guren. Anime-only. |
| **Steel (Kōton)** | — | Hiruzen's Adamantine Power |
| **Swift (Jinton)** | — | Extreme speed |
| **Dark (Meiton)** | — **[A]** | Absorbs and redirects chakra |

**Note on Magnet Release:** the exact two-nature composition is not definitively stated in the manga; treat as **[INF]**. Gaara's sand control is *not* Magnet Release — it derives from Shukaku and his mother's chakra.

**2. Physiological Kekkei Genkai** — bloodline traits of the body itself.

| Kekkei Genkai | Clan | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Sharingan** | Uchiha | Dōjutsu; perception, prediction, copying, illusion |
| **Byakugan** | Hyūga (and Ōtsutsuki descent) | Dōjutsu; near-360° vision, chakra pathway sight, long range |
| **Dead Bone Pulse (Shikotsumyaku)** | Kaguya | Total skeletal manipulation; bone as weapon and armor |
| **Hydrification** | Hōzuki | Convert the body to liquid — **see conflict note below** |
| **Earth Grudge Fear** | — (Kakuzu; a technique, not strictly a bloodline) | Multiple hearts, thread-based body control |

**[CONFLICT] — Hydrification's category.** The Jutsu Compendium's D-Rank file types this as *Water Release Ninjutsu — Hōzuki clan* and assigns it a **D rank**, describing it as an inherited clan ability. That framing makes it a **hiden technique with a letter rank**, not a kekkei genkai — and by the compendium's own logic, kekkei genkai carry no rank.

Both readings have support: it is unambiguously hereditary and unlearnable by outsiders (kekkei-genkai-like), yet it is ranked and typed as elemental ninjutsu (hiden-like). **Not resolved.** For play, the practical difference is whether it can be copied by a Sharingan — treat it as hiden and therefore **not copyable**, which matches both readings' outcome.

#### Kekkei Tōta (血継淘汰) — "Bloodline Selection"
A rarer step above kekkei genkai: **three natures combined simultaneously.**

- **Dust Release (Jinton)** — Earth + Wind + Fire. Users: **Mū** (Second Tsuchikage) and **Ōnoki**. Generates an expanding three-dimensional field that disintegrates everything within it at a molecular level. Effectively unblockable by conventional defense.

#### Kekkei Mōra (血継網羅) — "Bloodline Encompassing"
The Ōtsutsuki tier. **Rinne Sharingan**, **Infinite Tsukuyomi**, **All-Killing Ash Bones**, **Yomotsu Hirasaka**. See the Jutsu Compendium's *Outside the Ranks* file. Not player-accessible in any sane campaign.

#### The Persecution Problem
In **Kirigakure during the Bloody Mist era**, kekkei genkai carriers were hunted and killed — by neighbors as often as by the state — because bloodline users had been decisive and terrifying in the wars. **Haku's** father killed his mother and attempted to kill him when her Ice Release was discovered.

**This is a superb character hook.** A kekkei genkai PC from Kiri in Era A or B is hiding something that will get them killed.

### PART FIVE — DŌJUTSU (EYE TECHNIQUES)

Summarized here for context; full ability lists are in the Jutsu Compendium's *Outside the Ranks* file, where they correctly carry **no letter rank**.

#### Sharingan (Uchiha)
Awakened by **emotional trauma**, typically the loss of someone important. Progresses through one, two, and three tomoe.
**Capabilities:** chakra perception, motion prediction bordering on precognition, technique copying, and hypnosis/genjutsu through eye contact.
**Costs:** significant chakra drain; non-Uchiha users (Kakashi) cannot deactivate it and are drained constantly.

**Mangekyō Sharingan** — awakened through the trauma of losing a loved one, in the specific sense of *causing or witnessing* that loss. Grants unique techniques per user. **Degrades eyesight with every use until blindness.**

**Eternal Mangekyō** — transplanting a close relative's Mangekyō into a user who already has their own; removes the blindness cost.

#### Byakugan (Hyūga)
Near-360-degree vision (with a small blind spot behind the upper thoracic vertebra), telescopic sight measured in kilometres, X-ray-like penetration, and — most importantly — **direct visibility of the chakra pathway system and its 361 tenketsu.** This is what makes Gentle Fist function.

#### Rinnegan
The eye of the Sage of Six Paths. Grants the **Six Paths** techniques, mastery of all five natures automatically, and access to the Outer Path. Appears in the setting via Madara → Nagato → Obito → Sasuke/Madara. **Not player-accessible.**

#### Tenseigan
Ōtsutsuki/Hyūga-lineage. **[MV]** — quarantine.

### PART SIX — SENJUTSU & SAGE MODE

#### Natural Energy
A third energy source existing in the world itself. **Senjutsu** is the discipline of drawing natural energy in and blending it with the user's physical and spiritual energy, producing **senjutsu chakra**.

#### The Requirement and the Risk
- The user must remain **perfectly still** while gathering — which is why it is nearly impossible to enter Sage Mode mid-combat without help (shadow clones gathering in advance, or a partner holding position).
- **Absorbing too much natural energy petrifies the user into stone.** This is permanent and fatal. Jiraiya's partial toad features are a mild, arrested form of this.

#### The Three Sage Locations

| Site | Species | Practitioners |
|---|---|---|
| **Mount Myōboku** | Toads | Jiraiya, Naruto, Minato **[INF, limited]** |
| **Ryūchi Cave** | Snakes | Orochimaru (attempted), Kabuto (achieved), Mitsuki lineage |
| **Shikkotsu Forest** | Slugs | Hashirama **[INF]**; least depicted of the three |

#### Sage Mode Effects
Massive amplification of strength, speed, durability, and jutsu power; **the ability to sense chakra across a wide area**; and the ability to strike targets that are intangible or chakra-based. Physical features shift toward the sage species (toad-like eyes and pigmentation for Myōboku).

**Duration is limited** and ends when the gathered natural energy is spent — a hard clock that makes Sage Mode a burst resource rather than a permanent upgrade.

### PART SEVEN — OTHER POWER SYSTEMS

#### The Eight Gates (Hachimon)
Eight limiters on the chakra pathway system that cap the body's output to protect it. A specialist can force them open in sequence, each release multiplying physical capability and damaging the body proportionally.

| # | Gate | Effect | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Opening** | Removes the brain's muscular safety limiter | Muscle tearing |
| 2 | **Healing** | Forcibly re-energizes the body | Increasing strain |
| 3 | **Life** | Massive speed and strength; skin reddens | Circulatory stress |
| 4 | **Pain** | Further amplification | Muscle fibre rupture |
| 5 | **Limit** | Further amplification | Severe internal damage |
| 6 | **View** | Enough force to displace water and air | Approaching structural failure |
| 7 | **Wonder** | Body sweats blood, which evaporates into a chakra shroud | Bones and muscle beginning to fail |
| 8 | **Death** | The heart itself is forced to output maximum chakra. Power exceeding a Kage's. | **Certain death.** The user's body is destroyed. |

Users: **Might Duy**, **Might Gai**, **Rock Lee** (up to the fifth). Duy died opening the eighth to save his son's team; Gai opened it against Madara and survived only through Naruto's intervention.

#### Curse Marks (Juinjutsu)
Applied seals that grant power at the cost of autonomy or life.

| Mark | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Cursed Seal of Heaven / Earth** | Orochimaru | Grants senjutsu-derived power in two stages; erodes the will and binds the bearer to Orochimaru. Roughly 1 in 10 survive application. |
| **Caged Bird Seal** | Hyūga Main House | Inflicts agony or death at the Main House's command; destroys the Byakugan on death to prevent theft. |
| **Root tongue seal** | Danzō | Total paralysis if the bearer speaks of Root. |
| **Cursed Tongue Eradication** | Danzō | Kills the bearer outright on violation. |

#### Jinchūriki Power
See **Book II §3**.

#### Medical Ninjutsu
A distinct discipline requiring exceptional chakra control. Standardized across the world by **Tsunade**, who instituted the rule that **every four-person squad should include a medic** and that medics must prioritize survival over heroics.

**The Three Medic Rules (Tsunade's doctrine):**
1. A medical-nin must not stop treatment until every life is saved.
2. A medical-nin must never stand on the front line.
3. A medical-nin must be the last to die on their squad.

#### Puppetry (Kugutsu)
Suna's signature discipline. The user extends chakra threads from their fingertips to manipulate puppets at range. Advanced practitioners control multiple puppets simultaneously (Chiyo: two; Sasori: one hundred). Puppets can be armed with blades, poison, flamethrowers, and sealed jutsu. **Human puppets** — made from converted corpses — retain their original chakra and techniques.

#### Kinjutsu (Forbidden Techniques)
Techniques banned by a village, for one of three reasons:
1. **They kill the user** — Dead Demon Consuming Seal, Eight Gates' final gate, Twin Snakes Mutual Death.
2. **They violate ethical limits** — Impure World Reincarnation, Living Corpse Reincarnation, human experimentation.
3. **They cause harm disproportionate to any legitimate use** — Multiple Shadow Clone Technique is forbidden because it can kill a user who splits their chakra too far.

Being forbidden does not mean unknown. Kinjutsu are catalogued, sealed, and guarded — the **Scroll of Seals** in Konoha holds the village's most dangerous, and stealing it is a capital offense.

### PART ONE — KONOHA CLANS

---

#### Uchiha (うちは)

**Symbol:** The uchiwa fan (red-over-white).
**Kekkei Genkai:** Sharingan.
**Affinity:** Fire. Uchiha traditionally perform the **Great Fireball Technique** as a rite of passage — a child who can produce it is considered an adult.
**Location:** Walled district at Konoha's southeastern edge.

##### Structure
A clan head (**Fugaku Uchiha** in Era A/B) with a council of elders. Meetings are held at the **Naka Shrine**, beneath whose seventh tatami mat lies the **Uchiha Stone Tablet** — a text written by the Sage of Six Paths, legible in progressively greater depth depending on the reader's eye (Sharingan, Mangekyō, Rinnegan). Black Zetsu altered it long ago to steer the clan toward Kaguya's revival. **Nobody in either era knows this.**

##### The Curse of Hatred
Uchiha love more intensely than most, and when that love is severed, the resulting emotional surge awakens and escalates the Sharingan. The clan's power is literally fuelled by loss — which structures the entire tragedy.

##### Era Status
- **Era A:** Intact and formidable. Staffing the Military Police Force. Politically marginalized and aware of it. Fugaku is clan head; Mikoto is his wife; **Itachi is born near the war's end** and **Sasuke** in the interbellum. **Obito** is a low-ranking clan member on Team Minato, treated as an underachiever.
- **Era B:** Coup planning → the massacre (Year +7). Extinct except Itachi, Sasuke, and two survivors nobody knows about.
- **Era C:** Sasuke is the only publicly known living Uchiha. Obito and Madara both appear.

##### Notable Members by Era
**Era A:** Fugaku, Mikoto, Obito, Kagami (earlier generation), Setsuna, Teyaki, Uruchi, Yashiro, Inabi, Tekka.
**Era B/C:** Itachi, Sasuke, Shisui (until Year +7), Obito (hidden), Madara (hidden).

---

#### Hyūga (日向)

**Symbol:** A flame-like tomoe.
**Kekkei Genkai:** Byakugan.
**Fighting style:** **Gentle Fist (Jūken)** — strikes that inject chakra directly into the target's pathway system, damaging organs and sealing tenketsu without needing external force.

##### Structure — The Two Houses
The defining feature of the clan and its central injustice.

| | **Main House (Sōke)** | **Branch House (Bunke)** |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Leadership, inheritance | Protection of the Main House |
| Seal | None | **Caged Bird Seal** on the forehead |
| Techniques | Full hiden, including the Main-House-only arts | Restricted |

The **Caged Bird Seal** allows any Main House member to inflict crippling agony or death on a Branch member at will, and **destroys the Byakugan on the bearer's death** so it cannot be harvested by enemies. The clan justifies it as necessary bloodline security. It is also, unmistakably, a system of hereditary servitude.

**Twins are divided by minutes of birth order.** **Hiashi** (Main) and **Hizashi** (Branch) are twins; Hizashi died in Hiashi's place after the Hyūga Affair, exploiting the seal's convenient property of destroying the eye — Kumo got a corpse with nothing to take.

##### Era Status
- **Era A:** Hiashi and Hizashi are young. The clan is stable, conservative, and internally strained.
- **Era B:** The Hyūga Affair (Hinata's attempted abduction, Hizashi's death). Neji grows up hating the Main House.
- **Era C:** Hiashi, Hanabi, Hinata, and Neji all serve. Byakugan users are distributed across the divisions as living sensors — the Allied Forces' primary counter to White Zetsu infiltration.

---

#### Nara (奈良)

**Hiden:** **Shadow manipulation** — binding a shadow to the user's own and forcing the target to mirror their movements. Extends to shadow-based strangulation, sewing, and projectile techniques.
See Jutsu Compendium: *Shadow Imitation Technique* (C-rank, **[CONFLICT]** flagged), *Shadow Clutch* (A-rank), and *Shadow Sewing* / *Shadow Gathering* in the Unranked file.

**Character:** Extraordinary strategic intelligence paired with pathological laziness. Nara men are notoriously henpecked and notoriously the smartest people in any room.
**Secondary specialty:** The clan maintains a **deer forest** and produces rare medicines — a genuine economic asset and a plot hook (poison antidotes are Nara-supplied).

**Era A:** **Shikaku Nara** is a young jōnin, later Konoha's jōnin commander.
**Era C:** Shikaku is **chief strategist at Allied HQ** and dies in its destruction, transmitting final orders. **Shikamaru** inherits command.

---

#### Yamanaka (山中)

**Hiden:** **Mind-body techniques.** The user projects their consciousness into a target, taking over their body (Mind Body Switch Technique, C-rank), reading their memories, or transmitting thoughts. Foundation of Konoha's interrogation capability and its long-range communications.
**Weakness:** While projecting, the user's own body is defenseless, and a missed target means the mind travels on until it returns.

**Era A/B:** **Inoichi Yamanaka** is the premier mind-walker in the Torture and Interrogation Force.
**Era C:** Inoichi runs the **Allied mind-link communication network** from HQ — the single most important logistical function in the war. He dies with Shikaku. **Ino** serves as a field relay and takes over the network.

---

#### Akimichi (秋道)

**Hiden:** **Body expansion.** Converting caloric energy into chakra to enlarge body parts or the whole body to enormous scale. The clan's size is a *combat asset*, not a joke — and calling an Akimichi fat is the fastest way to start a fight you will lose.
**Three Coloured Pills** (spinach, curry, chilli) — a kinjutsu escalation granting massive power, with the red pill converting all the user's fat and, historically, killing them. Chōza's generation regarded taking the red pill as a death sentence.

**Era A/B:** **Chōza Akimichi**, clan head.
**Era C:** Chōza and Chōji both serve; Chōji's arc turns on whether he can act decisively under command.

#### The Ino-Shika-Chō Formation
A traditional three-clan combat unit reconstituted every generation:
**Yamanaka (Ino) + Nara (Shika) + Akimichi (Chō)**
- Nara immobilizes with shadow.
- Yamanaka possesses or disables.
- Akimichi delivers overwhelming force.

The formation is honored to the point of ritual — the fathers served together and expect their children to. Deploy it as a set piece.

---

#### Inuzuka (犬塚)

**Hiden:** **Combined man-and-beast techniques.** Each Inuzuka is partnered with a **ninken** (ninja hound) raised alongside them from childhood.

**The technique chain** (ranks per the Jutsu Compendium):
1. **Four Legs Technique** (D) — the mandatory foundation. Floods the body with chakra for beast-like claws, fangs, senses, and a quadrupedal stance. *Everything else requires this active.*
2. **Beast Human Clone** (D) — the ninken transforms into a fully corporeal, independently intelligent duplicate of its handler.
3. **Passing Fang** (D) — a solo spinning drill charge. Straight-line only; no mid-flight correction.
4. **Fang Passing Fang** (C) — the paired version, handler and ninken drilling together.
5. **Fang Wolf Fang** and the higher forms — later-tier escalations.

**Physical traits:** Enhanced smell, hearing, and night vision; slit pupils; distinctive red fang markings on the cheeks.
**Culture:** Pack-structured, blunt, loyal. The dog is family, not equipment.

**Era A/B/C:** **Tsume Inuzuka** (clan head, partner Kuromaru), **Hana**, **Kiba** (partner Akamaru).

---

#### Aburame (油女)

**Hiden:** **Kikaichū** — destruction bugs. At birth, an Aburame's body is offered as a nest to a colony of chakra-eating insects, which live inside them for life. In exchange the insects obey.
**Capabilities:** Drain a target's chakra on contact; track by scent markers left on a target; form swarms for reconnaissance, containment, or attack; serve as an early-warning sensor net.
**Character:** Reserved, precise, high-collared, sunglasses. Deeply logical. Often underestimated — an Aburame who has touched you has already won.
**Weakness:** Insecticide, fire, wind dispersal, and enclosed environments.

**Era A/B/C:** **Shibi Aburame** (clan head), **Shino**, **Torune** (Root; a rarer variant colony — nano-sized poisonous insects).

---

#### Sarutobi (猿飛)

Not a kekkei genkai clan — a distinguished lineage with a strong Fire affinity and ties to the **monkey summoning contract** (**Enma**, the Monkey King, who transforms into an indestructible adamantine staff).

**Era A:** **Hiruzen Sarutobi** is Third Hokage. **Biwako** is his wife (killed at Naruto's birth in Year 0). **Asuma** is a child.
**Era B:** Asuma serves with the **Twelve Guardian Ninja**, returns, and leads Team 10. Killed by Hidan and Kakuzu.

#### Senju (千手)

Three things from the official chart are worth restating here because they change how you *play* a clan character:

**1. Rarity is difficulty, not power.** Common (★☆☆☆☆) clans train easily and unlock early. Legendary (★★★★☆) clans are *"locked behind major story arcs and life-changing events."* A Nara PC is fully functional at level 1. A Senju PC is a promise the campaign has to keep.

# §3 — TAILED BEASTS & JINCHŪRIKI

The nine, their hosts across both eras, how sealing works, and what being a jinchūriki actually costs.

### PART ONE — WHAT THE TAILED BEASTS ARE

#### Origin
The nine beasts were created by **Hagoromo Ōtsutsuki**, the Sage of Six Paths, by dividing the chakra of the **Ten-Tails** — the beast form of the God Tree, itself a manifestation of Kaguya Ōtsutsuki's power. He gave each a name and set them free, intending that they would one day come together again under someone who understood them.

**Almost nobody in either era knows this.** To the shinobi world, the tailed beasts are simply enormous chakra monsters — natural disasters that can be captured and weaponized.

#### The Political Function
At the founding-era Kage Summit, **Hashirama Senju** distributed the beasts he had captured among the villages to create a **balance of power**. This is the origin of the jinchūriki system: every great village holds one or two, and the deterrence is mutual.

The system fails in exactly the way you would expect. Every village treats its jinchūriki as a weapon; most treat them as monsters; and Akatsuki eventually collects seven of the nine.

#### Beast Consciousness
The beasts are **individuals** — named, intelligent, with distinct personalities, grievances, and long memories of being used. Their default disposition toward humans is hatred, earned across centuries of being sealed, split, transferred, and deployed.

### PART THREE — THE JINCHŪRIKI CONDITION

#### Sealing
A tailed beast is sealed into a **living human host** — the container must be alive, and the seal is typically applied in infancy, because an adult body cannot easily adapt. Sealing techniques are **fūinjutsu of the highest order**, which is why the Uzumaki were so central to the practice.

**Key constraint:** removing a tailed beast from its host **kills the host**, with the sole known exceptions being an Uzumaki's life force (Kushina survived extraction briefly) and Naruto's later circumstances. This is why Akatsuki's collection campaign is a campaign of murders.

#### The Social Cost
This is the part that matters at the table.

Jinchūriki are, almost universally:
- **Feared.** Their villages know what they contain.
- **Isolated.** Deliberately, often by policy.
- **Used.** Deployed as weapons of last resort, then put away.

**Gaara** was an assassination target for his own village. **Naruto** was shunned by adults who would not explain why. **Yugito** was sealed as a child. **Utakata** and **Rōshi** both left their villages. **Killer B** is the exception, and it is *because Kumo treated him as a person* — which is the argument the setting is making.

#### Control Stages **[Mechanical framing GM]**

| Stage | Description | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **0 — Sealed** | No conscious access | Occasional chakra leakage under stress; enhanced healing and reserves |
| **1 — Cloak (1 tail)** | Beast chakra shrouds the host | Major boosts; the host's judgment degrades |
| **2–3 tails** | Deeper draw | Body damage from the beast's corrosive chakra; the host may attack allies |
| **4+ tails** | Partial transformation | Host consciousness largely displaced; a genuine danger to everyone present |
| **Full transformation** | Complete beast form | Host is not in control unless the relationship is cooperative |
| **Partnership** | Host and beast cooperate | Full power, full control, no degradation. **B and, later, Naruto.** |

**The critical distinction:** stages 1–4 are the host *taking* power. Partnership is the beast *giving* it. Everything between is self-destructive.

#### Suppression Methods
- **The Fire Sealing Method / suppression seals** applied by a village's specialists.
- **Chakra-suppressing restraints** and prison seals.
- **A dedicated handler** — Konoha assigned ANBU to Naruto; Kumo assigned Motoi to B.
- **A physical anchor** — Gaara's sand gourd doubles as containment.
- The **Uzumaki chakra chains** — Kushina could physically restrain Kurama with them.

### PART FIVE — JINCHŪRIKI PLAYER CHARACTERS **[GM]**

> ## ★ AUTHORITATIVE. Book IV §1 Part Seven delegates all jinchūriki mechanics to this section.
>
> The baseline package, the cloak table, the tail thresholds, and the partnership arc are defined **here and only here**. Book IV carries headlines and a pointer. **A live PC currently uses these rules** — see `characters/ardo.md`.

A jinchūriki PC is one of the strongest character concepts available and one of the easiest to run badly.

#### Baseline Package (Level 1)
- **Chakra pool doubled.** The headline benefit, and it is enormous.
- **Regenerate 5 Health at the start of each combat round.**
- **Advantage on saves against poison and disease.**
- **Sensing:** you feel malicious intent directed at you within 60 ft.
- **Social penalty:** disadvantage on Persuasion with anyone who knows what you are. Most villages know.

#### The Beast as a Character
**Run the tailed beast as an NPC the player negotiates with.** It should have opinions, resentments, and leverage. It should offer power at bad moments and withhold it at worse ones.

#### Tailed Beast Cloak

**The cloak costs Special, not Chakra.** That is deliberate — the beast's power is something you *earn access to* over the course of a fight, exactly like a Signature Technique (**Book IV §2, Part Four**).

| Tails | Special cost | Benefit | Cost when it ends |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 40 | +2 to attack and damage; speed ×2; resistance to physical damage | 25% max Stamina |
| **2** | 60 | As above at +4; chakra arms (reach 30 ft); **+1 combo slot** | 50% max Stamina; **CHA save DC 14 each round or attack the nearest creature** |
| **3** | — | **Not a player choice.** Triggered by failing the DC 14 save above. | 3d10 damage; save DC rises to 17 |
| **4+** | — | **The DM takes control of the character.** | 4d10 damage. Allies are in genuine danger. |

**Rule: a player cannot voluntarily go past two tails.** Three and beyond happen when the *player fails a save*, not when they choose to. That distinction is the entire jinchūriki experience.

> **[RESOLVED — was a live contradiction.]** Book IV §1 Part Seven previously said the DM takes the character at *"three or more tails."* **This table is correct and Book IV has been corrected to defer to it:** at **three tails** the character is still the player's — they take 3d10 and the save DC rises to 17. Control passes to the DM at **four**. The three-tail stage exists so a player *feels the slide* for a round before losing the wheel, and deleting it removes the best beat the subsystem has.

#### Adult sealing, and hosts who were not sealed as infants **[GM — added Year 0]**

Part Three notes that sealing is *"typically applied in infancy, because an adult body cannot easily adapt."* When it is not — an emergency battlefield seal into a grown host — apply the baseline package above with three changes:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Adaptation period** | The doubled Chakra pool arrives over **weeks, not instantly.** Until it settles, treat the host as carrying their normal maximum plus an unstable surplus the GM may rule unavailable at bad moments |
| **The beast is awake and knows exactly what happened** | An infant host's beast has decades to sulk. An adult host's beast starts the relationship **mid-argument**, with a grievance about the specific night in question |
| **Stage 0 leakage is louder** | Occasional chakra leakage under stress is not occasional. Any strong emotional spike is a GM prompt |

**Uzumaki hosts.** The clan's life force is the reason Kushina survived what she survived, and it is the reason an Uzumaki adult can take a seal that would kill anyone else. It does not make the host safe — it makes them *possible*.

#### The Partnership Arc
The point of a jinchūriki campaign is earning **stage 6 — partnership**. Structure it as a relationship, not a power-up:
1. The beast is hostile and the PC takes its power by force.
2. The PC learns why it hates them.
3. The PC does something for the beast that costs them.
4. The beast offers its name.
5. Partnership: full power, no exhaustion, no saves. The beast fights *with* them.

**Naruto's version takes the entire series.** Don't rush it.

### PART ONE — SUMMONING

#### How Contracts Work
A shinobi signs a **blood contract** with a species, writing their name in their own blood on a scroll that has been signed by every previous contractor. Thereafter they can summon members of that species by marking their hand with blood and performing the **Summoning Technique** (C-rank).

**Rules of the practice:**
- **The contract is generally exclusive** — one species per shinobi, for life. Exceptions exist but are notable.
- **Scale follows chakra.** A modest expenditure brings a small scout; a full commitment brings a boss creature the size of a mountain.
- **Summons are people, not equipment.** They have names, opinions, and the right to refuse. Toads argue; ninken sulk; slugs are polite about it.
- **Reverse summoning** — a summon can pull the contractor *to them*, which is how Sage Mode training at Mount Myōboku works.

#### Escape Kit **[GM]**

Because **there is no universal way to break out of a combo** (**Book IV §2, Part Six**), these are survival gear rather than accessories. Every squad should carry some.

| Item | Effect | Cost (ryō) |
|---|---|---|
| **Flash bomb** | **BREAK** — attacker CON save DC 13 or blinded, combo ends | 400 |
| **Smoke bomb** | **BREAK** if the attacker relies on sight (DC 13 Perception to continue) | 300 |
| **Makibishi (caltrops)** | **EVADE** — attacker stops or takes 1d6 and loses their next action | 200 |
| **Tagged kunai** | **EVADE** — thrown as a Reaction; attacker breaks off or eats 3d6 | 600 |
| **Tripwire (pre-set)** | **BREAK** — prepared terrain only | 200 |
| **Escape tag** | **DISPLACE** 30 ft, no line of sight required — a manufactured Kawarimi | 1,500 |
| **Blinding powder** | **EVADE** — attacker takes −4 on their next action | 250 |
| **Chakra-shroud cloak** | **EVADE** — negate one action per combat. **Recharges on a long rest, and holds nine charges total before the weave is spent** and it must be re-inked by a sealer for **2,000** | 8,000 |

**These are consumables by design.** Spending them and re-buying them is what turns D-rank mission pay into a meaningful reward — **which only works if the squad is being paid its share and not the whole contract.** See **Book I §4 → The Squad Share.**

> **⚠ The cloak was the one non-consumable in a consumables list.** *"Negate one action per combat, rechargeable"* with no limit is a permanent, free Evade every fight forever, in a system whose stated design is that **there is no universal way to break out of a combo.** It now has nine charges and a re-inking cost, which keeps it aspirational without making it a solved problem.

**Soldier pill warning:** these are the setting's version of amphetamines and the drawback should bite. A squad that pill-stacks through a mission should be wrecked afterward.

### PART FIVE — POISONS **[GM]**

Suna's specialty and the most under-used threat in most campaigns. Chiyo and Sasori built their reputations on it.

| Poison | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Paralytic (Suna standard)** | DC 15 Con save or paralyzed 1 minute; repeat save at end of each turn |
| **Sasori's compound** | DC 17 Con save; on failure, paralysis within 3 rounds and death in 3 days without the specific antidote. **Only Suna produces the antidote.** |
| **Necrotic contact poison** | 2d6 necrotic per round for 4 rounds, DC 14 Con save each round to end |
| **Ibuse's mist (Hanzō)** | 30-ft cloud; DC 18 Con save or incapacitated 1 hour. Area effect. Army-scale. |
| **Sedative senbon** | Non-lethal; DC 13 Con save or unconscious 10 minutes |

**Design note:** poison makes a fight *scary* rather than merely long, because the clock keeps running after the enemy is dead. Use it to make Suna feel different from every other village.
