### PART ONE — KONOHAGAKURE

---

#### Command

##### Hiruzen Sarutobi
**Japanese:** Sarutobi Hiruzen (猿飛ヒルゼン)
**Rank:** Third Hokage · **Age:** ~50s **[INF]**
**Abilities:** All five natures; the Adamantine Staff (Enma); an enormous catalogue of techniques earning him the title **"The Professor"** — reputedly the only shinobi to have mastered every technique in Konoha.
**Role:** Directing a war he did not want and cannot end quickly. Balancing the Uchiha, Danzō, and the clan council while a generation dies at the border.
**Voice:** Grandfatherly, deliberate, pipe in hand. Speaks in principles and lets the listener draw the conclusion. Genuinely kind and genuinely willing to authorize terrible things. His great flaw is that he defers — to Danzō, to the council, to the hope that a problem will resolve itself.
**Does not know:** That Obito survived. That Madara is alive. That Danzō is running unsanctioned operations at scale.

##### Danzō Shimura
**Japanese:** Shimura Danzō (志村ダンゾウ)
**Rank:** Root Commander, Council figure · **Age:** ~50s
**Abilities:** Wind and Fire natures; **Vacuum Blade**; later, transplanted Sharingan and Hashirama cells (**not yet in Era A** — his arm is acquired during/after the interbellum, so keep him conventional here). **[INF] on precise timing.**
**Role:** Building Root into a private army. Manipulating Hanzō into destroying Yahiko's Akatsuki. Conducting operations Hiruzen has explicitly forbidden.
**Voice:** Clipped, cold, entirely certain. Frames atrocity as arithmetic — *"How many die if we don't?"* Never raises his voice. Believes Hiruzen's compassion is a slow suicide the village is committing.
**Does not know:** That Obito exists. That the man he thinks he's manipulating in Ame is being manipulated in turn.

##### Homura Mitokado & Koharu Utatane
Hiruzen's former teammates and permanent advisors. Conservative, security-focused, and instrumental in every hard decision. **Voice:** They finish each other's arguments and speak in the plural — *"The village cannot afford…"* Treat them as one political organ with two mouths.

#### The Legendary Sannin

##### Jiraiya
**Japanese:** Jiraiya (自来也)
**Rank:** Jōnin / Sannin · **Age:** ~30s
**Abilities:** Toad summoning (Mount Myōboku); **Sage Mode** (imperfect — partial toad features); **Rasengan** (his invention, taught to Minato); Fire and Earth; extensive fūinjutsu; a global spy network.
**Role in Era A:** Splitting his time between the front, his intelligence network, and Amegakure, where he trained three war orphans. He has already taught **Yahiko, Nagato, and Konan** and left them.
**Voice:** Loud, theatrical, self-mythologizing — the "Toad Sage" entrance is a bit and he knows it. Underneath: genuinely observant, genuinely sad, and the best judge of character in Konoha. He deflects with lechery when a conversation gets too close to something real.
**Does not know:** What became of the Ame orphans. That Nagato has the Rinnegan for a reason.

##### Tsunade Senju
**Japanese:** Senju Tsunade (千手綱手)
**Rank:** Jōnin / Sannin · **Age:** ~30s
**Abilities:** **Chakra Enhanced Strength**; **Creation Rebirth** and the Yin Seal; slug summoning (Katsuyu); the world's foremost medical ninjutsu, which she is in the process of institutionalizing.
**Role in Era A:** Serving as a field medic and instituting the doctrine that every squad carries a medic — a reform that saves untold lives. Then **Dan Katō dies in her arms**, and she leaves. Her hemophobia — a paralytic terror of blood — dates from this, and she abandons the village for gambling and drink.
**Voice:** Blunt, profane, impatient. Barks orders in a medical crisis and is unanswerable when she does. After Dan: bitter, dismissive of anyone who talks about dreams or the Will of Fire, and *daring* you to argue with her.
**Do not have her present after the war's midpoint** unless your campaign explicitly retains her.

##### Orochimaru
**Japanese:** Orochimaru (大蛇丸)
**Rank:** Jōnin / Sannin · **Age:** ~30s
**Abilities:** Snake summoning; **Living Corpse Reincarnation** (body transfer); **Eight Branches Technique**; **Impure World Reincarnation** (perfected from Tobirama's incomplete work); vast ninjutsu breadth.
**Role in Era A:** A loyal and celebrated Konoha jōnin — and, privately, already experimenting on human subjects. He is the front-runner for Fourth Hokage and **is passed over for Minato**, which he never forgives. His experiments include the **sixty Wood Release test subjects**, of whom only **Tenzō** survives.
**Voice:** Silken, unhurried, faintly amused. Turns every conversation into an evaluation of the speaker's usefulness. He is *charming* in Era A — that's what makes him dangerous. He has not yet become the snake-man; he is a respected colleague whose lab nobody visits.
**Does not know (yet):** He is being watched. His discovery and flight belong to the interbellum.

#### Team Minato

##### Minato Namikaze
**Japanese:** Namikaze Minato (波風ミナト)
**Rank:** Jōnin → **Fourth Hokage** (late war) · **Age:** ~24 **[INF]**
**Abilities:** **Flying Thunder God Technique** (his refinement of Tobirama's); **Rasengan** (his completion of Jiraiya's concept); Wind and Lightning; genius-tier fūinjutsu; the fastest shinobi alive.
**Reputation:** **"Konoha's Yellow Flash."** Iwagakure has a standing **flee-on-sight order** against him — the only one of its kind.
**Role:** Holding the Iwa front while his genin team executes the Kannabi Bridge mission. Later, Hokage.
**Voice:** Warm, level, quietly funny. Speaks to his students as people rather than assets. Never postures — he doesn't need to. In command he's decisive and economical; in private with Kushina he's completely outmatched and enjoys it.
**Does not know:** That the masked man who will come for his son exists.

##### Kakashi Hatake
**Japanese:** Hatake Kakashi (はたけカカシ)
**Rank:** Jōnin at **13** · **Age:** 12–13 at Kannabi Bridge
**Abilities:** Lightning affinity; **Chidori** (his own invention, and initially a flawed one — it commits him to a straight-line charge he cannot see out of); the White Light Chakra Sabre; ninken summoning. **Post-Kannabi: Obito's Sharingan in his left eye.**
**Role:** The prodigy son of a disgraced father, promoted to jōnin as a child and given command of teammates his own age.
**Voice — before Kannabi:** Rigid, humorless, rule-quoting. *"Those who break the rules are trash."* He is unbearable, and that's correct — he is a traumatized twelve-year-old enforcing the code that destroyed his father, because if the code is right then his father was wrong and the world makes sense.
**Voice — after Kannabi:** Quieter. Late to everything. Reads in public and deflects with laziness. The transformation is not instantaneous — play the seam.
**Does not know:** That Obito is alive. This defines him for twenty years.

##### Obito Uchiha
**Japanese:** Uchiha Obito (うちはオビト)
**Rank:** Chūnin · **Age:** 13
**Abilities:** Awakens the **Sharingan** during the Kannabi Bridge mission. Fire affinity. Otherwise unremarkable at this stage — he is genuinely behind his peers.
**Role:** The clan's underachiever. Chronically late, loud, and openly in love with Rin, who is openly in love with Kakashi. Dreams of becoming Hokage.
**Voice:** Brash, whiny, warm. Argues with Kakashi constantly and loses. His defining line — *"those who abandon their comrades are worse than trash"* — is delivered not as wisdom but as a furious accusation.
**After the cave-in:** Play him as a separate character entirely. See Book III §4; nobody may know he lives.

##### Rin Nohara
**Japanese:** Nohara Rin (のはらリン)
**Rank:** Chūnin · **Age:** 13
**Abilities:** Medical ninjutsu — the team's medic, and competent enough to perform the **Sharingan transplant in a collapsing cave**.
**Role:** The team's connective tissue. Loyal to Kakashi, kind to Obito, and the reason the Kannabi mission goes the way it does.
**Voice:** Gentle, practical, quietly firm. She is the only one who talks to both boys as though they're being ridiculous, because they are.
**Her death** is covered in Book I §1. Handle with care: she chooses it, and Kakashi does not.

### PART TWO — IWAGAKURE

Konoha's principal enemy. Iwa shinobi in Era A should be competent professionals with a real grievance, not mooks.

##### Ōnoki
**Japanese:** Ōnoki (オオノキ) · **Rank:** Third Tsuchikage
**Abilities:** **Dust Release (kekkei tōta)** — Earth + Wind + Fire, producing an expanding disintegration field; **Lightened Boulder / Added-Weight Rock** techniques allowing flight and weight manipulation. **[CONFLICT — Added-Weight Rock's rank is flagged in the Jutsu Compendium.]**
**Voice:** Cantankerous, short (physically and temperamentally), constantly complaining about his back. Beneath it: ruthless, grudge-holding, and capable of ordering anything. He calls people "brat" regardless of their age.
**Era A role:** Prosecuting a war he intends to win, and watching Minato dismantle his forces.

##### Mū — **Deceased** (Second Tsuchikage)
"Non-Person." Dust Release; complete presence erasure — undetectable even to sensors. Ōnoki's teacher. Relevant in Era A only as legend; he returns via Edo Tensei in Era C.

The Iwa unit opposing Team Minato. Use them as a full-featured enemy squad.
- **Kakkō** — Jōnin commander. Earth Release; a lethal, methodical veteran.
- **Taiseki** — **Hiding with Camouflage Technique** (A-rank); functionally invisible. Kills the unsuspecting.

### PART THREE — SUNAGAKURE

##### Chiyo
**Japanese:** Chiyo (チヨ) · **Rank:** Elder, Puppet Master
**Abilities:** **White Secret Technique: Chikamatsu Collection of Ten Puppets** (S-rank); master poisoner; the **One's Own Life Reincarnation** technique (S-rank kinjutsu) that trades her life for another's.
**Role:** Suna's most feared field asset and one of its two ruling elders. Her poisons are responsible for a substantial share of Konoha's Third War casualties. **She personally sealed the Three-Tails into a host at one point** — Suna has jinchūriki history too.
**Voice:** Sharp, mocking, ancient. Talks to grown jōnin like errant grandchildren. Deep, unspoken grief about Sasori — she raised him after his parents died on a mission, and she made his first puppets.

##### Ebizō
Chiyo's brother and co-elder. More diplomatic, less lethal. **Voice:** The reasonable one, wearily managing his sister.

##### The Third Kazekage
**Abilities:** **Iron Sand (Satetsu)** — Magnet Release manipulation of iron particles into any shape. Considered the strongest Kazekage in Suna's history.
**Status in Era A:** **Missing.** Suna searches for him for years. He was killed by **Sasori** and converted into a **human puppet** — Sasori's masterpiece and most-used weapon.
**Use:** A superb long-running mystery for a Suna campaign.

##### Rasa (Fourth Kazekage)
**Abilities:** **Gold Dust (Sakin)** — Magnet Release.
**Role:** Takes office after the Third's disappearance. Presides over a village strangled by its own daimyō's cost-cutting. Later seals Shukaku into his newborn son Gaara — a decision he makes as *policy*.
**Voice:** Formal, cold, tired. Speaks about his own family in the vocabulary of resource allocation.

##### Sasori
**Japanese:** Sasori (サソリ) · **Rank:** Missing-nin (defects during this era)
**Abilities:** Puppetry beyond any peer; **human puppets**; converted his own body into a puppet, retaining only a living core; **Red Secret Technique: Performance of a Hundred Puppets** (S-rank); poison mastery.
**Era A arc:** Grows up under Chiyo, becomes Suna's greatest puppeteer, kills the Third Kazekage, defects. Later joins Akatsuki.
**Voice:** Flat, impatient, obsessed with **not keeping people waiting** — a fixation with an ugly logic behind it. Discusses art and permanence in the same tone he discusses killing.

##### Pakura
**Abilities:** **Scorch Release (Shakuton)** — Fire + Wind; desiccates a target to a husk. "Pakura of the Scorch Style," a Suna hero.
**Fate:** **Betrayed and killed by her own village**, handed to Kirigakure as a gesture during peace negotiations. Suna's leadership traded her life for a treaty.
**Use:** The single best Suna-side tragedy available in Era A. She returns via Edo Tensei in Era C, and she is *furious*.

##### Baki
Young jōnin. Wind Release, **Blade of Wind** (A-rank). Later Gaara's instructor.

### PART FOUR — KUMOGAKURE

##### The Third Raikage
**Abilities:** The **strongest defense** in the setting — a Lightning Release Armour rendering him effectively invulnerable to conventional attack; the **Hell Stab**, a spear-hand strike of escalating finger count.
**Feat:** Held off a combined force of **ten thousand shinobi** alone for three days and nights, dying of exhaustion rather than defeat. **[M]**
**Voice:** Immovable. Speaks rarely and in absolutes. Regards retreat as an unfamiliar concept.

##### A (Fourth Raikage)
**Era A status:** The Third's son; young, immensely strong, not yet in office.
**Abilities:** **Lightning Release Chakra Mode** — extreme speed and reaction; Lariat; raw physical power that reshapes terrain.
**Voice:** Explosive, loud, direct. Every conversation is a confrontation. Deeply loyal to his brother.

##### Killer B
**Japanese:** Kirābī (キラービー)
**Era A status:** Young. Becomes the **Eight-Tails (Gyūki) jinchūriki** after previous hosts failed.
**Uniquely:** He and Gyūki reach a **genuine partnership** — the only fully cooperative jinchūriki relationship of the era.
**Abilities:** Seven-sword kenjutsu (the "Acrobat" style, A-rank); full tailed-beast transformation; Lightning.
**Voice:** Rhymes. Constantly. Raps his dialogue, badly, and is unbothered by anyone's reaction. Beneath it he is a disciplined, deadly professional — the goofiness is armor and it is also sincere.

### PART SIX — AMEGAKURE & THE UNAFFILIATED

##### Hanzō of the Salamander
**Japanese:** Sanshōuo no Hanzō (山椒魚の半蔵)
**Abilities:** A **poison sac surgically implanted in his own chest**, making his breath and blood lethal; the giant salamander **Ibuse**, whose poison mist can wipe out an army; a kusarigama and unmatched personal combat skill.
**Reputation:** Defeated Jiraiya, Tsunade, and Orochimaru simultaneously and **named them the Sannin** — the only person in the setting to have done anything like it.
**Voice:** Measured, honorable in form, paranoid in substance. He genuinely respects strength and genuinely fears anything that might replace him. Manipulated by Danzō into destroying Yahiko's Akatsuki, which is his ruin.

##### Yahiko
Leader of the original **Akatsuki** — an idealist organization founded to end the cycle of war in the Land of Rain through mediation and force-of-example. Charismatic, hopeful, and Jiraiya's most promising student.
**Voice:** Bright, forceful, a natural rallying point. He talks about peace like it is a plan rather than a wish.
**Fate:** Impales himself on Nagato's kunai to spare Konan. His body is later used as the **Deva Path** of Pain.

##### Nagato
Holder of the **Rinnegan** — implanted as an infant by Madara without anyone's knowledge, including his own. Quiet, physically frail, devoted to Yahiko and Konan.
**Voice in Era A:** Soft-spoken, anxious, deferential. **Nothing like Pain.** The transformation is the story.

##### Konan
Paper Release specialist. Calm, precise, the group's steady center.

##### Madara Uchiha
**Status:** Alive, ancient, immobile, sustained in a cavern by the **Demonic Statue of the Outer Path**. Blind in one eye, physically failing, and running a plan across decades.
**Role in Era A:** Recovers Obito from the Kannabi cave-in, heals him with **Hashirama cells and White Zetsu**, and spends years shaping him.
**Voice:** Grand, patient, utterly contemptuous of the world as it is. Speaks in cosmic timeframes. He is not persuading Obito so much as waiting for reality to persuade him.
**Absolutely nobody in the world knows he is alive.**

##### Zetsu (White & Black)
**White Zetsu:** Grown from Hashirama's cells; countless copies; can merge with terrain, travel underground, and impersonate individuals.
**Black Zetsu:** The manifested will of **Kaguya Ōtsutsuki**, posing as Madara's creation. Has been steering Uchiha history for a thousand years.
**Voice:** White is chatty and oddly casual. Black is sibilant and formal. They finish each other's sentences and it is deeply unsettling.

### PART SEVEN — USING THIS ROSTER

#### Casting a Third War Campaign
- **Give the PCs a jōnin sensei who will die.** Era A's engine is the loss of mentors. Might Duy, a nameless jōnin, or a beloved NPC.
- **The enemy squad should recur.** Iwa's Kannabi team model — a jōnin plus two specialists — is the right shape. Let the PCs beat them once and meet them again.
- **Put one named legend on the horizon.** The PCs should hear about Minato, Hanzō, or Chiyo long before they meet them, and meeting them should be terrifying.

#### Cameo Discipline
Resist the urge to have Minato personally save the party. Era A works because the great figures are *elsewhere*, winning or losing a different battle. Let the PCs hear that the Yellow Flash cleared the eastern front while they were bleeding in a ditch on the northern one.

#### Dialogue Rule
**No character in Era A may reference:** Akatsuki as a threat (it's a small idealist group in Ame), the tailed beasts as a collection project, Obito's survival, Madara's survival, the Eye of the Moon Plan, Otogakure, or anyone born after Year 0. See **Book III §4**.

### PART ONE — ALLIED HIGH COMMAND

##### Mifune
**Rank:** Commander-in-Chief of the Allied Shinobi Forces; General of the Land of Iron
**Abilities:** Samurai swordsmanship with chakra flow; can cut through techniques other blades cannot touch.
**Role:** Appointed at the Kage Summit precisely *because* he is not from any of the five villages — the only figure all five Kage would accept above themselves.
**Voice:** Formal, spare, immovable. Speaks like a man who has already decided and is informing you.

##### Gaara
**Rank:** Fifth Kazekage; **Regimental Commander** of the Allied Forces; Fourth Division commander
**Abilities:** Absolute sand manipulation; Shield and Armour of Sand; Sand Coffin/Burial; Magnet Release (inherited); Desert Layered Imperial Funeral.
**Role:** The youngest Kage on the field, commanding shinobi from villages that were killing each other a year ago. **His pre-battle speech is the moment the Allied Forces actually become one army.**
**Voice:** Quiet, deliberate, entirely without ego. He speaks about having been hated and having changed, and he does it plainly, which is why it works. No longer the murderous child — but the stillness is the same.
**Does not know:** That his father Rasa will be reanimated and sent against him.

##### Tsunade Senju
**Rank:** Fifth Hokage; HQ
**Abilities:** Chakra Enhanced Strength; **Creation Rebirth**; the **Yin Seal: Release** (S-rank) accumulated over years; **Katsuyu: Immense Network Healing** (A-rank), by which she heals the entire Allied army simultaneously through distributed slug divisions.
**Role:** The strategic and medical backbone. Later joins the Kage in direct combat against Madara and is critically wounded shielding them.
**Voice:** Sharp, commanding, profane under pressure. Her medical orders are non-negotiable. She has stopped running.

##### A (Fourth Raikage)
**Abilities:** **Lightning Release Chakra Mode**; Lariat; Liger Bomb **[CONFLICT — rank flagged in Jutsu Compendium]**; raw strength on a landscape-altering scale.
**Voice:** Bellows. Interrupts. Argues with Tsunade constantly and defers to her exactly once per crisis. His love for Killer B is the softest thing about him and he expresses it by shouting.

##### Ōnoki (Third Tsuchikage)
The old man from Era A, still in office, now fighting *alongside* Konoha. **His arc in Era C is swallowing the grudge.** Dust Release remains one of the Allies' few genuine trump cards.
**Voice:** Complains about his back, then disintegrates a battlefield. Sardonic toward Ōnoki-aged peers, dismissive toward everyone under sixty.

##### Mei Terumī (Fifth Mizukage)
**Abilities:** **Lava Release** and **Boil Release** — two kekkei genkai in one shinobi.
**Role:** Rebuilding Kirigakure's international standing. Pragmatic, diplomatic, and lethal.
**Voice:** Charming and warm in negotiation, instantly cold when threatened. Sensitive about being unmarried in a way that derails meetings, which is played for comedy and should be used sparingly.

##### Shikaku Nara
**Rank:** Konoha Jōnin Commander; **Allied HQ chief strategist**
**Role:** Designs the Allied battle plans. **Killed when HQ is destroyed**, transmitting his final strategic order through the mind-link as he dies.
**Voice:** Bored-sounding, several steps ahead, explains things once. Father and son speak identically.

##### Inoichi Yamanaka
**Role:** Operates the Allied **mind-link communication network** — every order across the entire front passes through him. Dies alongside Shikaku.
**Voice:** Calm relay discipline under fire. He is the sound of the war.

### PART TWO — THE DIVISIONS

#### First Division — Mid-Range Combat
**Commander: Darui** (Kumo) — **Storm Release**, Black Lightning. Laconic; says "how lame" about mortal peril. Competent to the point of understatement.
Notable: **Kitsuchi's** cross-attachments; Samui, Atsui, Karui (Kumo).

#### Second Division — Short-to-Mid-Range Combat
**Commander: Kitsuchi** (Iwa) — Earth Release on a terrain-shaping scale; Ōnoki's son.
Notable: **Kurotsuchi** (Lava Release, Ōnoki's granddaughter), **Akatsuchi**.

#### Third Division — Short-Range Combat
**Commander: Kakashi Hatake** (Konoha) — Sharingan, **Kamui**, Lightning Cutter, all five natures via the Sharingan's copying.
**Voice:** Dry, unhurried, deflective. Commands by making people feel steadied rather than ordered. Faces Zabuza and Haku reanimated — his own history weaponized against him.
Notable: **Might Gai** (Eight Gates), **Rock Lee**, **Kiba & Akamaru**, **Shino Aburame**, **Chōji & Ino** (attached at points).

#### Fourth Division — Long-Range Combat

**Voice:** Wry, capable, permanently exasperated with his siblings.

#### Logistics & Medical Division

**Commander: Ao** (Kiri) — transplanted **Byakugan**; the Allies' primary counter to White Zetsu infiltration.

### PART THREE — KEY INDIVIDUALS

##### Naruto Uzumaki
**Rank:** Genin (technically — and everyone finds this absurd by now)
**Abilities:** **Nine-Tails Chakra Mode** and full **Kurama Link Mode**; **Sage Mode**; **Rasenshuriken** and its variants (S-rank); **Multiple Shadow Clone Technique** (A-rank kinjutsu) at a scale nobody else can match; negative-emotion sensing.
**Role:** Distributed across the entire battlefield via shadow clones, transforming Allied capability division by division. The war's morale center.
**Voice:** Loud, direct, allergic to subtlety. Talks to enemies as though they can be reasoned with, and is sometimes right. Says the obvious sincere thing that everyone else is too guarded to say. **He is not stupid** — play his tactical instincts as genuinely sharp.
**Does not know at the start:** That his father was Fourth Hokage — *(actually he learns this before the war)* — but he **does not know** that "Madara" is Obito, that Black Zetsu exists, or that Kaguya exists.

##### Sasuke Uchiha
**Rank:** International criminal; joins the Allied side mid-war
**Abilities:** **Mangekyō Sharingan** (Amaterasu, Blaze Release: Kagutsuchi, Susanoo); Chidori and its variants; **Kirin** (S-rank); later **Rinnegan**.
**Role:** Learns the truth of Itachi from the reanimated Hokage, reanimates the four previous Hokage via Orochimaru, and commits to protecting Konoha on his own terms.
**Voice:** Terse. Answers questions with statements. Contempt as a default register, with rare fractures. He does not explain himself and resents being asked to.

##### Sakura Haruno
**Abilities:** Chakra Enhanced Strength; **Yin Seal: Release** (S-rank, achieved during the war); **Creation Rebirth**; front-line medical ninjutsu at Tsunade's level.
**Voice:** Direct, sharp-tongued, and by Era C entirely done being underestimated. Her competence is not in question and she does not seek permission.

##### Killer B
Eight-Tails jinchūriki, in full partnership with Gyūki. Trains Naruto in beast control at the Falls of Truth. Still rhyming.

##### Shikamaru Nara
Takes over Allied command after HQ falls. **Voice:** "What a drag," followed by a plan that works. Grief over Asuma and then over his father sits under everything.

##### Might Gai
Opens the **Eighth Gate** against Madara — the strongest purely physical attack in the setting (**Night Guy**, S-rank). Survives only through intervention.
**Voice:** Absolute sincerity at maximum volume. No irony whatsoever. His challenge to Kakashi is a lifelong bit that is also completely genuine.

##### Neji Hyūga
Byakugan; Eight Trigrams techniques. **Dies shielding Naruto and Hinata.** His death is the emotional hinge of the war's middle act.

##### Orochimaru
Returns mid-war, no longer an antagonist so much as an opportunist with an interest in the outcome. Reanimates the four Hokage at Sasuke's request.
**Voice:** Unchanged — silken, amused, evaluating. Now with the added texture of everyone being extremely uncomfortable that he's helping.

##### Kabuto Yakushi
**Rank:** S-rank; **Sage Mode** (Ryūchi Cave, snake senjutsu)
**Role:** Controls the entire **Edo Tensei** army and extorts partnership with Obito by holding Sasuke and the reanimations as leverage. The war's second brain.
**Voice:** Smooth, needling, obsessed with identity — he speaks constantly about what he has *become*, because he has never known what he is.

##### Tenten, Chōji, Ino, Kiba, Shino, Hinata, Temari, Kankurō, Chōjūrō, Kurotsuchi, Omoi, Karui, Samui
Second-tier named combatants. Use them as squad-level NPCs attached to the PCs' division — this is the single best way to make the war feel populated.

### PART FOUR — THE ENEMY

#### Obito Uchiha ("Tobi" / "Madara")
**Role:** Field commander of the Akatsuki war effort. Architect of the **Eye of the Moon Plan** — using the Ten-Tails and the **Infinite Tsukuyomi** to place all humanity in a permanent shared dream, ending suffering by ending reality.
**Abilities:** **Kamui** — phase-through intangibility and dimensional transport, the most frustrating defense in the setting; Wood Release (Hashirama cells); later, **Ten-Tails jinchūriki** and **Truth-Seeking Balls**.
**Voice as "Tobi":** Idiotic, singsong, bumbling — a deliberate mask that fooled Akatsuki for years.
**Voice unmasked:** Cold, philosophical, exhausted. He speaks about the world as *worthless* with the flatness of settled conviction. He does not rant.
**The reveal sequence matters:** the Allies believe they are fighting Madara Uchiha for most of the war.

#### Madara Uchiha
**Reanimated by Kabuto on Day One.** Then frees himself from Edo Tensei control entirely.
**Abilities:** **Eternal Mangekyō Sharingan**; **Perfect Susanoo**; **Wood Release**; later the **Rinnegan** and **Ten-Tails jinchūriki** status; **Limbo: Border Jail** — invisible shadow duplicates that cannot be perceived without a Rinnegan or Six Paths chakra.
**Feat:** Fights and defeats the **five Kage simultaneously**.
**Voice:** Delighted. Genuinely enjoying himself — the only major villain in the setting who is *having a good time*. Grandiose, contemptuous, and completely at ease. He praises worthy opponents sincerely and kills them anyway.

#### Black Zetsu
Reveals itself as the **will of Kaguya Ōtsutsuki**, having engineered a thousand years of Uchiha history — including Madara's entire life — to bring about her return. Betrays Madara at the decisive moment.
**Voice:** Sibilant, formal, gloating. Its reveal monologue should be genuinely humiliating for Madara.

#### Kaguya Ōtsutsuki
**Rank:** Beyond ranking. **Kekkei Mōra.**
**Abilities:** **Rinne Sharingan**; **Infinite Tsukuyomi**; **All-Killing Ash Bones**; **Yomotsu Hirasaka** (dimensional travel); Truth-Seeking Balls; the Expansive Truth-Seeking Ball.
**Voice:** Barely speaks. When she does, it is fragmentary and maternal in the worst possible way. She is less a character than a natural disaster with a face — which is the correct way to run her.

#### The White Zetsu Army
~100,000 clones grown from Hashirama's cells. Individually weak (a competent chūnin handles one), collectively overwhelming. **Can perfectly impersonate specific Allied shinobi**, including memories and mannerisms.
**Use at the table:** The Zetsu infiltration is the best paranoia engine in the setting. Run it as a social horror problem, not a combat one.

#### Notable Edo Tensei Reanimations
The enemy's most devastating asset: your own honored dead, unkillable, fighting under compulsion — and often **conscious of it and unable to stop.**

| Reanimated | Deployed against | Why it hurts |
|---|---|---|
| **The reanimated Kage** (2nd Mizukage, 2nd Tsuchikage, 3rd Raikage, 4th Kazekage) | Fourth Division / Gaara | Gaara must fight his father |
| **Zabuza Momochi & Haku** | Third Division / Kakashi | Kakashi's own past |
| **Asuma Sarutobi** | Ino-Shika-Chō | Their teacher, sent against his own students |
| **Deidara, Sasori, Kakuzu, Hidan** (Akatsuki dead) | Various | Known quantities with known counters |
| **Itachi Uchiha** | Sasuke's front | Breaks free via Izanami; the war's best redemption beat |
| **Nagato** | Naruto & Killer B | The man Naruto already forgave once |
| **Pakura, Gari, Chūkichi** | Various | Villages fighting their own betrayed heroes |
| **Madara Uchiha** | Everyone | The war's actual monster |
| **The Seven Ninja Swordsmen** | Third Division | An entire elite unit at once |
| **Hanzō of the Salamander** | Fifth Division / Mifune | Poison on a battlefield scale |

**The four Hokage** are reanimated separately, by **Orochimaru at Sasuke's request**, and fight *for* the Allies. **Minato meeting Naruto on the battlefield** is the war's emotional apex.

##### Running Edo Tensei **[GM]**
- Reanimations **regenerate from any damage** and cannot be killed.
- They are stopped only by **sealing** (fūinjutsu), **immobilization**, or **the controller releasing them**.
- Many retain their own will and are **fighting against their own bodies** — this is where the drama is.
- **Give the PCs a sealing specialist NPC or item**, or the encounter has no win condition and the table will feel it.

### PART FIVE — RUNNING THE FOURTH WAR

#### Keep the PCs in one division
The war's scale is meaningless if the party teleports across it. Assign them to a single division under a single named commander (Kakashi's Third and Gaara's Fourth are the richest) and let the rest of the war arrive as *news*.

#### Use the reveals as act breaks
1. **Act One:** The dead are fighting us.
2. **Act Two:** HQ is gone; we have no coordination.
3. **Act Three:** The Ten-Tails is real; "Madara" wasn't Madara; now Madara is.
4. **Act Four:** None of it was ever Madara's plan.

Each reveal should land as a *loss of ground the players thought they'd won*.

#### Dialogue Rule
**No Allied character may say "Obito"** in reference to the masked man until the manga-canonical reveal, and no character on any side may reference **Kaguya or Black Zetsu's true nature** before the final act. Even Obito believes he is executing *Madara's* plan. Even Madara believes it is *his own*.

See **Book III §4** for the complete knowledge tables.

### PART ONE — AKATSUKI (暁, "Dawn")

The setting's defining antagonist organization, and one that exists in **two entirely different forms.** Getting the era right is essential.

---

#### Akatsuki, First Form — The Ame Idealists
**Era A. Land of Rain.**

Founded by **Yahiko**, with **Nagato** and **Konan**, after Jiraiya's training. A genuine peace movement: a small group of war orphans who intended to end the cycle of violence in the Land of Rain by force of example, mediating between the great powers that used their country as a battlefield.

- **Membership:** Grows into a real movement with popular support.
- **Uniform:** No red clouds yet — that comes later.
- **Threat level:** Political, not military.
- **Fate:** **Hanzō**, fearing displacement and manipulated by **Danzō**, sets a trap. Konan is taken hostage; Nagato must choose between her and Yahiko; **Yahiko impales himself** on Nagato's kunai. Nagato awakens the Rinnegan's full power in grief and annihilates the ambush.

**If your campaign is set in Era A, this is what Akatsuki means.** A PC could join it, fight for it, or die with it. Nobody is hunting tailed beasts.

---

#### Akatsuki, Second Form — Pain's Organization
**Era B into Era C.**

Rebuilt by **Nagato** as **Pain**, guided from the shadows by **Obito** (posing as Madara). Ten members, operating in **two-person cells**, financing themselves through mercenary contracts while pursuing the nine tailed beasts.

##### Uniform & Signifiers
- **Black cloak with red clouds**, high collar.
- **Scratched-through village forehead protector** — every member is a missing-nin.
- **A ring**, worn on a specific finger, bearing a specific kanji. The ten rings correspond to the ten members and are required to operate the Gedō Statue's sealing ritual.
- Straw hats with paper tassels for travel concealment.

##### Member Profiles

**Itachi Uchiha** — Mangekyō Sharingan (Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi, Susanoo), Totsuka Blade and Yata Mirror. A Konoha loyalist executing a lifelong deception. **Voice:** Quiet, weary, elliptical. Says less than he means. Every cruelty is calculated to serve a purpose he will never explain.

**Kisame Hoshigaki** — "Monster of the Hidden Mist." **Samehada**, a sentient chakra-devouring blade; Water Release on an ocean-generating scale; chakra reserves comparable to a tailed beast's. **Voice:** Genial, sharky, enthusiastic about violence. Dryly funny. Loyal to the *cause* rather than to any person.

**Sasori** — See Book III §1. **Voice:** Impatient, flat, obsessed with permanence.

**Deidara** — **Explosion Release** and Explosive Clay; mouths in his palms; considers explosions the highest art form. **Voice:** Cocky, aggrieved, ends sentences with a verbal tic. Endlessly baiting Sasori about what "art" means — fleeting beauty versus eternal form. Hates the Sharingan.

**Hidan** — **Curse Technique: Death Controlling Possessed Blood**; effectively immortal; a fanatical devotee of Jashin who must ritually sacrifice to his god. **Voice:** Foul-mouthed, gleeful, theologically obsessed. Complains constantly about how long his own rituals take.

**Kakuzu** — **Earth Grudge Fear**; five hearts, each with a different nature affinity; thread-based body manipulation; centuries old. **Voice:** Terse, mercenary, exclusively motivated by money. Regards partners as expenses and has killed several for irritating him.

**Zetsu** — See Book III §1/08.

**Konan** — Paper Release. Loyal to Nagato absolutely. **Voice:** Formal, minimal, sorrowful.

**Nagato / Pain** — **Rinnegan**; the **Six Paths of Pain**, six corpses controlled remotely via chakra receivers, each embodying one Path. Physically ruined and immobile. **Voice as Pain:** Detached, oracular, speaking of pain and peace as an impersonal law. **Voice as Nagato:** the frightened boy underneath, if you ever get to him.

**Orochimaru** — Departs after failing to take Itachi's body.

**Obito ("Tobi")** — See Book III §2.

- **Amegakure** — a whole country under total surveillance.

### PART TWO — KONOHA'S INTERNAL ORGANIZATIONS

#### ANBU (暗部)
**Full name:** Ansatsu Senjutsu Tokushu Butai — Assassination and Tactical Special Squad.
**Reports to:** The Hokage directly.
**Founded by:** Tobirama Senju.

- **Recruitment:** By invitation. Jōnin or exceptional chūnin.
- **Identity:** Animal masks, codenames, concealed faces. Members are anonymous even to allies. Konoha ANBU bear a **tattoo on the left shoulder**.
- **Kit:** Grey armored vest, black bodysuit, arm guards, **tantō** on the back.
- **Duties:** Assassination, infiltration, Kage bodyguard, defector retrieval, and the missions that do not appear in any record.
- **Culture:** Emotionally corrosive by design. Members are expected to suppress personal attachment. **Kakashi's** ANBU years and **Itachi's** captaincy at 13 are the canonical examples.

**At the table:** ANBU membership is a superb mid-campaign development for a jōnin PC — but it should cost them. Their name disappears from the roster. Their old squad doesn't know where they are.

#### Root (根, Ne)
**Danzō's** organization. Nominally an ANBU subdivision; in practice a private army.

- **Recruitment:** Children, often orphans, taken young and stripped of names, emotions, and personal history.
- **Training:** Emotional suppression to the point of affectlessness. Recruits are made to kill one another.
- **The Tongue Seal:** A fūinjutsu marking that inflicts **total paralysis** on any member who speaks of Root's affairs. A more severe variant kills outright.
- **Status:** Officially disbanded by Hiruzen. Fully operational throughout Eras A and B.

**At the table:** A Root PC is mechanically forbidden from telling the party who they work for. Enforce it. It is the best-structured secret in the setting.

#### The Konoha Military Police Force
Uchiha-staffed internal law enforcement. See Book I §3 and Book II §2. **Dissolved Year +7.**

#### The Torture and Interrogation Force
**Ibiki Morino** commanding; **Inoichi Yamanaka** supplying mind-walking. Handles prisoners, suspected traitors, and sealed-memory retrieval.

#### The Twelve Guardian Ninja
An elite twelve-member unit protecting the **Fire Daimyō**, drawn from Konoha but serving the daimyō directly. Wield **chakra blades**. **Asuma Sarutobi** served and left. The unit's existence is itself a political statement: the daimyō does not fully trust the Hokage.

### PART FIVE — FACTION MECHANICS **[GM]**

#### Reputation with Factions
Track PC standing with each faction on a simple −3 to +3 scale.

| Standing | With a village | With Akatsuki | With Root |
|---|---|---|---|

#### Infiltrating Akatsuki **[GM]**
It is possible and it is a great campaign. Requirements:
1. **Be a missing-nin with a real Bingo Book entry** — Akatsuki recruits proven S-rank threats.
2. **Kill a member and take their ring.** This is the actual mechanism.
3. **Survive Zetsu**, who watches everything and reports to Obito.
4. **Understand that Itachi is doing this already** and may either expose or protect you.

#### Joining Root **[GM]**
- Recruitment is not voluntary. Danzō takes people.
- The **tongue seal** is applied on entry. Mechanically: the character is **incapable of speaking about Root** — attempting it causes paralysis for 1 minute and 4d6 psychic damage. No save. This should be a genuine constraint on the player, agreed in session zero.

- **Kiri's bloodline purge vs. its own Swordsmen** — several of whom are bloodline carriers.
- **The Allied divisions vs. each other** — Era C, in every quiet moment between battles.

# §4 — INFORMATION HORIZONS

**Read this before writing any NPC dialogue.**

The most common failure in a Naruto campaign is a character knowing something they cannot possibly know. A Third War jōnin who mentions "Akatsuki collecting the tailed beasts." A Fourth War soldier who casually says "Obito." A Konoha chūnin in Era A who knows Naruto's parentage — before Naruto exists.

This file exists so that never happens.

---
---

### PART ONE — THE GOVERNING RULE

> **A character speaks only from what they have seen, been told, or could reasonably infer from their own position.**

Three practical tests before any line of NPC dialogue:

1. **Where were they?** A Suna genin does not know what happened inside Konoha's council chamber.
2. **Who would have told them?** Information travels through *specific channels* — clan, squad, rank, village. A chūnin does not receive Kage-level briefings.
3. **Is this a secret someone is actively keeping?** If so, assume they don't know it, and that the person keeping it is *good at their job*.

**When in doubt, the character does not know.** Ignorance is always more interesting than exposition.

### PART THREE — ERA A: THE THIRD WAR

#### Common Knowledge — anyone may reference

- The five great nations are at war. Konoha's principal enemy is Iwagakure.
- **Minato Namikaze, "Konoha's Yellow Flash,"** is the most feared shinobi on the Iwa front. Iwa has a flee-on-sight order.
- **The Legendary Sannin** — Jiraiya, Tsunade, Orochimaru — named by Hanzō of the Salamander.
- **Hanzō of the Salamander** rules Amegakure and is regarded as nearly unbeatable.
- **Hiruzen Sarutobi** is Hokage. **Ōnoki** is Tsuchikage. The Third Raikage is a legend.

- **Sakumo Hatake**, the White Fang, is dead and disgraced. Everyone knows the story and opinions differ.
- **Uzushiogakure was destroyed** in the previous war.
- Kirigakure does horrifying things to its own students. This is rumor at genin level, confirmed at jōnin level.

#### Village-Level Classified — jōnin and above, within one village

#### Known to Almost Nobody

- **Obito is alive.** Kakashi and Rin watched him die. His name goes on the Memorial Stone.
- **Madara is alive**, in a cave, keeping a boy alive on Hashirama's cells.
- The Kiri operation to seal Isobu into Rin **was orchestrated externally.** Kiri believes it is their own plan.
- Danzō is manipulating Hanzō against Yahiko's Akatsuki. **Hanzō believes it is his own idea.**

#### Era A — Absolute Dialogue Prohibitions

**No character in Era A may say or imply:**
- "Akatsuki" as a threat. In Era A it is a small idealist peace group in Amegakure, known to few outside the Land of Rain, and **not hostile to anyone.**
- Anything about tailed beasts being *collected*.
- "Otogakure" or "the Sound village" — **it does not exist for another fifteen-plus years.**
- The names Naruto, Sasuke, Sakura, Gaara, Neji, Hinata, Shikamaru, or any of their generation. **None of them are born.**
- "The Fourth Hokage" before Minato's appointment near the war's end.
- Any reference to the Uchiha massacre, the Nine-Tails attack on Konoha, or Pain.
- "The Eye of the Moon Plan."

### PART FOUR — ERA B: THE INTERBELLUM

#### Becomes Common Knowledge During This Era
- The **Nine-Tails attacked Konoha** in Year 0 and the Fourth Hokage died stopping it. *The masked man is not part of the public account.*
- **The Uchiha clan was massacred by Itachi Uchiha**, who defected. *This is the official version and it is a lie.*
- **Orochimaru defected** from Konoha and is an S-rank criminal.
- **Akatsuki exists** as an organization of S-rank missing-nin — but its *goals* are not public. It is understood as a mercenary group.
- **Kirigakure's civil war** and Mei Terumī's ascension.
- Tsunade becomes Fifth Hokage.

#### Still Classified
- Naruto is the Nine-Tails jinchūriki. **The adults of Konoha know; a law forbids telling the children.** Naruto himself does not know until Mizuki tells him.
- **Naruto's parentage.** Known to a very small circle.
- Itachi's true orders.
- Root's continued operation.
- **Akatsuki's actual objective** — the tailed beasts — is pieced together only gradually, largely by Jiraiya.

#### Era B — Dialogue Notes
- Adults in Konoha **avoid saying anything about Naruto's status in front of children**, and are visibly uncomfortable around him. That discomfort is playable without a single word of exposition.
- **Nobody knows what happened to Obito's body.** His name is on the stone.
- Akatsuki members do not explain their organization to anyone. Kisame and Itachi answer questions with threats.

### PART FIVE — ERA C: THE FOURTH WAR

#### Common Knowledge at the war's start
- The Allied Shinobi Forces exist and who commands each division.
- **"Madara Uchiha" has declared war** and demands the last two jinchūriki. *Everyone believes this is Madara.*
- Akatsuki has captured seven tailed beasts.
- The enemy fields a **White Zetsu army** and an **Edo Tensei army**.
- Naruto is the Nine-Tails jinchūriki, is the Fourth Hokage's son, and this is now openly known.
- **Sasuke Uchiha** is an international criminal.
- Edo Tensei reanimations **cannot be killed** — this is learned in the first engagements and disseminated by HQ.

#### Revealed During the War, in Order
1. **The real Madara is reanimated** — meaning the masked man is someone else. *This is a genuine shock to the Allies.*
2. **The masked man is Obito Uchiha.** Kakashi is the one it destroys.
3. **The truth of Itachi** — revealed to Sasuke by the reanimated Hokage.
4. **The Ten-Tails is real and is being revived.**
5. **Obito, then Madara, becomes the Ten-Tails' jinchūriki.**
6. **Black Zetsu betrays Madara** and reveals itself as Kaguya's will.
7. **Kaguya Ōtsutsuki.** Known to no living person before this moment.

#### Era C — Dialogue Prohibitions

**Before the reveal, no Allied character may say "Obito."** They say "Madara." Kakashi says "Madara" while fighting a man using Kamui and does not put it together, because the alternative is unthinkable.

**Nobody on any side may reference Kaguya, the Ōtsutsuki, or Black Zetsu's true nature until the final act.** This includes Madara. **Especially** Madara — his entire life was authored by something he never noticed, and playing him as unaware is the whole point of the betrayal.

**Obito believes he is executing Madara's plan.** He does not know he is a pawn. Play his conviction as sincere.

| **Naruto** | His parents; Kurama; that "Madara" leads the enemy | Obito's identity; Kaguya; Black Zetsu |
| **Kakashi** | Everything about his own past | That Obito lives — until the reveal, which is his worst moment |
| **Sasuke** | Itachi's truth (mid-war) | Kaguya; the full Indra/Asura history until Hagoromo tells him |

### PART SIX — INFORMATION AS A GAME MECHANIC **[GM]**

#### Intelligence Sources and Their Reliability

| Source | Reliability | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct observation** | High | What the PCs saw |
| **Sensor report** | High for numbers and position, low for identity | Sensors detect chakra, not intent |
| **Byakugan** | Very high within range | The Allies' counter to Zetsu |
| **Yamanaka mind-reading** | High, but only what the subject knows | A subject who was lied to relays the lie |
| **Jiraiya's spy network** | High but slow | Weeks of lag |
| **Bingo Book** | Reliable for identity, **often outdated on capability** | Great source of dangerous overconfidence |
| **Village rumor** | Low | Excellent for seeding false leads |
| **A captured enemy** | Variable | Akatsuki members do not talk. Zetsu lies fluently. |
| **The Uchiha stone tablet** | Appears authoritative. **Is altered.** | The most dangerous "reliable" source in the setting |

#### Running Secrets Well
1. **Let the players find out before the characters do**, sometimes. Dramatic irony is more fun than surprise.
2. **Give ignorance texture.** A character who doesn't know something should have a *wrong theory*, confidently held.
3. **Reward legwork.** If the party spends a session investigating, they should get real information — not a "you learn nothing" wall.
4. **Never have an NPC monologue a secret to be helpful.** If a secret comes out, someone should be *losing something* by saying it.

#### The Dialogue Checklist
Before an NPC speaks, confirm:
- ☐ Are they using names of people who exist yet?
- ☐ Are they referencing organizations that exist yet, in the form they exist *now*?
- ☐ Do they have a plausible channel for this information?
- ☐ If this is classified, does their rank and village clear them for it?
- ☐ Are they revealing something because it serves *them*, or because it serves the GM?

If the last box is checked, rewrite the line.
